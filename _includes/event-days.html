The heading is visually hidden: it is what a screen reader hears and what the calendar's day links target, and the tile, which says the same, is hidden from screen readers. Needs whats_on_today, set by whats-on-events.html. A tile shows no month, so a visible month label (aria-hidden: the hidden heading says it) goes before a day in another month than the day before it, or, for the first day, than the build's; months=false leaves it off where a heading already names the month. hide_town=true leaves the town off every row. ids=false leaves the heading ids off, where the days repeat another list's on the same page. areas=true marks each row, day and month label with its areas, for the saved-area filter on the What's on pages; the month labels' come from a first pass, since a label precedes its days.
