The heading is visually hidden: it is what a screen reader hears and what the calendar's day links target, and the tile, which says the same, is hidden from screen readers. Needs whats_on_today, set by whats-on-events.html. A tile shows no month, so a visible month label (aria-hidden: the hidden heading says it) goes before a day in another month than the day before it, or, for the first day, than the build's; months=false leaves it off where a heading already names the month. hide_town=true leaves the town off every row.
