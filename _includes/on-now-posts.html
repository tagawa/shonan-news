{% comment %} Things running on include.day: every post whose on_until (a closing date, backend spec "Closing date") is on or after it, and, with include.town_filter (an area's towns), whose town is in it. Prints nothing: sets on_now_posts, soonest closing first and the newer post first on a tie, and on_now_areas, the area slugs of those posts, so callers can count them in their "nothing here" lines before printing the list with on-now.html. The sort is on a key string, YYYY-MM-DD|(9999999999 minus the date epoch)|index, because sort raises on a mix of Date and string. Reset first, since includes share the page's scope.
