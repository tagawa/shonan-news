A complete performance of Mozart's string quintets, commemorating the 270th anniversary of his birth, will be held at Jochiji. There are two sessions: Part 1 at 11:00 and Part 2 at 14:00; admission is free, seating is unreserved, and reservations are required. Visitors should note a temple admission fee of 300 yen applies. To reserve, use the provided QR code or the link listed in the announcement.
