Oppama Ginza Street will become a pedestrian zone on July 12, with a lineup of diverse dances including Awa Odori and hip hop, plus energetic performances such as fancy drill. The event runs from 11:00 to 18:00, will proceed in light rain but be canceled in severe weather, and takes place along the entire Oppama Ginza Street; the Rio Carnival will not perform and telephoto lenses are prohibited for privacy. For inquiries call 046 (822) 4000 Oppama Administrative Center.
