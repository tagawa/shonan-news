The Yokosuka City Tourism Association is displaying special panels, scripts, and props from productions it helped attract, including "明日はもっと、いい日になる" and "喧嘩独学," with autographs from admired actors. The exhibition runs 10:00–22:00, except on the 24th when it closes at 15:00, at Yokosuka More's City 8th floor event space "Sampatio." For inquiries, contact Yokosuka City Tourism Association at 046（822）8256.
