On July 19, 2026, the Yokosuka Arts Theater Boys and Girls Choir, consisting of 100 members, will perform uplifting songs such as "Anpanman's March" and "Inochi no Uta" with clear, pure voices. The concert starts at 15:00 with doors opening at 14:30 at Yokosuka Bayside Pocket; S-seat adult tickets are 1,800 yen, preschool children are not admitted, and inquiries can be made by calling 046 (823) 9999, Yokosuka Arts Theater Telephone Reservation Center.
