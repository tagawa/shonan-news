Seven dance groups that practice at local community centers will perform genres including flamenco, hula, country, and hip-hop at the Samukawa Dance Festival. The event is on July 25 from 13:30 to 15:30 with doors opening at 13:00, held at Samukawa Town Civic Center Hall; admission is free though advance tickets are distributed. For inquiries, call 0467-74-2333 (the same center).
