A talk event titled "Memory, Place, and Cinema—The Form Called Cinekoya" will be held on July 4 (Saturday). The representative of Cinekoya in Kugenuma Kaigan will join the discussion to consider what a movie theater is; it runs from 14:00 to 16:30 with doors opening at 13:30 at 391THEATRE (Fujisawa Meiten Building 6F). Admission is 1,500 yen and includes a drink and light snack; advance registration is required via the QR code and limited to the first 20 applicants.
