To celebrate its 1st anniversary, Michi-no-Eki "Shonan Chigasaki" will hold events including a hula dance show on July 7, a vegetable all-you-can-pack event, and tasting sessions. Commemorative tickets will be distributed to the first 300 customers who spend ¥1,000 or more (tax included). Check the facility website for event times; location is Michi-no-Eki "Shonan Chigasaki" and inquiries by phone at 0467-33-6360.
