An amateur rakugo performance will be held at the church on June 13, aimed at first-timers and children; no reservation required and there will be a shamisen hands-on experience. Time is 14:00–16:30 with doors opening at 13:30, location is Kugenuma Megumi Lutheran Church, admission is 500 yen for adults and free for elementary school children or younger when accompanied by a guardian; contact via the official LINE account.
