Radio Shonan, celebrating its 30th anniversary, will host a gratitude event on June 6 featuring disaster-preparedness activities, stage performances, a flea market, food and hands-on experiences. The event runs from 10:00 to 16:00 and will be canceled in case of rain; it takes place at Kenritsu Tsujido Seaside Park. For inquiries call 0466（25）7000.
