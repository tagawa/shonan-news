From June 6 to July 12, Kurihama Hana no Kuni will host the Blue Flower Festival where hydrangeas, agapanthus, lavender, and other blue-toned flowers bloom vividly along a roughly 1.2 km slope up to the summit, creating a special blue world for the season. The event runs 10:00 to 16:00 (Mondays from 13:00), is open daily during the period, and admission is free; inquiries to the management office at ☎046（833）8282.
