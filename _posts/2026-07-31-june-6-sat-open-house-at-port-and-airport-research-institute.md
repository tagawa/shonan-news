The Port and Airport Research Institute will hold a special open house on June 6, with hours from 10:00 to 15:00 and reception starting at 9:45. Visitors can observe tsunami behavior, try a drone simulator, and learn about the latest technologies related to ports and airports through hands-on experiences. Admission is free; inquiries by email to pari-koukai@p.mpat.go.jp. More information is available at https://www.pari.go.jp/event/open/2026/index.html.
