On July 15 an ohayashi music float will appear, and on July 16 after the Shinto ritual a Kara-hafu curved gable, lacquered Edo-style portable shrine will parade through six shopping streets in Zushi City. A festival fair will also be held and the event will be lively. Location: centered on Kameoka Hachiman Shrine and the six shopping streets of Zushi; inquiries: ☎︎046（871）4869 Kameoka Hachiman Shrine.
