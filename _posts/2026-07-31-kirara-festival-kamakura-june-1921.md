From June 19 (Friday) to June 21 (Sunday), the Kirara Festival will be held at Kirara Kamakura (Kamakura Lifelong Learning Center) featuring performances and presentations by groups active at the learning center, including music, dance, chorus, and readings, as well as sale of works. On June 19 at 10:00 there will be a paid screening of director Ozu Yasujiro's Tokyo Twilight. Event hours are 10:00–16:00 (until 15:00 on the 21st), admission is free, and inquiries can be made to the center at 0467-25-2030.
