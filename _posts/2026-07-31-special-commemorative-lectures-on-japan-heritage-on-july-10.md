The Kamakura Guide Association will hold special commemorative lectures titled "Kamakura Buddhist Sculptors and Kamakura Carving" on July 10, "Japan Heritage Walked by Lafcadio Hearn" on July 16, and "Writers Who Loved Kamakura" on July 21. Attendance is limited to the first 70 people; time is to be confirmed on the HP, and the venue is Kamakura City Welfare Center 2F. The fee is 800 yen payable at reception on the day, and inquiries can be made by phone at ☎︎0467(24)6548.
