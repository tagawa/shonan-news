An exhibition exploring the relationships between drifting seaweed and marine creatures will run July 18 (Sat) to September 27 (Sun) at the Kannonzaki Nature Museum, open 9:00–17:00 with last entry at 16:30. Original illustrations from Noriko Narahiro's picture book "Nagaremono Tabi" are on display simultaneously. The museum is open daily in July and August; admission is 600 yen for adults and 400 yen for elementary and junior high students. For inquiries call 046(841)1533.
