About 100,000 sunflowers will be in bloom at Nagai Uminote Park Soleil Hill from late July to late September. The Flower Garden, which spreads out immediately inside the park entrance, is especially spectacular, offering a striking combination of blue sky, Sagami Bay, and sunflowers. Hours are 9:00 to 18:00, with seasonal variations, admission is free, and inquiries can be made to 046（857）2500.
