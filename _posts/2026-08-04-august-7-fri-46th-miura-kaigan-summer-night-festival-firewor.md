The annual Miura Kaigan summer tradition returns with fireworks launched from barges on the water, viewable while seated on the wide sandy beach. Highlights include underwater fireworks and a starmine display. The event runs from 19:30 to 20:00 and will be canceled in stormy weather. For inquiries contact the Miura City Tourism Association at 046（888）0588.
