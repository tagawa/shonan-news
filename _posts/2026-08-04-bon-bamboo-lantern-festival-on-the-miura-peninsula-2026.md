Temples in Yokosuka City and Miura City reused bamboo harvested during bamboo grove maintenance to create bamboo lanterns for a serene Obon prayer event. Each temple lit lanterns on its grounds and offered varied activities such as visiting ancestral letters, Bon dances, and food trucks. The event started at 17:00 at each participating temple, and inquiries were handled by Joraku-ji at 046 (856) 8622.
