BRANCH茅ヶ崎 is hosting a summer festival on August 22 (Saturday) and August 23 (Sunday) with traditional festival stalls, Bon Odori dancing, kitchen cars, and other attractions. The event runs from 10:00 to 20:30, with individual activities following their own schedules, and will be held at BRANCH茅ヶ崎 2・3 special event areas and Shiroyama Park. For full details and updates, attendees are asked to check the BRANCH茅ヶ崎 website via the QR code or the event page listed by the venue.
