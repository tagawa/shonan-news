The National Museum of Nature and Science's traveling exhibition is appearing early in Yokosuka, offering hands-on displays that explore the four museum roles: collecting, preserving, communicating, and researching. The exhibit is on view in the main building's special exhibition room at Yokosuka Museum of Natural History, is free of charge, and is open 9:00–17:00 with closure on Mondays. For inquiries, contact the museum at 046-824-3688.
