Kamakura City will hold a report meeting for its peace education program "へいわの学校～修学旅行" on 2026-08-30 (Sunday) at the Kamakura City Hall Third Branch Office auditorium. Twelve elementary and junior high school students who visited Hiroshima will present, in their own words, the thoughts and lessons about peace they gained during the trip. The event showcases student reflections gathered through the city's peace education initiative.
