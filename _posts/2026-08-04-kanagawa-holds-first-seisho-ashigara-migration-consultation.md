Kanagawa Prefecture is organizing a consultation event on 2026-09-06 in Yurakucho, Tokyo, to encourage relocation to the Seisho-Ashigara area, which includes Odawara City, Manazuru Town, and Yugawara Town. The free event requires no advance registration and offers attendees an opportunity to hear firsthand accounts about work, childrearing, and daily life from people who have already moved there. The consultation aims to provide practical information and support for those considering relocation to the region.
