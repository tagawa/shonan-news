Kashō Shōman is running a temporary shop on the 2nd-floor Center Court of Cosca Bayside Stores in Yokosuka from August 1 to 17. The stall offers affordable wagashi including daifuku, warabi mochi, and dango. Purchases at the stall are cash only.
