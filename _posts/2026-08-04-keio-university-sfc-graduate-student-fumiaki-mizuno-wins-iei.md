Fumiaki Mizuno, a third-year doctoral student in the Graduate School of Policy Management and Media Studies at Keio University SFC, received the 21st Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers Communications Society Paper Award (Excellence Paper Award). This marks his second time receiving the award, having previously been honored at the 19th iteration. The recognition highlights Mizuno's continuing contributions to communications research.
