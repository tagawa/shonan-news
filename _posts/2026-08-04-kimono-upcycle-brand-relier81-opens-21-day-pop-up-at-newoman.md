Relier81, a brand that upcycles kimono and obi fabrics, is holding a 21-day pop-up store starting August 6, 2026, on the 6th floor of NEWoMan Yokohama. The shop will showcase new sandals, bags, stoles and other notable items made from repurposed traditional textiles. The event highlights sustainable fashion and traditional Japanese materials given new life through contemporary design.
