The restaurant AGIO natura on the 7th floor of Lumine Ofuna is running a limited Margherita Pizza and Italian Beer Fair for the month of August 2026, from 2026-08-01 through 2026-08-31. Customers who order a pizza receive a raffle ticket, and a lottery will be held with prizes including Kirifuri Kogen beef and pizza vouchers. The promotion highlights Margherita pizza paired with Italian beers as its main offerings during the event.
