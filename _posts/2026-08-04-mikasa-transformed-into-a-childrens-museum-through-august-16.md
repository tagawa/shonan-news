The World War I-era memorial ship Mikasa in Yokosuka is operating as a children’s museum with activities including a stamp rally, craft workshops, dressing in military uniforms for commemorative photos, and a hammock experience. The event runs daily from 9:00 to 17:30, with last boarding at 17:00, at World War I Memorial Ship Mikasa, 82-19 Inaokacho, Yokosuka. Admission is 600 yen for adults, 300 yen for high school students, and free for elementary and junior high students. For more information contact the Mikasa Preservation Foundation at 046-822-5225.
