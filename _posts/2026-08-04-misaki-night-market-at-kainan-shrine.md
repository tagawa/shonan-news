The MISAKI NIGHT MARKET in conjunction with the "Natsumoude 2026" summer shrine visit was held at Kainan Shrine on August 2, 2026. Visitors could offer prayers at the shrine and enjoy food stalls and bargain finds between 16:00 and 19:30. A dedication program of hula dance and shrine maiden dances began at 17:00. For inquiries, the shrine listed the contact number ☎046（881）3038.
