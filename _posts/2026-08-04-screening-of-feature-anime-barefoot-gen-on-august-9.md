As part of Zushi Peace Day (August 7–11), a screening of the feature anime "Barefoot Gen" will be shown on August 9 from 10:00 to 11:45 at Zushi Bunka Plaza Nagisa Hall. The event aims to convey the importance of life and peace through an anime accessible to children, and reproduced original manga drawings will be on display. Admission is free and no reservation is required. For inquiries, contact Iida at 046 (871) 5189.
