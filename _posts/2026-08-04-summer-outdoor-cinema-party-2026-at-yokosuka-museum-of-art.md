As a pre-event ahead of the museum's September 5 reopening, the Yokosuka Museum of Art will turn its Sea Plaza into an outdoor theater on August 29 (Saturday) and August 30 (Sunday). The screening, scheduled from 18:30 to about 20:10, will show "ひつじのショーン～バック・トウ・ザ・ホーム～" and is free with open attendance on the day; in case of rain, advance registration will be required.
