An AI hands-on workshop called "AIで、自分だけのLINEスタンプ画像をつくろう" was held on August 5 at the GS貸会議室藤沢南口店, a rental conference room near Fujisawa Station South Exit in Fujisawa City. The event introduced elementary school students to AI tools for making their own LINE sticker images and was pitched as a possible summer vacation independent research project. The workshop was reported by 湘南経済新聞.
