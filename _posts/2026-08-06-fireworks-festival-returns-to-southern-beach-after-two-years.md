The event, titled the 52nd Southern Beach Chigasaki Fireworks Festival, marked the first time fireworks returned to the venue in two years. Organizers reused shells that had been canceled nationwide, allowing the display to go ahead at Southern Beach Chigasaki in Chigasaki City, Kanagawa Prefecture.
