OISO BEACH SIDE BBQ, an outdoor barbecue facility, opened on July 25 inside Oiso Long Beach in Oiso Town's Kofuhongo area. The site is located next to the park's wave pool and is divided into three areas organized by use. The new facility aims to offer visitors a variety of barbecue experiences within the popular Shonan seaside complex.
