An event titled “Shall we draw on the nursery wall together?” will be held on August 8 at Potofu Hiratsuka Nursery in Amanuma, Hiratsuka City. Parents and children are invited to join Chigasaki-based painter Yamanaka Tomio to create artwork on the nursery's exterior wall. The event was announced by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
