The Shonan Bali Friendship Association presented the results of its inspection trip to Bali, Indonesia, to Mayor Tsuneo Suzuki on July 31. The report outlines findings intended to expand exchange between Fujisawa and Bali. The meeting signals a push to strengthen cultural and possibly economic ties following the association's visit.
