A pop-up store called SPICY HAPPINESS IN summer! featuring Shin Ramyun brand PUNSIK has opened inside the AOSHIRO RESORT beach house at Katase Nishihama Beach, Katase Kaigan 2, Fujisawa City. The store opened on July 18 and offers a themed experience including an automatic cooking device that evokes the Han River dining style. The pop-up is part of summer beach offerings at the Katase Nishihama bathing area.
