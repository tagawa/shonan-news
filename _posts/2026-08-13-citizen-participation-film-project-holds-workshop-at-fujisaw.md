A workshop for the citizen-participation film project titled "Meiten nite 'Workshop no you na mono'" was held on August 7 at the Fujisawa Meiten Building (2 Minami-Fujisawa), in front of Fujisawa Station. The event invited local residents to take part in creating the film through hands-on activities and collaborative sessions. The project is presented as a community-driven production aiming to involve citizens in various aspects of filmmaking.
