An ikebana (flower-arranging) artist from Fujisawa organized a free screening of the anime film "ペリリュー －楽園のゲルニカ－." The event will take place on August 19 at the beachside hut DEEP BLUE at Katase Higashihama swimming beach, located at Fujisawa-shi Katase Kaigan 1. The screening is open to the public with no admission charge.
