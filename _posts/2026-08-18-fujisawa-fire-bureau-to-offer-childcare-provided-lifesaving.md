The Fujisawa City Fire Bureau is offering a "childcare-provided lifesaving course" that lets parents bring young children while they learn infant and pediatric cardiopulmonary resuscitation and how to use an automated external defibrillator. The session is scheduled for September 11 at Chogo Civic Center in Fujisawa's Chogo neighborhood. The program aims to encourage parents raising young children to take lifesaving training they might otherwise miss due to childcare constraints.
