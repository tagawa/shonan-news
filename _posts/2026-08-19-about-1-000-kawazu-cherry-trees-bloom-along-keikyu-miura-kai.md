Approximately 1000 Kawazu cherry trees lining the tracks from Keikyu Miura Kaigan Station have come into full bloom, creating a popular viewing spot. Visitors and tourists flocked to the area and the related "Sakura Matsuri" cherry blossom festival, enlivening the neighborhood and local businesses. The blooms along the railway are a seasonal attraction promoted in local media such as Yokosuka Keizai Shimbun.
