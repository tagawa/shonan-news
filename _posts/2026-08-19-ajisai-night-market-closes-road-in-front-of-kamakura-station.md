The event, called かまくら駅前あじさい夜市, will feature booths run by local restaurants and other vendors and will be held on June 26. Organizers are blocking vehicle traffic on the road in front of the Tokyu Store at Kamakura Station East Exit to host the market. The announcement was reported by 鎌倉経済新聞.
