The summer Koshien tournament has started, and at the prefectural level locals cheered for home teams while watching Ibaraki's competition closely. Joso Gakuin High School, captained by Mizuguchi Kotaro, a graduate of Nobi Junior High, advanced to the prefectural final. The coverage highlighted local ties and community interest in Joso Gakuin's run toward the national tournament.
