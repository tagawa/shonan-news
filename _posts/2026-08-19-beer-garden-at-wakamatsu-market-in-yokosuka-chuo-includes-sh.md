The outdoor event, called Yokosuka Beer Garden, will close the main street of the Wakamatsu Market dining district in front of Yokosuka-Chuo Station to create a pedestrian zone. It takes place at Wakamatsu Market, 3 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka City, and will feature Showa-era retro "nagashi" street music performances along with food and drink stalls. The event aims to activate the local dining street and attract visitors to the area.
