An experiential event called BLUE FESTA 2026 took place on May 30 in the parking plaza of Kamakura City Hall to commemorate the 60th anniversary of the sister city partnership between Kamakura and Nice, France. The event highlighted cultural and maritime connections between the two cities as part of anniversary celebrations. The report was published by 鎌倉経済新聞.
