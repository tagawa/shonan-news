Daihatsu Ninomiya Store and Hiratsuka Sakuragaoka Store are promoting a leasing service that lets customers drive a new Daihatsu kei-car for as little as ¥15,000 per month while keeping costs minimal. The campaign responds to surging private car demand during the intense summer heat, with residents saying a car is essential for daily life. The offer emphasizes affordability and convenience for people seeking their own vehicle amid rising need.
