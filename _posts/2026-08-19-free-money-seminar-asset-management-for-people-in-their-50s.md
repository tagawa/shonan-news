Amid persistent worries about retirement funding and questions like "Is it still possible to prepare for retirement?" and "Should I start NISA?", financial planner Naoto Ishikawa led a free seminar focusing on asset management for people in their 50s. The seminar emphasized moving beyond relying solely on savings, explained options such as NISA, and offered practical steps to seek lifelong financial security. Attendees were encouraged that it is still possible to prepare for retirement by adjusting investment approaches and using available tax-advantaged accounts.
