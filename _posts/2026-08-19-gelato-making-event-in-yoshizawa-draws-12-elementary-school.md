Organized to strengthen community ties, a gelato-making workshop was held at Hiratsuka City Yoshizawa Community Center on July 24. Twelve pairs of elementary-school students and their guardians participated and learned to make gelato using milk from a local牧場直営のジェラート店. The event aimed to connect residents with the Yoshizawa district’s local dairy and promote interaction among families.
