The Misaki Gospel Circle, a group based in Miura City, performed a live gospel concert on the stage at the MIURA FUN BEACH Miura Beach bathing area on August 8. The event featured members including a singer from Moroiso and aimed to share spiritual and uplifting music with beachgoers. Organizers say the outdoor seaside setting added emotional resonance to the performance and attracted local residents and visitors.
