Kanagawa Prefectural Hadano High School will mark its 100th anniversary on October 29. Town News Hadano Branch (Hadano Editorial Office and Isehara Editorial Office) will publish a "Hadano High School 100th Anniversary Special Issue" to commemorate the milestone and is soliciting sponsorships from individuals and businesses. The special issue aims to feature alumni, local organizations, and contributors; details on sponsorship levels and deadlines are provided by the publisher.
