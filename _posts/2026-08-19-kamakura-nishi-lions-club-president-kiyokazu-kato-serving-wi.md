Kato Kiyokazu, a member since the club's 1985 founding, has become president for the fifth time and emphasizes ‘service with gratitude.’ He acknowledges the club’s aging membership and has made spreading the Lions spirit to young people in the community a theme for his term. Activities will be conducted cheerfully and enjoyably while focusing on engaging younger residents and sustaining the club’s service work.
