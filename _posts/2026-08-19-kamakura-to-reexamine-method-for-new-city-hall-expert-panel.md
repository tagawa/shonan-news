Kamakura City plans to hold the first meeting of an external expert panel on August 17 to discuss new construction methods for the planned new city hall and related facilities in the Fukasawa district. The panel will examine approaches aimed at reducing the city’s fiscal burden for the project. Mayor 松尾崇 is overseeing the review as the city seeks financially sustainable options for the development.
