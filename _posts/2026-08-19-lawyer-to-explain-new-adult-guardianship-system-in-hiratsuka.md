The lecture, titled "変わる成年後見 新制度の最新動向," is for Hiratsuka City residents and will take place on Friday, September 18, from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. The venue is the Museum Hall on the first floor of Hiratsuka City Museum, 1-3-3 Nishi-Yahata, Hiratsuka. A lawyer will explain the new adult guardianship system and recent developments; the event is intended to inform citizens about legal changes affecting guardianship.
