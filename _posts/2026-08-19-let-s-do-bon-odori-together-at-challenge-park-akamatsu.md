The Akamatsu, Honjuku, and Akamatsucho Summer Bon Odori Executive Committee hosted a neighborhood Bon Odori festival on August 9 at Challenge Park Akamatsu (Chigasaki City, Akamatsucho 13). The event ran from 6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m., with the first half featuring beginner-friendly lessons for those new to Bon Odori. Participants enjoyed traditional summer dancing and community festivities throughout the evening.
