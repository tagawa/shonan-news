Matsubayashi Junior High School’s girls basketball team defeated their Chigasaki rivals in the final of the Shonan block of the 60th Kanagawa Prefectural Junior High School Basketball Tournament on July 24 at Shinko Sports Samukawa Arena. The victory earned them the block championship and a place in the prefectural tournament, where they reached the best eight. The team’s performance was highlighted by strong teamwork and standout contributions from key players supplied by the school.
