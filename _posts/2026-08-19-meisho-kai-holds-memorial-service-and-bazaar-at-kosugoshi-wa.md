Meisho-kai, led by chairman 坂巻克美, held a memorial service and bazaar on August 13 at the Reiko Mujin Tower, the Kosugoshi war-dead memorial, where members offered flowers and maintained the monument. The group conducts regular cleanings and other activities to preserve the memory of the war. The event combined commemorative rituals with a community bazaar to engage local residents and support ongoing upkeep of the site.
