The Nakanoi Town Social Welfare Council will host the 'Welfare Summer Festival' at Nakanoi Town Health and Welfare Center (Hinakubo) on August 23, Sunday, from 10:00 to 14:00, with activities proceeding even in rain. The event will feature hands-on experiences and displays, including wheelchair demonstrations and other welfare-related exhibits and activities. Organizers aim to raise awareness of welfare services and provide opportunities for residents to learn about accessibility and care through practical experiences.
