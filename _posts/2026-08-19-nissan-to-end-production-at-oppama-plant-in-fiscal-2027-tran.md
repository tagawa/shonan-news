Nissan Motor Co. said on July 15 that as part of its Re:Nissan restructuring plan it will cease vehicle production at its main Oppama plant in Natsushima-cho, Yokosuka City, by the end of fiscal 2027. The company said the move is part of a global review of production sites and that output will be shifted to its Kyushu plants. The decision is framed as a measure to streamline production under the broader corporate turnaround effort.
