The Otakaraya Nishi-Yawata store is currently promoting higher purchase prices for brand watches and items made of gold and platinum, with a special campaign limited to August. Brands such as Rolex and Omega are highlighted as particularly likely to receive high appraisals. The store emphasizes its experienced female staff and track record in valuing items, encouraging customers to bring in watches and precious metals during the campaign period.
