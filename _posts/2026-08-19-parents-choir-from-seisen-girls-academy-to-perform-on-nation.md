La Fonte Serena, a choir formed mainly of parents of students at Seisen Girls' Academy in Kamakura and led by Ayaka Yokose, qualified to perform at the national stage. The group will sing at Fukuyama Arts and Culture Hall Riden on August 22 and 23 as part of the event. The choir's achievement highlights community involvement in the school's music program and the members' dedication to ensemble singing.
