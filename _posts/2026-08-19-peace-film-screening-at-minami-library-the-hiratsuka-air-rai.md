A general public peace film event titled '平塚大空襲〜あの日、焼夷弾の雨が降った' will be shown on August 23 (Sunday) at the Nagisa Fureai Center second-floor open space, located within the Minami Library facility at 20-1 Sodegahama in Hiratsuka. The screening is organized by the Minami Library and takes place in the same building as the library. The event is intended to remember and educate about the Hiratsuka air raids through film.
