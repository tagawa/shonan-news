In Chigasaki city middle school softball competition, longtime rivals Hagisono Junior High and Hamasuka Junior High have been splitting championships in recent years. The city tournament held on July 4 and 11 saw the two schools face each other in the final. The closely contested final showcased strong performances from both teams, continuing their pattern of trading titles across local tournaments.
