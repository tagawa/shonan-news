The Fujisawa Citizens' Comprehensive Sports Tournament succession meet, the 39th gymnastics apparatus competition, was held August 1–2 at Akibadai Cultural Gymnasium. Eighteen teams and 321 athletes from kindergarten, elementary, junior high, high school and general categories showcased routines reflecting their regular practice. Events included performances with rings and ribbon work, highlighting both rhythmic and artistic gymnastics elements. Organizers promoted the meet as a chance for community athletes to display skill development and foster continuity in local sports.
