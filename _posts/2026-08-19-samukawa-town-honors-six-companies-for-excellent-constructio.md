Samukawa Town held the "令和８年度寒川町優良建設工事表彰式" on July 17 at the Town Citizen Center to recognize exemplary contractors and designers responsible for town-commissioned projects. The awards promote proper execution and technical improvement of municipal construction work; six companies received certificates of commendation and letters of appreciation. Recipients included firms judged to have demonstrated superior workmanship and design, serving as models for others.
