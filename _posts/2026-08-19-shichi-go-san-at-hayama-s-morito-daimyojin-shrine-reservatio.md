Morito Daimyojin, Hayama's chief guardian shrine founded by Minamoto no Yoritomo and located at Horiuchi 1025 facing Sagami Bay with views of Mount Fuji, is hosting Shichi-Go-San celebrations. The shrine and the nearby marina held reservation events for families planning Shichi-Go-San ceremonies. Kimono dressing services for the celebrations are being provided by Marusho Yokohama.
