The buyback shop Uru Navi, on the second floor of Ito-Yokado Shonan-dai near Shonan-dai Station west exit, is holding a campaign strengthening purchases of pearl necklaces this month. For this store only, authentic pearl necklaces will be accepted and the shop is guaranteeing at least 1,000 yen for qualifying items through the end of August. Customers are encouraged not to throw away pearl jewelry but to bring it to the store for appraisal under the campaign terms.
