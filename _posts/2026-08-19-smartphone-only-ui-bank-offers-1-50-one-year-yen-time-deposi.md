UI Bank, a smartphone-only bank promoting digital shifts across all generations, is holding a time-deposit campaign through September 30 that offers a one-year yen fixed deposit at an annual 1.50% interest rate. The bank pairs its high-rate and fee-preference services with in-person assistance provided at Kiraboshi Bank branches to help customers who prefer face-to-face support. The campaign is part of UI Bank’s efforts to combine competitive yields and customer service as it expands digital banking adoption.
