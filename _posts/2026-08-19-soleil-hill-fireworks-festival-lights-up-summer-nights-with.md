An event featuring approximately 500 fireworks was held over two evenings, August 15 (Saturday) and August 16 (Sunday), at Nagai Uminote Park "Soleil Hill." The production was led, as last year, by a teacher from Shonan Gakuin High School. The festival aimed to brighten summer nights for local visitors and families attending the park.
