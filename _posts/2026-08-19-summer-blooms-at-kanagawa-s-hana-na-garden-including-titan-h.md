Hana-na Garden in Teradanawa, Hiratsuka City, is showcasing summer blooms such as titan hibiscus and crape myrtle. The titan hibiscus has reached peak season with petals more than 20cm in diameter and heights of 180cm. Visitors can enjoy the vibrant summer colors despite strong sunshine. The garden is highlighting a variety of heat-tolerant summer flowers currently at their best.
