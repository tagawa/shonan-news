The campaign invites homeowners to apply for a free valuation to help plan for retirement, downsizing after children move out, or inheritance measures. The offer is run by three real-estate companies in collaboration with Town News and aims to encourage families to share and understand their home’s value and assessment. Applications must be submitted by September 11. The program emphasizes preparing for the future by clarifying property worth and options for one’s residence.
