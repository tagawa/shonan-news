Kamakura Park Hotel, known for its panoramic Sagami Bay views, is offering a summer-limited lunch fair that highlights locally sourced seasonal ingredients. The event includes a lavish lunch buffet plus a seasonal kaiseki course, designed to showcase the best of regional summer produce. The promotion targets guests seeking a high-end dining experience during the peak summer season. Reservations and details are available through the hotel.
