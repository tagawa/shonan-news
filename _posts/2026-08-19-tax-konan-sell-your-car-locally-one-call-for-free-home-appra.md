Tax Konan advised local car owners to consider selling to a neighborhood dealer when faced with costly repairs, noting that cars, like people, suffer in summer and often have AC failures. The store promotes a simple process: one phone call arranges a free, no-obligation on-site appraisal. This service targets owners weighing expensive summer repairs against the option to sell their vehicle locally for convenience and potentially better value.
