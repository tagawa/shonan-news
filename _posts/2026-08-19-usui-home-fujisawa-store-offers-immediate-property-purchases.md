Usui Home, a real estate-focused company, says its Fujisawa store is stepping up purchases of houses, land, condominiums, and apartments. The company will buy properties even if they show dirt, damage, or age-related wear, and it does not require owners to clean or clear out interiors before sale. The campaign is aimed at owners of vacant properties who want a quick sale without the burden of preparing the property for market.
