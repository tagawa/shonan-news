Usui Home's Yokosuka Chuo branch warns that persistent interest rate hikes and broad inflation are increasing costs from daily goods to energy, so owners should closely watch maintenance costs of properties they hold. The company advises that high-price conditions can make asset reorganization or sale advantageous, helping reduce ongoing upkeep and tax burdens. Usui Home recommends reviewing property expenses and market conditions now to decide whether to sell, consolidate, or otherwise manage real estate assets.
