Usui Home Zushi, one of 40 branches across the prefecture, is promoting its immediate cash purchase service for single-family homes, land, and condominiums. The company is known for buying properties regardless of age and can purchase interiors as-is, allowing owners to sell without cleaning or renovations. The service aims to resolve a range of housing-related problems quickly by providing direct, on-the-spot payments. Usui Home positions this offer to simplify transactions for sellers who need fast liquidity or want to avoid the usual preparation required for resale.
