National chain 買取店わかば (Wakaba Buyback Shop) is strengthening purchases of telephone cards and alcoholic beverages at its 湘南モールフィル店 (Shonan Mall Fill branch) through August 20. During the campaign, unused 50-unit phonecards are being bought for 400 yen each. The shop is promoting increased cash payments for these items while the limited campaign period lasts.
