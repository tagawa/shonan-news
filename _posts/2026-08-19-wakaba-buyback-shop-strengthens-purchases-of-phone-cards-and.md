Nationwide chain 買取店わかば, with over 250 locations, is running a limited campaign at its Chigasaki South Exit store to increase purchases of telephone cards and alcoholic beverages through August 20. During the period, unused 50-unit telephone cards are guaranteed at ¥400 each. The campaign aims to attract local sellers by offering set buyback terms for these specific items.
