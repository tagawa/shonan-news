Nationwide chain 買取店わかば, with more than 250 locations, says the Tamaya Kamakura Tebyō store is strengthening purchases of telephone cards and alcoholic beverages until August 20. During the campaign, unused 50-unit telephone cards are guaranteed a buyback price of 400 yen each. The promotion applies while supplies last and is intended to encourage customers to bring in eligible items for immediate cash payment.
