Yokosuka Bilingual School (YBS), based in Yokosuka City, received a Brands For Good Awards 2026 international award in Singapore on July 22. The school was honored for its educational approach that emphasizes respect for diversity and inclusive practices. YBS highlighted its bilingual curriculum and community engagement as key factors in earning the recognition. School officials said the award will encourage further development of programs that support varied learning needs and cultural backgrounds.
