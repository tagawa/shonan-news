The long-running summer event, the 52nd Southern Beach Chigasaki Fireworks Festival, took place on August 1 at Southern Beach Chigasaki, drawing cheers after a one-year cancellation due to severe weather. Organizers launched a program of 2,000 fireworks, marking a rare return after many firework displays nationwide were canceled. Local residents and visitors enjoyed the display that restored a familiar summer tradition to Chigasaki.
