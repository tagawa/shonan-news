President Nakagawa Nobuyoshi said the club’s slogan for this fiscal year is "伝統の継承を軸とするスマートな運営" (Smart management centered on preserving traditions). He outlined three priorities: meaningful, creative meetings; enjoyable fellowship activities; and sustained, connected service projects. The aim is to strengthen mutual support among members while continuing the club’s traditional service work.
