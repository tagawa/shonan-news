This fiscal year the club celebrates its 50th anniversary, a milestone President 須藤伸 (Nobu Sudo) called an important year to enjoy change and pass the baton together. To mark the anniversary, the club plans public programs including a disaster-preparedness event for elementary school students and other community activities aimed at strengthening member unity and outreach. President Sudo emphasized making the year grand while keeping members engaged and collaborative.
