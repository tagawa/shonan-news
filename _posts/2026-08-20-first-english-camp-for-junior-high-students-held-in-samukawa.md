The Samukawa English Camp for junior high students was held for the first time on July 30 at the Samukawa Town Citizens' Center. Ten local junior high students participated in the all-English program designed to expose them to practical, conversational English. Organizers aimed to help students become familiar with 'living English' through immersive activities and interaction. The event marked a new local effort to boost English communication skills among young residents.
