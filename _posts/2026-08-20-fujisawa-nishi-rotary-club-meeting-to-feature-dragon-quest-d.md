The Fujisawa Nishi Rotary Club will host a meeting on September 10 (Thursday) at Mina Park where Kazuhiko Nakanishi, who worked on the nationally beloved RPG series Dragon Quest, will give a lecture. His talk is titled "From Zero, Create a World" and will cover game development and world-building from initial concept. The event is organized by the service group as part of its regular meetings; further details such as start time or attendance requirements were not provided in the notice.
