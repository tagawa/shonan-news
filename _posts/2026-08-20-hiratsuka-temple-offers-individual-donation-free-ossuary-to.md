Jorenenji temple in Satsubacho, Hiratsuka City, named the project "Annon" and marketed individual-type ossuary plots designed to relieve families' future burial anxieties. The first phase sold out, and a limited second phase of 80 plots is now available, so early visits are recommended. The facility emphasizes a calm, peaceful atmosphere and does not require donations for use.
