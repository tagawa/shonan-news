Chigasaki City Regional Medical Center (Chigasaki 3-4-23) operates holiday and nighttime emergency services. On Sundays and public holidays, internal medicine and the pharmacy are open from 9:00 to 23:00, while pediatrics, surgery, and dentistry have different hours. Patients are asked to contact the center in advance before visiting, and dental cases are handled separately. The center’s schedule and procedures aim to ensure orderly care during off-hours.
