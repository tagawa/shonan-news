The citizen group "被爆体験を語り継ぐ会" will hold a study session on August 26 titled "核兵器と私たち 〜非核三原則を自分ごとに〜". Speaker Takahashi from the group "かたわら" will discuss Japan's government policy of not possessing, not producing, and not permitting nuclear weapons and explore what those principles mean for individuals. The event aims to connect national non-nuclear policy to everyday responsibilities and encourage civic reflection on nuclear issues.
