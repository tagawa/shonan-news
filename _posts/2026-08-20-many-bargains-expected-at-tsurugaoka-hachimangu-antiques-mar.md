The 24th Kamakura Antiques Market will take place on August 29 (Saturday) on the grounds of Tsurugaoka Hachimangu. The event is organized by the festival executive committee chaired by 吉川淳也. At the previous market held in late July, families and antique enthusiasts attended; the upcoming event is expected to feature many bargain finds and attract similar crowds.
