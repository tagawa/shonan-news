The Fujisawa Fusen no Chikai Peace Action Executive Committee held a citizen-participation peace gathering on August 15 (Saturday) at the Tsujido Station north exit deck under the banner NO WAR. The event ran from 16:00 to 17:30 and aimed to pass on a country that will not wage war to children, inviting public participation in prayers and actions for peace. Organizers emphasized remembrance of the end of the war and commitment to nonviolence.
