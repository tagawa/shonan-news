The campaign calls out, “Do you have any sleeping jewelry?” and highlights four local branches: Otakara-ya Hiratsuka Nakahara, Otakara-ya MaxValu Hiratsuka Kawachi, Otakara-ya Seiyu Hiratsuka, and Otakara-ya Oiso Ekimae. The TV commercial is part of the nationwide Otakara-ya chain’s effort to reassure customers and encourage them to bring in unwanted accessories for appraisal or sale. The story emphasizes the convenience and trust of using a national chain for handling personal items.
