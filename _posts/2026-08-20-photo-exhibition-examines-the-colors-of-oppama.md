Students from Yokohama National University are holding a unique photo exhibition that focuses on the colors present in the streets of Oppama, Yokosuka. The show highlights vivid reds of shop signs, greens of plants growing on Mount Takatori, and blues of playground equipment, presenting everyday town scenes through a color-based perspective. The exhibition is taking place inside the Oppama Eki-Machi-Michi Design Center, inviting visitors to notice and reflect on the local palette.
