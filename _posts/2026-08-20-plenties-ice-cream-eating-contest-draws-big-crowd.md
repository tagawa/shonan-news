The annual summer event at Plenties Chigasaki Main Store in Higashikaigan-kita, Chigasaki, took place on July 25, drawing many spectators. Contestants competed to eat as much of the shop’s cup ice cream as possible in five minutes, delivering an intense, closely fought competition in the high temperatures. The event continues to be a popular local attraction that highlights summer festivities at the store.
