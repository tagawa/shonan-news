Masuyama, who lives in Kamakura and is 48 years old, released his debut picture book on July 26. The book, centered on the themes of life, stars, and dragons, traces its origins to ideas he began developing five years earlier after the death of his father-in-law. Masuyama said the work reflects his view of the world and his interest in depicting life, and it marks his first published effort in children’s literature.
