A screening of the film '花緑青が明ける日に', which uses Miura City as its motif, was held on August 1 at Miura Beach, where scenes evoke sunset skies, sea breezes and waves. Director Shinomiya, who was born in Miura City and wrote the original and the screenplay, attended and gave a talk alongside the screening. The event highlighted local landscapes remembered in the film and connected attendees with the director's personal memories of the area.
