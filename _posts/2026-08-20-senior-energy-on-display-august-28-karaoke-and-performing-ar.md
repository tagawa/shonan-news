The 15th Karaoke and Performing Arts Contest will take place on August 28 (Friday) at Kirara Kamakura (Kamakura City Lifelong Learning Center) Hall. The event is organized by Mirai Furu Kamakura and the Yokohama City Sakae Ward Senior Club Federation and will feature performances by their senior members. The two groups collaborate to showcase seniors' vitality through singing and other stage arts.
