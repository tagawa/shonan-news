Shōichi Kuroda, who has supported the organization since its founding in 2023, took office as the satellite club’s fourth chair. The club is affiliated with Minami Rotary Club and has many young members, fostering strong relationships that cross age and occupational boundaries. Kuroda aims to continue strengthening those ties and supporting activities that reflect the club’s diverse membership.
