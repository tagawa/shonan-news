The Shonan Yosakoi Festival held a photo contest on June 7 that drew about 100 submissions, and the prize winners were recently announced. The top prize went to Kazuo Suzuki of Nishi-Koiso, Oiso Town, for his photo titled "夏はじけて". Organizers selected additional winning entries from the pool of submissions, completing the contest awards.
