The 21st annual light and sound festival, Southern Beach Festa 2026, took place at Southern Beach Chigasaki on July 25. Although part of the program was canceled mid-event, a total of 7,500 people attended, with 2,500 visitors recorded at one point during the day. Performances included acts such as "LOVE & PRIDE," and organizers reported strong overall participation despite disruptions.
