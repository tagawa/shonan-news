With inheritance and will disputes on the rise, Sunagawa Judicial Scrivener Office warns that lack of preparation is a key factor when problems arise. Now that inheritance registration is mandatory, the office urges people to seek expert advice in advance to avoid unnecessary family conflicts. It emphasizes consultations cover inheritance registration, wills, and debt-related issues, and the office provides free advice as often as needed to help families plan and resolve disputes.
