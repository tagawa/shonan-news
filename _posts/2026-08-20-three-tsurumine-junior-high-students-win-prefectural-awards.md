The Kanagawa Prefecture awards ceremony for the 48th All-Japan Junior High Water Essay Contest was held on July 28 at Kanagawa Prefectural Office. Students Keita Kobayashi, Miu Nakamura, and Akito Yoneyama from Chigasaki City Tsurumine Junior High School received honors in the prefectural competition. The contest encourages awareness of water issues and writing skills among junior high students across Japan.
