The Tsutsumi Summer Bon Dance Festival is scheduled for August 8 (Saturday) in the Tsutsumi Community Hall parking lot, running from 5:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. If it rains, the event will be postponed to August 9. The program includes traditional Bon dance songs as well as music aimed at young people and children, and additional activities will take place at the venue.
