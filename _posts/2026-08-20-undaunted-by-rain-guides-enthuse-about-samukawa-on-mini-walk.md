The Samukawa Tourism Association ran a hands-on mini tour called “Would you like to try being a tourism guide?” at the Samukawa Chamber of Commerce to promote the Samukawa Tourism Guide Club. Despite rain, club guides led participants on short walks, demonstrated commentary techniques, and shared local sights and history to attract new volunteers and visitors. Organizers emphasized practical experience for prospective guides and the club’s role in boosting local tourism. The event aimed to recruit and train people interested in guiding and to raise awareness of Samukawa’s attractions.
