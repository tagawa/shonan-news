Usui Home's Kinugasa store is offering to purchase vacant and ageing houses in the Kinugasa area, which includes lively shopping streets, flat urban zones, and steep residential hills. The service responds to problems caused by owner ageing and generational change, and it relieves owners of the need to clean up or remove remaining possessions before sale. The company aims to address the urgent issue of neglected vacant homes and old properties by simplifying transactions for owners who can no longer maintain their houses.
