The nationwide Wakaba buyback chain, which operates more than 250 stores, is running a limited campaign at its A-Coop Asahi location in Manda, Hiratsuka, through August 20. During the campaign, unused 50-unit telephone cards are being bought for 400 yen each. The store is also increasing purchases of alcoholic beverages; customers are encouraged to bring items they wish to sell before the campaign ends.
