Wakaba, part of a nationwide network of over 250 buyback stores, is reinforcing purchases of telephone cards and alcoholic beverages at its Aeon Kurihama location. The campaign runs through August 20 and guarantees 400 yen for each unused 50-unit telephone card during the period. Other details about alcohol purchase terms were promoted as part of the limited-time campaign at the store.
