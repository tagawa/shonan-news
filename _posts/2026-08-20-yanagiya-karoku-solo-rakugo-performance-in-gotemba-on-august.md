Yanagiya Karoku, a popular rakugo performer who frequently appears on television, will hold a solo performance at Gotemba Civic Hall large hall (御殿場市民会館大ホール), 183-1 Hagiwara, Gotemba, Shizuoka, on August 23. The show starts at 2:00 p.m. The item was part of a reader giveaway with an August 19 deadline for entries.
