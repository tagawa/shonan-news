The Yokosuka High School Mita-kai, a group of alumni who also graduated from Keio University, held its regular general meeting on July 19 at the Central Hotel in Wakamatsucho, Yokosuka City. About 20 people attended, and the event featured a lecture by a former Chief of Joint Staff (元統合幕僚長). Attendees also used the gathering to reconnect with fellow alumni and discuss association matters.
