On July 15, Yoshizawa Post Office and Japan Post Bank delivered a financial-education class at Hiratsuka Municipal Tsuchiya Elementary School, led by Principal Toru Igarashi. The lesson, arranged after last year’s initial session prompted by the post office, used hands-on activities that made money flow around the room, as noted by students. The program aimed to teach children how money circulates and basic banking concepts through interactive teaching methods.
