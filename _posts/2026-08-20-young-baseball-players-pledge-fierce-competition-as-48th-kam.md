The 48th Kamakura Lions Club Youth Baseball League kicked off on August 1 at Fueda Park Stadium in Kamakura, with 11 teams taking part. The teams are split into Eastern and Western leagues and will compete for the Lions Club flag. Players and coaches pledged spirited play at the opening ceremony. The league will continue with scheduled games as teams vie for the championship.
