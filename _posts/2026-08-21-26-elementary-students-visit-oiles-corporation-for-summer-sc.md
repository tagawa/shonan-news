A summer school for local fourth- to sixth-grade students from Fujisawa City was held on August 6 at Oiles Corporation in Kirihara Town. The program, aimed at providing a safe place for children during summer vacation when parents may be absent, introduced participants to manufacturing through hands-on activities and facility tours. A total of 26 children took part in the event, which combined education about industry with supervised childcare.
