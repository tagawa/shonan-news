The Miura City Social Welfare Council is holding a Welfare Equipment Idea Contest and is seeking submissions of clever, practical tools that support daily living for elderly and disabled people. The contest aims to raise awareness of welfare needs and collect inventive solutions that can improve accessibility and independence. Organizers encourage wide participation from community members with ideas that are feasible and beneficial for vulnerable residents.
