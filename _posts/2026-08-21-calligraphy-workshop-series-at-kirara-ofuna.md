The Japan Sogo Shogeiin is hosting a three-part calligraphy course at Ofuna Learning Center (Kirara Ofuna, Ofuna 2-1-26). Sessions are scheduled for September 2 (Wednesday), October 7 (Wednesday), and November 4 (Wednesday). The series aims to introduce participants to the beauty of calligraphy through guided lessons.
