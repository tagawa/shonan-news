The duo Curtains consists of Bunta (stage name 三年熟成) and Keito (stage name ミサイルポット), two elementary school students from Zushi City. Formed three years ago, they challenge the annual M-1 Grand Prix each year and have built a reputation for energetic, fast-paced back-and-forth that consistently generates laughs. Their performances mix traditional manzai rhythms with youthful flair, drawing attention in the comedy world despite their young age.
