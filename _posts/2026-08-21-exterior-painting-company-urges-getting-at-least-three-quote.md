Okabe, a staff member at Kamakura-based company (株)高一, advised that homeowners seeking exterior wall painting or roof work should get estimates from three or more companies to compare. The warning follows a recent rise in fraud cases in which individuals pose as legitimate contractors to deceive customers. The company urged vigilance and careful verification of contractors before signing contracts or making payments.
