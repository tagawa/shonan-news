The organization "不動産相談室かながわ" holds monthly free consultations on inheritance and real estate across Kanagawa and reports serving over 3,000 people. The 253rd session is scheduled for August 22 (Saturday) in Room 506 of the Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce, a three-minute walk from Fujisawa Station north exit. Sessions are handled by professionals in inheritance and real estate, and reservations are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis.
