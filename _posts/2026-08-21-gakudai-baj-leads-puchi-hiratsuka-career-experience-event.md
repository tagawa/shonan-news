Gakudai Bajō, 50, who lives in Nesakama, Hiratsuka, was the executive committee chair for the 'Puchi Hiratsuka' event on August 21. The event lets elementary school students try more than 50 jobs and simulate social systems through a mock 'small Hiratsuka City.' Bajō says he is motivated by turning the idea of 'for someone else' into action to help children learn about work and community.
