Kiddy Koshigoe Nursery, run by social welfare corporation Shinkou Fukushikai at Koshigoe 5-11-17, will begin accepting children who require medical care starting April next year. Kamakura City will use national subsidies and other funding to support the initiative, and the nursery will have a nurse to monitor and assist children with medical needs. The move aims to expand care options for families in the city and ensure safe daily attendance for medically dependent children.
