The job-experience event 'Puchi Hiratsuka' will take place on August 21 (Friday) at Hiratsuka Velodrome, located at 久領堤５の１. The event runs from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., is free to attend, and is organized by the Puchi Hiratsuka Executive Committee led by 馬上岳大. It offers local residents, especially children, an opportunity to try out different occupations and learn about regional work through hands-on activities.
