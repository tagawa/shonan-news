When decay progresses to infect the nerve, many dentists say extraction is the only option, but Oral Care Clinic Fujisawa says some of those teeth may still be salvageable. Located a three-minute walk from Fujisawa Station, the clinic offers treatments aimed at preserving natural teeth and is pursuing measures to raise the overall quality of care. The clinic emphasizes techniques and approaches that increase the chances of keeping teeth that would otherwise be removed.
