Kannonzaki Park is seeking five part-time park workers to carry out maintenance duties within the park; people interested in plants are especially welcome. Work hours are generally 8:30–16:30, up to 14 days per month including weekends and holidays, and the hourly wage is 1324 yen. The position offers bonuses paid annually and other employment details are provided by the park administration.
