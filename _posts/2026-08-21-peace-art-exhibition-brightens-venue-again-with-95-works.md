The 38th Chigasaki Peace Art Exhibition took place July 29 to August 1 at the Chigasaki City Cultural Hall exhibition room. Ninety-five works by 65 artists who passed the open-call screening were displayed. The annual show continued its tradition of presenting peace-themed artwork to the local community.
