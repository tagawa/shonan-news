Fujisawa North Police Station, led by Chief Hiroshi Kojima, ran an Open Company event on the 29th aimed at people who hope to take the police recruitment exam and other members of the public. Attendees were invited to observe and participate in hands-on experiences and tours designed to explain and demonstrate various aspects of police work. The event is part of the station's effort to raise awareness and understanding of policing careers.
