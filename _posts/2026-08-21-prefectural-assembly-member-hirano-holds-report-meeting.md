Hirano Migiwa held a prefectural report meeting at the Chigasaki Chamber of Commerce on July 25. She spoke about ongoing debates over designated cities becoming independent from the prefecture and taking over all local administrative functions within their municipal boundaries. Hirano explained the issues and implications for local residents and fielded questions from attendees.
