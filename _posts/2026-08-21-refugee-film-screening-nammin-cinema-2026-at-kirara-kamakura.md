Nammin Cinema 2026 will be held on September 4 at the Kamakura Lifelong Learning Center, also known as Kirara Kamakura. The event is organized by the certified NPO Arupe Nammin Center in Kamakura's Juniso and will screen the film LOST LAND. The organizer is accepting advance applications from participants. Further details on time, fees, and how to apply are being provided by the Arupe Nammin Center.
