Kamakura-gu in Nikaido is holding its annual reitaisai on August 19 and 20. On August 19, events include a children’s mikoshi procession starting at 10:00, dedication performances, Bon dancing and Kamakura night stalls. Activities continue on August 20 with morning events and additional festival offerings.
