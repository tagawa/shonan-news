Samukawa Town Tourism Association is accepting participants for the "Samukawa Shrine Ley Line Experience Tour" scheduled for September 23 (Wednesday, a public holiday). There are two tour options: Course A includes an official worship at the main hall followed by a walk around the shrine grounds and the Kannatake area, while Course B focuses on shrine grounds and nearby spots without the formal main-hall worship. The association is inviting local residents and visitors to apply and experience the shrine's spiritual landscape.
