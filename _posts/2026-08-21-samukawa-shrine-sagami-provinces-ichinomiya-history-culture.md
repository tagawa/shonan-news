Samukawa Shrine, the ichinomiya of Sagami Province, has a history exceeding 1,600 years and is uniquely venerated across Japan as the guardian deity for happon-yoke (protection against misfortune from all directions). The shrine draws more than 3,000,000 worshippers each year and is known for its expansive, solemn precincts where visitors can experience historical architecture, cultural rituals, and abundant natural surroundings. The article highlights the shrine’s long-standing faith, cultural significance, and the serene atmosphere of its grounds.
