The Shonan Regional Workers’ Welfare Council, chaired by Miyagi Hiroyuki and made up of labor unions from Fujisawa, Chigasaki, and Samukawa, donated 150 unused towels to the Koureikyo Fujisawa District Welfare Facilities Liaison on the 5th. The donation was presented at a social gathering held the same day. Council members said they intend to continue supporting the local community.
