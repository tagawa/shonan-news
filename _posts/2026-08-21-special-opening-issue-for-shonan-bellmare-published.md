The Hiratsuka, Oiso, Ninomiya, and Nakai editorial office published the "Shonan Bellmare Opening Special Issue" on August 14 (Friday). The special issue was released together with the August 14 edition of Town News Hiratsuka, Oiso, Ninomiya, and Nakai. The publication highlights the start of the season for Shonan Bellmare and is being distributed locally.
