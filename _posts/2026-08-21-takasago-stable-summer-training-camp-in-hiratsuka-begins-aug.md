The 30th annual Takasago-beya summer training camp will be held at Hiratsuka City General Park sumo arena. Public morning keiko sessions are scheduled for August 21 (Friday) through August 23 (Sunday), and will go ahead even in rain. On August 21, a dohyo matsuri ceremony will start around 8:00 a.m. before the practice begins.
