TOTO Watercolor Studio Shonan Hiratsuka store operator Sankoh Co., Ltd. will host a "Reform Big Consultation" at the TOTO Atsugi Showroom (Atsugi City, Okada 4-3-12) on August 22 and 23. The event will run from morning to afternoon and will display newly released toilets and other product exhibits while offering consultation on home renovation projects. Company staff will be available to discuss estimates and planning for bathroom and other home reforms, and attendees can view the latest fixtures and ask questions about renovation options. The consultation is intended for local residents considering renovations and aims to provide hands-on information and advice.
