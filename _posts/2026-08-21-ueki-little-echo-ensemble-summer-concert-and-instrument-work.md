The Ueki Little Echo Ensemble will present its fourth Summer Concert and Instrument Workshop on August 29 at Ueki Elementary School gymnasium in Kamakura (Ueki 1). The instrument workshop begins at 3:30 p.m., followed by the concert; organizers invite local residents to attend and try instruments. Admission details and program specifics were not provided in the notice.
