Nationwide chain 買取店わかば is promoting purchases at its イオン久里浜店 during August, focusing on telecards and alcoholic beverages. During the campaign, unused 50-unit telecards are guaranteed at a purchase price of 400 yen each. The store is also accepting alcohol items, including ones customers find difficult to dispose of, as part of the strengthened buying program.
