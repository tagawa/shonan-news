The nationwide chain '買取店わかば' with more than 250 stores is strengthening purchases of telephone cards and alcoholic beverages at its Tamaya Kamakura Tebiro branch during August. During the campaign, unused 50-unit telephone cards are guaranteed a buyback price of 400 yen each. The store is also accepting alcohol for cashing in as part of efforts to help customers dispose of unwanted items.
