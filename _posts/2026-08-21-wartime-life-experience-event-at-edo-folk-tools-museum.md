The event, titled "Experience Life During Wartime," was held on August 15 at the Omoshiro Taiken Museum Edo Folk-Tools Road in Nakai Town (Kusho 418). It ran from 1:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m. and was limited to the first 20 participants who signed up. The program was a special企画 for the day marking the end of the war, offering hands-on demonstrations and exhibits about civilian life during wartime.
