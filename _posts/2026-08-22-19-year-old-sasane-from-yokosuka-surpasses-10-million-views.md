Sasane, a 19-year-old singer, model and influencer from Yokosuka City, released her debut single "mosi mosi?" in June 2026 and promoted the song with a music video shot around her hometown. The MV showcases familiar local spots and activities, drawing attention to Yokosuka as well as to Sasane's charms. By the time of the report, the video had surpassed 10,000,000 views, boosting local interest in the artist and her roots.
