The tournament, organized by NPO Chigasaki Baseball Association and hosted by former professional player Yamamoto Masahiro and Chigasaki City, was held at Chigasaki Park from August 8 to August 16. The Chigasaki Chamber of Commerce and Industry Youth Division contributed to youth development by providing medals to the participating baseball boys. Local officials and organizers emphasized the event's role in encouraging young athletes and supporting community sports programs.
