Chigasaki Care Festa 2026 is scheduled for September 5, Saturday, at Chigasaki Shopping Center (Shinsakae-cho 2-30) from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. The event will feature a stage program including a fall-prevention exercise demonstration, as well as other activities and information aimed at promoting caregiving and supporting local residents. Organizers hope the festival will energize the community by raising awareness of care services and encouraging participation.
