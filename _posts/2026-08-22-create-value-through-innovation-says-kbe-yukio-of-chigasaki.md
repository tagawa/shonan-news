Kōbe Yukio, who serves as the inaugural chair of the Chigasaki Ocean Lions Club Asset Consultation Center and is widely trusted, announced the club’s 2026 theme as “革新〜新たな価値の創造〜” (Innovation — Creating New Value). He plans to draw on his experience to take on new challenges and revitalize the club, including increased engagement with student members and other initiatives to boost activity. The emphasis is on combining tradition and fresh ideas to create value for members and the local community.
