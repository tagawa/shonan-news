The Kanagawa middle school beach volleyball tournament was held at Kugenuma Beach on July 31, and Fujisawa Club A, a strong team from the city, captured the boys' title. The victory marked Fujisawa Club A's sixth consecutive championship in this event, reinforcing their dominance in the prefecture. Match details from the boys' division were reported from the day’s competition.
