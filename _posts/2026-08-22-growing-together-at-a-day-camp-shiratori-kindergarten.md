Shiratori Kindergarten in Kawachi, Hiratsuka City, held a day camp for its senior class on July 21. The children made curry, performed wadaiko drums, went on a fun exploration, and enjoyed fireworks. The event was intended as an important summer memory and a chance for the children to grow together.
