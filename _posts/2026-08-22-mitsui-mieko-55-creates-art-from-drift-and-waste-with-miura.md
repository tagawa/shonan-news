Mitsui Mieko is a member of the group Miura Drifting Museum aSOUBOU and uses drifted debris and scrap recovered during beach cleanups as her artistic medium. The word "drifting" in the group's name refers both to the flotsam she collects and to her two-base lifestyle, which nurtures her body and mind. Her work turns environmental cleanup into creative practice, highlighting reuse and coastal conservation.
