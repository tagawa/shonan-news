The Miura Coast Art Festival brought artworks to the shoreline to propose new uses for the beach, staging one project at Akiya Gallery Fuyoso (Minamishitaura-cho, Kamimiyata 3323) that evokes a submerged living space. A Miura-based local art collective transformed the gallery into an otherworldly scene resembling a six-tatami room lying on the seafloor, part of the festival’s effort to create immersive, site-specific works that engage with the coastal environment.
