The MrMax Shonan Fujisawa Shopping Center celebrated its 20th anniversary in June 2026, and Terrace Mall will mark its 15th anniversary in November 2026, highlighting the district’s retail longevity. Together, the two large commercial facilities have shaped Tsujido’s urban landscape, attracting customers from within and beyond Shonan and supporting local businesses and services. Their continued presence and complementary roles are cited as key factors in consolidating a top-class economic area in the Shonan region.
