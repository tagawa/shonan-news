The Shindo Gakuen educational foundation operates two facilities, "Yōho-renkei-gata Nintei Misato/Kakinomi Kodomoen" and "Ōgami Misato Yōchien" as certified childcare centers that emphasize nature experiences and diverse play. Their mission is to foster life-affirming growth through outdoor activities that let children explore, play, and develop emotionally. The centers integrate natural settings and varied play opportunities into daily routines to encourage curiosity, physical activity, and social development.
