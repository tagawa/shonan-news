The Oiso High School alumni association general meeting is scheduled for Saturday, September 19, at the school, beginning at 3:00 p.m. The event coincides with the school's culture festival, "Uminari-sai," which will be open to the public, allowing alumni to watch current students before attending the reunion. Attendees are invited to join the alumni meeting after observing festival activities.
