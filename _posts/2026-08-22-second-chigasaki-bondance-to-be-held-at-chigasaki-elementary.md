The second Chigasaki Bondance will take place on August 22 (Saturday) on the Chigasaki Elementary School playground, running from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM. The event is organized by the Minami Station Front Shopping Street Association and the Minami Hondori Shopping Street Association, with support from Chigasaki City and the Chigasaki Restaurant Association. The program includes vendors and food stalls, and community members are expected to enjoy traditional bon dance performances and festival activities.
