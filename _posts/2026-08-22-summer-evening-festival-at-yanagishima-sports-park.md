A Noryo-sai (summer evening festival) will take place on August 28 (Friday) at the Yanagishima Sports Park community plaza, hosted by the park and the Shonan District Machidikara Council. The event runs from 4:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. and will feature food stalls, a kitchen car, and other festival booths for visitors to enjoy.
