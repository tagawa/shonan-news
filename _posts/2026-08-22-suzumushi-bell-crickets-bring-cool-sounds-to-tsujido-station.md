On the Shonan Ginza shopping street by the south exit of Tsujido Station, the local merchants' association led by chairman 程嶋秀幸 is presenting the sound of鈴虫 (suzumushi, bell crickets) under the name "The 響 of 鈴虫 at くまの森モール" to provide nostalgia and relaxation for station users. The initiative broadcasts or displays the crickets' delicate chirping to create a soothing, cool atmosphere for commuters and shoppers.
