Tetsu Yamamoto, a prefectural assembly member representing Samukawa Town, will hold "Prefectural Government Report Meeting 2026" on August 30 (Sunday) at the fourth floor assembly hall of Samukawa Shrine (Miyayama 3835). Registration opens at 10:30 a.m. and the meeting begins at 11:00 a.m.; an admission fee is required.
