The Yokosuka Corporate Association will host a concert by the U.S. Navy 7th Fleet Band, based at U.S. Navy Yokosuka, on September 18. As part of its public service activities, the association will invite 100 local residents to attend free of charge. The program will feature marches and other pieces performed by the band.
