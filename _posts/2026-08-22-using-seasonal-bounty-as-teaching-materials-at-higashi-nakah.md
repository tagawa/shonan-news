Higashi Nakahara Kindergarten (licensed) centers its childcare guidance on giving children diverse experiences and fostering friendships. Principal Kiyokazu Ozawa says the school uses seasonal bounty as a starting point for play and as teaching materials, integrating natural elements into activities to stimulate curiosity and learning. The approach emphasizes hands-on interaction and social development through materials drawn from the changing seasons.
