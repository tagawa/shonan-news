As summer events like the sea, fireworks, and Obon end, Usui Home's Takeyama branch reports a regular uptick in inquiries from people who want to sell real estate and complete the sale before year-end. The branch advises that initiating the process now gives sellers time for valuation, marketing, and paperwork needed to close by December. Staff emphasize seasonal demand makes late summer an important planning window for local homeowners considering a sale.
