The nationwide chain Wakaba, with over 250 stores, is running a strengthened purchase campaign at its Chigasaki South Exit location during August. During the campaign the store guarantees 400 yen cash for each unused 50-unit telephone card. The promotion also accepts alcohol for cash, and the campaign runs through August 31.
