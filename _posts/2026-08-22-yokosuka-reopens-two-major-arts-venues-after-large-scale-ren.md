Yokosuka's two representative cultural facilities, Yokosuka Arts Theater (Honcho 3-27) and Yokosuka Museum of Art (Kamoi 4-1), completed extensive renovation work and have reopened. The renewed venues are marking the reopening with commemorative programs and events to welcome visitors back. The projects aimed to update and preserve the city’s main arts and cultural hubs for continued public use.
