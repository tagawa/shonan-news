Brothers who won Japan's top Tsugaru shamisen honors bring the instrument's percussive, soul-stirring style to Fujisawa, where their playing is described as striking rather than plucking. Based at Keio University Shonan Fujisawa, they pursue the instrument's driving power and precise teamwork, using bold bachi strokes that transmit heat and resonate between players. Their performances emphasize timing, shared breath, and the physicality that distinguishes Tsugaru shamisen from other musical forms.
