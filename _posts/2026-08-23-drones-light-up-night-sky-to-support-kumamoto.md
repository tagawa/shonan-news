A drone show took place on the 18th at Katase Nishihama and Kugenuma beaches, organized by the Enoshima Beach Cooperative Association. The 500 drones created fantastical art themed on the legends of Enoshima, including images of a dragon god and celestial maidens. The performance transformed the summer night sky into a luminous spectacle intended to cheer on Kumamoto. Local organizers presented the event as both a cultural showcase and a gesture of support.
