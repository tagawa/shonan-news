The skills test, part of a prefecture-run program to nurture young technicians, covers production of signs and other advertising materials. Murayama Mizuki passed the advertising art finishing exam, earning the skill certificate while still a high school student. Her achievement is the first time a high school student in the prefecture has obtained this certification. The school and local officials praised her accomplishment as an example of practical vocational training leading to industry-recognized qualifications.
