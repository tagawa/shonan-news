Hearing Store Odakyu Fujisawa has seen good reception for its 'invisible' hearing aids, which sit deep in the ear canal and are hard to see. The shop noted increased interest during Obon when family conversations often reveal hearing difficulties, and it offers free trial loans to customers who say they saw the promotion in Town News. Staff encourage people concerned about hearing—prompted by comments like "Isn't the TV loud?" or "You asked me to repeat that"—to try the devices and consult for hearing tests.
