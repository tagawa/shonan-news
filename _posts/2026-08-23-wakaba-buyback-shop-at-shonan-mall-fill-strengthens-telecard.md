Nationwide chain "Wakaba" operates over 250 buyback stores; the Shonan Mall Fill branch is strengthening purchases of telecards and alcohol during August. During the campaign, unused 50-unit telecards are guaranteed at 400 yen each, and the store is offering increased cash value for alcoholic beverages. The campaign runs until August 31.
