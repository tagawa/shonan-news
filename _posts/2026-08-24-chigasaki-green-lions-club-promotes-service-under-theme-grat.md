The club has adopted the themes “報恩謝徳” and “元気に明るく楽しく” (energetic, bright, and fun) for the current term. Members say they want to highlight not only service projects but also the bonds between members, and to increase awareness of the appeal of Lions activities. The club is enthusiastic about expanding community service while strengthening member connections.
