Kawamawari Shrine, located at 二宮町山西２１２２, held the event titled "大人も子どもも川勾神社ｄｅつながる ご縁日" on August 23 from 3:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. Admission was free and the event proceeded in light rain. The festival featured food such as takoyaki and various hands-on experience booths for visitors.
