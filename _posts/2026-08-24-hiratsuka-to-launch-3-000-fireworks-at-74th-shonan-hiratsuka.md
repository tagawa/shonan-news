The Shonan summer tradition, the 74th Shonan Hiratsuka Fireworks Festival, is scheduled for August 28 (Friday) at Hiratsuka Tama Saburo Fishing Port (Shinko). About 3,000 fireworks will be launched between 7:00 p.m. and 8:00 p.m., with the highlight being the star mine (starmine) display. Gates open at 5:00 p.m.; additional event details such as viewing areas and access were not provided in the brief notice.
