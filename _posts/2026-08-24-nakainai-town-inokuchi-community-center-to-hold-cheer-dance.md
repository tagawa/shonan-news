Inokuchi Community Center (井ノ口１８４３の１) is recruiting participants for a cheer dance trial class on September 6 (Sunday). Two sessions are offered: for kindergarten seniors through 3rd graders from 10:00 with capacity 10, and for 4th graders through 3rd-year middle school students (time and further details in original notice). Interested participants should contact the community center to apply.
