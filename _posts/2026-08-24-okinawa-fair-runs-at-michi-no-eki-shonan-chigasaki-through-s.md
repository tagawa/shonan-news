The Okinawa Fair is taking place at Michi-no-Eki Shonan Chigasaki in Yanagishima, Chigasaki City, through September 13. The event features popular Okinawan foods such as pork tamago, taco rice, and sweet thick omelets served with Spam and rice. Local vendors are offering specialty products and dishes that showcase Okinawan flavors to visitors of the roadside station.
