Shonan Early Childhood School, led by representative Shigenori Tanaka, hosted its customary summer evening festival at Shiomidai Ground in Chigasaki City on July 18. The event drew many attendees, including current pupils and parents as well as alumni, and featured activities that produced bright smiles and warm memories. Organizers said they hoped the festival would become a wonderful shared memory for everyone who came.
