The "Job Fair 2026" for students training to work at private kindergartens and those seeking re-employment will take place on August 29 at Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce Minapark, 5th floor, Fujisawa 607-1. Participating kindergartens from within Fujisawa City will explain job openings and working conditions, and on-the-spot interviews may be available. The event is aimed at recruiting staff for Fujisawa's private kindergarten sector and providing information to prospective applicants.
