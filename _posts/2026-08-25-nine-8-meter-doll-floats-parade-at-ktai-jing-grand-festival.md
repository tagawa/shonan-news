Kōtai Jingū, also known affectionately as Karasumori Shrine and founded in 832, held its customary Reitaisai on the 17th. The shrine’s famous doll floats, some about 8 meters tall, appeared in a lineup of nine, entertaining visitors who came to watch the procession. The event is a long-standing local tradition that draws residents and festivalgoers to the historic shrine.
