An event called "黙々読書会" invited people to enjoy reading with drinks and snacks on August 23 at the paid rest area "三浦の間" on Miura Beach. The gathering was organized by a person aiming to open a bookstore in Miura City and focused on quietly immersing attendees in the world of books against the backdrop of the sea. Participants appreciated the combination of the beach atmosphere and uninterrupted reading time.
