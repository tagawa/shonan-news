The July 28 Kumamoto earthquake, which registered a maximum seismic intensity of 7 and caused severe damage, is still leaving many victims and the full extent of harm unknown. In Zushi City and Hayama Town, local merchants, civic groups, and volunteer organizations have launched fundraising and collection drives, saying the disaster feels personal rather than distant. Events include street collections and donation boxes at shops, with participants urging continued public support and contributions to aid recovery. Local officials and group leaders have called for sustained assistance as relief needs and damage assessments continue.
