Yasuhiro Shibata, head of the surf therapy group Umigame Club (NPO The SCC Surf Therapy Japan) based in Yuigahama, received the Aloha Award as the first Japanese winner. His organization uses the sea and waves to support children with disabilities and their families, providing physical and mental care through surfing activities. The award recognizes his efforts to promote healing, inclusion, and community connection through ocean-based therapy.
