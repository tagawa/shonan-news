A lecture featuring Nobuo Arimoto, former director of the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan's Hawaii observatory (Subaru Telescope), will take place on September 12 at the Hasegawa Bookstore Nespa Chigasaki branch gallery on the sixth floor. The event is scheduled to begin at 2:00 p.m. and will be followed by a question-and-answer session. The announcement invites local residents and astronomy enthusiasts to attend for an opportunity to hear about space and the Subaru Telescope from a leading astronomer.
