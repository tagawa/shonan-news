Usui Home's Shonandai branch is advising owners of unneeded family homes who worry about annual fixed property taxes and upkeep costs such as garden maintenance. The company recommends selling or otherwise converting such properties into cash before they become so-called '負動産' or liability properties. Their outreach addresses the broader problem of vacant houses and encourages practical solutions to reduce financial and maintenance burdens on owners.
