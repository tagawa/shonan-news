The council, led by Mutsumi Honda of Zushi, has started work to create connections among residents and local organizations so people recognize each other's faces before emergencies. Its goal is to raise disaster awareness and strengthen community self-help through regular interaction and communication. The initiative emphasizes building trust in normal times to enable quicker, coordinated responses when disasters occur.
