The Chigasaki City Calligraphy Association is hosting a calligraphy training session titled "How to Make Calligraphic Works" on September 13 (Sunday) in the A and B conference rooms on the 5th floor of the Chigasaki City Hall branch office at Chigasaki 1-1-1. The workshop will run from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. Further details about instructors and registration were not provided in the notice.
