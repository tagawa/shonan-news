The 15th Samukawa Orange Festa will take place on Saturday, September 12, in the sub-arena of Shinko Sports Samukawa Arena (Samukawa General Gymnasium). The event runs from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., with free admission. Further program details and activities were not specified in the announcement.
