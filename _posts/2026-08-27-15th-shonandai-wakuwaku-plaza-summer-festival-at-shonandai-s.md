The 15th Shonandai Wakuwaku Plaza Summer Festival is scheduled for Sunday the 30th at the underground plaza of Shonandai Station. The event will feature hands-on activities such as balloons and traditional games, a food court, and a flea market. Stage performances, including brass band and other acts, are planned for the program.
