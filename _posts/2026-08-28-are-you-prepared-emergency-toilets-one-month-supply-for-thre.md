A reporter who was visiting home and affected by the Noto Peninsula earthquake recalled that lack of toilet preparedness was a hidden problem, as household toilets became unusable even after water service was restored due to broken indoor pipes. Repair requests piled up, delaying fixes, so emergency toilet supplies became vital. The article highlights a packaged set intended to cover roughly one month for three people to encourage households to prepare for future disasters.
