Chigasaki FM (Eboraji / 89.2 MHz) will air a disaster prevention special titled "SAVE CHIGASAKI — Considering Disaster Prevention from the Sea" on August 30 (Sunday) from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. The program centers on tsunami preparedness and will feature hosts and segments (details not provided in the brief). Technology company Alvac is cooperating with the broadcast to support the program.
