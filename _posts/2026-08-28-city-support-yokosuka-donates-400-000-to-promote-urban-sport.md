The general incorporated foundation City Support Yokosuka donated ¥400,000 on August 12 to Yokosuka City's "Urban Sports Athlete Support Project," which began in July. The program supports athletes registered with the city, including parkour practitioners, by subsidizing competition and training expenses and helping with event organization. The donation will help expand the program's activities and promote urban sports locally.
