Early on the 19th, the Katase District Living Environment Council organized a beach cleanup at Katase-Nishihama and Kugenuma swimming beaches following a drone show. Members wearing green and yellow vests collected trash along the shore, focusing on removing debris left after the event. The activity aimed to restore the beaches' appearance and maintain a pleasant environment for residents and visitors.
