Periodontal disease is said to affect 80% of adults, and Fujisawa Dental Perio & Implant Center addresses this with team-based care led by specialists. The clinic includes dentists certified by the Japanese Society of Periodontology and other experts working together to provide comprehensive treatment and patient-centered support. Their approach emphasizes specialized diagnosis, coordinated therapy, and ongoing maintenance to manage periodontal disease and help preserve oral health.
