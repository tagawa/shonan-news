Kanagawa Prefecture has issued the September–November edition of its booklet ‘‘Event Calendar,’’ which compiles information on cultural and arts events across the prefecture. The purpose is to attract people with the appeal of culture and the arts and to help create local vibrancy. The booklet provides schedules and details to help residents and visitors find performances, exhibitions, and other arts activities.
