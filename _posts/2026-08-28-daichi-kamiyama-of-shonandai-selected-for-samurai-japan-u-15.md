Daichi Kamiyama, a third-year student at Shonandai Junior High School, was named to Japan's national U-15 baseball team for the WBSC U-15 Baseball World Cup 2026. The tournament opens September 25 in Mexico, and Kamiyama will wear uniform number 51, the same number worn by a player he admires. His selection brings local pride in Shonandai as he prepares to join the Samurai Japan roster.
