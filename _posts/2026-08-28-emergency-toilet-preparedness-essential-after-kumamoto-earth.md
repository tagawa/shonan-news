The July 28 Kumamoto earthquake created dire toilet conditions in affected communities. During the 2011 Great East Japan Earthquake, only 34% of municipalities received temporary toilets within three days, showing slow distribution. The report stresses that having emergency toilets and plans is essential because temporary facilities often arrive late or are insufficient after major quakes.
