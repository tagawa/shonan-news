The cafeteria on the first floor of Hayama Town Office reopened on August 17 under the name Hayama FOREST Cafe. The cafe is operated by a local organization that uses the town's forest resources and runs various related projects. The renewed space aims to circulate Hayama’s local attractions and resources, offering food and activities that reflect the town’s forestry and community initiatives.
