The event, titled "歴史はこう読め！〜お笑い芸人による歴史の現代風解釈法〜," will explain ways to enjoy and learn history through comedy. It is organized by the Miura Chamber of Commerce and Industry Youth Division and takes place at Kainan Shrine in Misaki, Miura City on September 2 (Wednesday). The program aims to present modern interpretations of historical topics using humor to engage attendees.
