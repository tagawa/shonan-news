Four JTB branches serving the Shonan and Seisho areas will jointly host an event introducing Japan-origin cruises on September 12. The session will take place at Cocoterasu Shonan, a five-minute walk from Tsujido Station, with the first session starting at 10:00 a.m. Attendees can learn about the appeal of cruising as an accessible way to enjoy an extraordinary travel experience.
