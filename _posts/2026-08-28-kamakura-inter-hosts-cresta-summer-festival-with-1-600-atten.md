Kamakura International FC (Kamakura Inter) held its Cresta Summer Festival at the Gold Crest Stadium Kamakura on August 11, continuing the event from last year. Despite disruptions from a typhoon, the club welcomed 1,600 visitors who enjoyed food, games, and activities, creating a day of smiles. The festival aimed to strengthen community ties and showcase the club’s youth programs.
