Kanagawa Shinkin Bank (President Takahashi Takeshi) and Zushi City signed a disaster-preparedness agreement on August 20. Under the pact, the bank’s Zushi branch will supply stored goods when large-scale disasters trigger the opening of evacuation shelters. The agreement clarifies the bank’s role in supporting local disaster response by providing prepositioned supplies to the city for use at evacuation centers.
