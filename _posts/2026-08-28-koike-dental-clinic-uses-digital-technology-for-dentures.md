Koike Dental Clinic director Koike Gunpei described how digital scanning, CAD production, and 3D printing allow dentures to be created in a shorter time than with traditional methods while improving fit and comfort. The clinic can store digital data to reproduce replacement dentures quickly, reducing patient visits and downtime. Staff say the approach preserves aesthetics and function and offers a more convenient option for people who have lost teeth.
