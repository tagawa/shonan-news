The Kenmin Kyosai Cinema Hall yose titled “Miura Performance” will take place on October 17 (Saturday) at Urari’s Miura Civic Hall in Misaki, Miura City. The event is part of the Miura Chamber of Commerce and Industry’s 70th anniversary commemorative projects. Further program details and participation information were announced by the organizers.
