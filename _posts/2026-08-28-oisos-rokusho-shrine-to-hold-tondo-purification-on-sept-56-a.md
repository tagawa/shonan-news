Rokusho Shrine (935 Kofuhongo, Oiso Town), a historic power spot in the prefecture, will stage the Kushidama festival on Sunday, September 6. The shrine will hold a tondo-style ceremonial burning on September 5 and 6 and is accepting items such as stuffed animals for the ritual. Worshippers can bring offerings for purification during those two days; further details about times or any fees were not provided in the item.
