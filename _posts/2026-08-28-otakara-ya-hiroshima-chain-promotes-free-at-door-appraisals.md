The stores named are おたからや平塚中原店, おたからやマックスバリュ平塚河内店, おたからや西友平塚店, and おたからや大磯駅前店, and they are strengthening their “出張買取” (home visit buyback) service. Staff will appraise items at customers’ doorsteps and offer free home pickup, and the campaign is being promoted through television commercials. The notice emphasizes that customers can feel secure because Otakara-ya is a nationwide chain.
