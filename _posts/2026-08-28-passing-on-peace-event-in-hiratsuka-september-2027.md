The 21st “Passing on Peace” executive committee is presenting an exhibition titled “We will not let the present become the eve of war!!” from September 20 (Sunday) through September 27 (Sunday) at Hiratsuka City Central Community Center, address Oiwake 1‑20. The event runs daily from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and aims to promote peace awareness and remembrance through displays and programming. Further details about exhibits or special programs were not provided in the announcement.
