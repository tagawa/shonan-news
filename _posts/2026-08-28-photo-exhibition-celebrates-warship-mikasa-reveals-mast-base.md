The exhibition commemorates Mikasa’s July designation as a “Ship Heritage” and runs daily from 9:00 to 13:00 through August 30. Photographs highlight the steel battleship’s majestic appearance and for the first time publicly show the base of its mast. The show aims to convey the vessel’s historical importance and craftsmanship to visitors in Yokosuka.
