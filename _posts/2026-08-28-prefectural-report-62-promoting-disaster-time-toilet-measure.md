Yamamoto Satoshi highlights increasing earthquake and localized heavy rain risks and urges improved toilet measures during disasters. The prefecture supports municipalities by helping stockpile supplies and conduct joint training with related organizations. These efforts aim to bolster local disaster-response capacity and ensure hygienic, functional toilet provision for affected residents.
