In Sato Communications No. 42, Kanagawa Prefectural Assembly member Satoshi Yoshikawa expressed condolences to victims of the Kumamoto earthquake and wished for a swift easing of survivors' anxiety. Ahead of September's Disaster Prevention Month, he encouraged people to make disaster preparedness part of their everyday lives and reviewed local efforts to strengthen readiness. The bulletin emphasizes personal responsibility and community cooperation in reducing disaster risks.
