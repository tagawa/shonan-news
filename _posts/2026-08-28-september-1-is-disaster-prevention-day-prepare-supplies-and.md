Recent natural disasters, including the magnitude-7 earthquake in Kumamoto Prefecture on July 28 and extensive storm damage in Chiba Prefecture, have underscored the need for preparedness. With September 1 designated as Disaster Prevention Day, officials are reminding residents to keep emergency supplies stocked and to cultivate mental resilience through regular drills and planning. The emphasis is on daily readiness for both physical needs and psychological coping during disasters.
