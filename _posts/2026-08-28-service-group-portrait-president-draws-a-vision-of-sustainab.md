The Hayama Rotary Club named its 46th president, a specialist who founded a company in 2018 that supports municipal tourism and conducts sustainability audits for hotels. He has built a career advising local governments and businesses on sustainable practices and tourism development. As club president, he plans to promote initiatives that balance community vitality with environmental stewardship.
