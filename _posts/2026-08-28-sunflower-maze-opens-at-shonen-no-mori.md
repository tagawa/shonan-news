The sunflower maze at Uchimochi's Shonen no Mori will be open starting Saturday the 29th, and a viewing event will be held. The event is part of the lifetime learning program run by Goshomi Civic Center and is co-sponsored by the district revitalization executive committee and others. The maze is located in the park's northern area.
