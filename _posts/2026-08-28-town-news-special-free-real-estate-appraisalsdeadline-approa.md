Town News is running a free property appraisal program in partnership with local real estate firms. This round includes participation by 大神不動産 and 神奈中不動産センター, with Town News acting as the local intermediary. Residents can submit inquiry forms and check sheets through the service, and the application deadline is September 4 (Friday).
