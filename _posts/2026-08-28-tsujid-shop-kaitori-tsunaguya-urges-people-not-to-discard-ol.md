Many people leave old banknotes and coins tucked away in drawers and chests, but some old coins and commemorative currencies can be worth more than face value. Kaitori Tsunaguya, located two minutes on foot from Tsujidō Station, is asking people not to throw such money away and is conducting careful appraisals regardless of whether the items are usable. The shop examines old currency thoroughly to determine collector value and encourages residents to bring in forgotten money for evaluation.
