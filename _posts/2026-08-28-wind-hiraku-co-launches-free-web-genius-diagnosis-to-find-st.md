Wind Hiraku Co., a firm that supports business creation in Zushi City, has published a free Web diagnostic called the "Genius Diagnosis" to verbalize value-creating abilities people often do not notice themselves. The test aims to identify individuals' strengths and how they generate unique value, helping clients and businesses better understand and leverage those talents. The company positions the tool as a way to discover overlooked competitive advantages for personal or organizational development.
