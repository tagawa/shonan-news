Masahiro Kosaka took office as president of the Ninomiya Lions Club for the first time and selected the theme "行動こそ王道〜未来を創る熱き奉仕の力〜" (Action Is the True Way: The Passionate Power of Service That Creates the Future). He emphasized returning to the basics of service, working diligently and steadily, and moving forward one step at a time. The theme expresses his commitment to earnest, grassroots volunteer work as the club's guiding principle during his term.
