An event was held on August 8 at the Potofu Hiratsuka daycare in Amanuma, Hiratsuka, where current attendees and local children together painted pictures on the daycare's wall. More than 30 children participated, each creating their own designs and contributing to a colorful mural intended to brighten the facility and engage the community.
