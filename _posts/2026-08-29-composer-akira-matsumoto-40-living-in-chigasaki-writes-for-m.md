Akira Matsumoto, who uses the pen name RUSH EYE and lives in Chigasaki, is a 40-year-old lyricist and composer pursuing music that reflects his own identity. He wrote INI's song 'Rendezvous,' which began streaming in July and was featured on Fuji Television network's information program 'SUNDAY Break.' Matsumoto has provided compositions to many well-known artists and continues to focus on creating work that feels true to himself.
