Shonan Orange Day, a dementia prevention event, will take place on September 5 (Saturday) at Branch Chigasaki 2, 2nd-floor Machi-Spo Chigasaki Sunnoichi, located at 3-1 Hamamihira, Chigasaki City. The event runs from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. and offers programs aimed at preventing cognitive decline. Organizers expect local residents to attend and provide information and activities related to dementia prevention.
