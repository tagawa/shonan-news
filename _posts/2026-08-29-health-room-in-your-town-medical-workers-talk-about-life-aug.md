The event, organized as a special summer program called “Children...” by the Health Room in Your Town series, invites residents to casually discuss health, daily life, and childrearing with doctors and other medical professionals. It aims to provide an accessible space for conversations that do not require a hospital visit. The program offers an opportunity for families to ask questions and receive practical guidance in a community setting.
