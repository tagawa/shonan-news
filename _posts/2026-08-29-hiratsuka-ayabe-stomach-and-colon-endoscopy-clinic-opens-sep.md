The new clinic, Hiratsuka Ayabe Stomach and Colon Endoscopy Clinic, opened on September 1 near JR Hiratsuka Station West Exit. The director is Miho Ayabe, a certified specialist of the Japanese Society of Gastroenterology and the Japanese Gastroenterological Endoscopy Society, who will provide care and perform endoscopic examinations. The clinic emphasizes women doctors providing consultations and endoscopy services. It is located a three-minute walk from the station's west exit.
