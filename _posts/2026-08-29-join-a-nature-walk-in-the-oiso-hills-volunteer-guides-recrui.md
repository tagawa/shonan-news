The NPO Oiso Guide Association is inviting participants to its guided nature-observation event titled “Komayama–Shonandaira Nature Observation Guide: Let’s Go to the Forests of the Oiso Hills! Learn and Talk!” scheduled for September 27, a Sunday. The walk will begin at Oiso Station, and volunteer guides will lead attendees through local woodland areas around Komayama and Shonandaira to observe and discuss natural features. The association is seeking people to join the outing and learn about the local ecology.
