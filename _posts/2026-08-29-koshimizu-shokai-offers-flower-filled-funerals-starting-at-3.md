Koshimizu Shokai, a local company with about 90 years of history and close community ties, promotes "warm-hearted funerals" that honor the wishes of bereaved families. The company’s popular low-cost packages include a floral altar course starting at ¥330,000 (tax included), and they emphasize sending the deceased off surrounded by flowers. Located a five-minute walk from Chigasaki Station north exit, Koshimizu Shokai offers a range of set plans to meet different needs and budgets.
