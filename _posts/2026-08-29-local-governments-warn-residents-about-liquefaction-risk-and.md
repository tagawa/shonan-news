Liquefaction, a major concern alongside tsunamis and building collapse during large earthquakes, occurred far from the epicenter in the 2011 Great East Japan Earthquake, affecting reclaimed land in Tokyo Bay and parts of Chiba and Saitama. Municipalities across affected prefectures are actively publishing maps, guidelines, and preparedness advice so residents know where liquefaction is likely, what damage it can cause, and how to respond. Officials emphasize checking local hazard maps, securing furniture, and planning evacuation routes, especially in low-lying reclaimed areas. The campaigns aim to reduce damage and speed recovery by improving public awareness before the next large quake.
