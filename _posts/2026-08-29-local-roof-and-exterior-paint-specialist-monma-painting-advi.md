Monma Painting, a community-based company specializing in roof and exterior painting, warns that typhoons and heavy rain can cause leaks from roofs and walls and blow off roofing materials. The company says aging makes roofs hard to see but prone to cracks, displacement, and lifting, and recommends homeowners perform pre-typhoon inspections to spot and address such problems. Preparing in advance can reduce damage from strong winds and downpours and help determine whether repairs or repainting are needed.
