Renovation of the Ogosumi Sports Ground baseball field in Okazaki, Hiratsuka, is being carried out mainly by members of the over-40 division of adult baseball team SHONAN03. The effort has involved volunteer labor and coordination by the team's senior players, who have been spearheading the cleanup and improvement activities since July 12. Local authorities and community members are cooperating with the team to restore the facility for continued recreational and amateur baseball use.
