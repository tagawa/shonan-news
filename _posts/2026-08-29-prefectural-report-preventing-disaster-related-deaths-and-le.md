Migiwa Hirano, a Kanagawa Prefectural Assembly member, expressed sympathy for those affected by the recent Kumamoto earthquake and heavy rain damage in Chiba, hoping for a swift return to normal life. She emphasized the need to prevent disaster-related deaths and called for inclusive support measures so that no one is left behind during recovery. Hirano highlighted the importance of continued attention to vulnerable people and coordinated local government responses to restore safety and daily routines as soon as possible.
