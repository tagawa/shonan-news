Samukawa Town is urging residents to use its official LINE account to participate in "smartphone disaster drills," allowing people to train "anytime, anywhere." The initiative aims to raise preparedness for potential large quakes such as a possible Tokyo metropolitan earthquake and the Nankai Trough earthquake. The town emphasizes easy access and civic participation to strengthen community readiness through repeated, convenient practice on mobile devices.
