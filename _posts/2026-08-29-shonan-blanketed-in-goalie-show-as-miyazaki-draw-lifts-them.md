Tegevajaro Miyazaki, in their first J2 season after winning their group in the Hundred-Year Vision League, hosted Shonan Bellmare in their first-ever meeting. The match in Miyazaki finished 0-0, a result that left Shonan with three consecutive clean sheets and propelled them to second place. Shonan goalkeeper Sanada produced standout saves that kept the score level and were noted as the defining factor of the game.
