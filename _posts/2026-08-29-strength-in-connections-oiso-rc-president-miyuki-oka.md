Miyuki Oka is serving her first term as president of the Oiso Rotary Club as the club marks its 60th anniversary. To commemorate the milestone, the club plans a charity concert in February and a formal ceremony in April next year. The anniversary theme is “Strength in connections, toward the future,” reflecting a focus on community ties and forward-looking projects.
