Makoto Itō, in his fourth year as a member, began his first year as president of Nakai LC and said he wants to involve local residents and try new initiatives. He sponsored the Bon Odori event held at Sato Machi Café on August 15 and set this term’s theme as “With friends, together with the community.” Itō aims to broaden the circle of participation in local activities through events and collaboration.
