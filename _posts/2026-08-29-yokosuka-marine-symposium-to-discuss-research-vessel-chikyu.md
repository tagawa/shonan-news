The citizen group Yokosuka Civic Conference (YCC) will hold the Yokosuka Marine Symposium on September 26 (Saturday) with the theme "Research Vessel Chikyu and Japan's Marine Mineral Resources." Experts will explain Japan’s domestic situation regarding marine minerals, drawing on the Chikyu’s deep-sea exploration capabilities. The event aims to inform the public about current research, resource potential, and related issues.
