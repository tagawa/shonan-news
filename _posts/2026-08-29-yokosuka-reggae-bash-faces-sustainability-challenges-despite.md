The two-day YOKOSUKA REGGAE BASH on September 12 and 13 at Natsushima Ground gathers leading domestic reggae musicians for a free, community-supported live festival. Organizers and volunteers emphasize the event as "planting seeds for the future," but they face ongoing challenges in sustaining the free format and covering costs. The festival relies on donations, sponsorships, and local cooperation to continue, and organizers are seeking broader support to keep the event free and viable long-term.
