The public interest incorporated association Amayadori, led by Misao Kikuchi, provides life support for young people aged about 15 to 29 who are seeking help. Starting in October this year, Amayadori launched a mobile outreach program that uses a kitchen truck to visit schools across the prefecture to meet young people who fall between gaps in existing systems. The visits aim to make contact more accessible by going to schools directly and offering support services there.
