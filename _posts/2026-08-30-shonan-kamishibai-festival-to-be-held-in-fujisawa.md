The event, titled "第1回湘南紙芝居まつり," is planned as a community festival to introduce the traditional art of kamishibai to a wide local audience. It will take place at ちびっこ保育園藤沢園 in Fujisawa's Asahicho and is designed so people of all ages, from children to adults, can participate. Performances by creators and talk sessions are among the activities intended to showcase the appeal of kamishibai.
