Atsushi Ishii, 67, a resident of Chogo, was chosen this spring to head the council that coordinates 13 district neighborhood association federations in Fujisawa City. He also serves as chair of the Chogo district neighborhood association federation and says he will focus on fostering community bonds and creating a safe, enjoyable future for residents. The piece highlights his belief in building local ties through activities centered on enjoyment to support secure daily life.
