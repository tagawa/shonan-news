Music, food, and performances will come together at the Autumn Fest in Miura Beach on September 27, 2026, from 10:00 to 19:00, with sky lanterns launched at 18:40. About 25 food stalls will offer dishes using ingredients from Miura. Sky lanterns are sold by advance reservation at 1,500 yen each. For inquiries contact 046-881-5111 (Public Interest Incorporated Association Miura Junior Chamber).
