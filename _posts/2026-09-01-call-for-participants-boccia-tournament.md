The 15th Hiratsuka Boccia Tournament is scheduled for October 18 (Sunday) at Hiratsuka Sun Life Arena from 9:50 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Entry is open to city residents, workers, and students; 24 pairs will be selected by lottery, with each pair consisting of three players. Applicants should follow the event's application procedures and deadlines as set by the organizers.
