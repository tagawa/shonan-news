The Enoshima Lantern 2026 event is currently being held on Enoshima in Fujisawa City, with 1,000 lanterns installed across the island. The seasonal display, described as a summer tradition, is scheduled to continue through Silver Week. The event was reported by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
