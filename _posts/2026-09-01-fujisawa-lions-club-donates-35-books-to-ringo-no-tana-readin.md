The Fujisawa Lions Club, led by President 上條和明, donated 35 books worth 120,000 yen to the Fujisawa City General Public Library on the 10th. The donated books are mainly large-print children's titles intended to support young readers with disabilities and will be added to the library's 'Ringo no Tana' program. The contribution aims to expand accessible reading options for children who need larger type or other reading supports.
