The facility, located in Kamakura City’s Fuketa area, aims to let people continue living in Kamakura in their own way through the end of life, whatever their condition. It combines nursing small-scale multifunctional in-home care and a group home model to provide continuous support. The operator has begun recruiting residents ahead of the planned opening this autumn and emphasizes local ties and person-centered care.
