Kokubu Metropolitan, a major food and beverage wholesaler based in Tokyo, partnered with Kamakura Ethical Lab, Sanbon Coffee of Yokohama, and Kamakura City to launch a wrapped train called the Kamakura Ethical Train. The themed train started service on September 1 and will operate on the Enoshima Electric Railway (Enoden). The collaboration promotes ethical consumption and highlights participating local businesses and initiatives.
