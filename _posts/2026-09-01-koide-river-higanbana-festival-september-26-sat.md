Red higanbana spider lilies blooming along the river mark the arrival of autumn and form the festival’s scenic backdrop. Local specialties and regional vegetables will be sold, and a free stamp rally around the area offers prizes to participants. The event runs from 9:00 to 15:00 between Daikokubashi and Oidebashi along the Koide River in Samukawa Town’s Okura; inquiries are handled by Goshomi Civic Center at 0466-48-1002.
