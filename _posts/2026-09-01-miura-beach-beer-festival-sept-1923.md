Independent breweries from the Miura Peninsula and along the Keikyu line will serve specialty beers paired with seasonal Miura ingredients and local snacks. The event runs daily from 11:00 to 19:00, with closing at 17:00 on the final day, and will be canceled in severe weather. Admission is free; food and drink are paid separately. For inquiries call 045-225-9696, Keikyu Information Center.
