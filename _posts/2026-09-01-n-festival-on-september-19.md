The festival runs from 12:00 to 21:00 at Nagai Fishing Port and will be canceled in case of rain. Attendees can enjoy dance and band performances while sampling local seafood and farm produce from vendor stalls and kitchen cars. This year the event is holding its first-ever Fisheries Employment Consultation Meeting. Admission is free, there is no parking, and inquiries can be directed to Nagai Suisan at ☎046（858）1010.
