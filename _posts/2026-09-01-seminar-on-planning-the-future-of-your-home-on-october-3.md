Meiji Civic Center is offering a lifelong learning event titled "Home End-of-Life Seminar: Would You Like to Consider the Future of Your Childhood Home or Your Own Home?" on October 3 (Saturday) in the first discussion room on the center's second floor. The program will feature a lecturer who will discuss issues related to what to do with family homes and one’s own house as circumstances change. The center is recruiting participants for the seminar, which is part of its community lifelong learning activities.
