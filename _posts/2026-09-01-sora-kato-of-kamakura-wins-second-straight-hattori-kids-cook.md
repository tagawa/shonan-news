The 31st Hattori Kids Food Education Cooking Contest, organized by Hattori Nutrition College in Tokyo, was held on July 31 at the college. Sora Kato, a sixth grader at Kamakura Municipal Kosaka Elementary School, won the competition for the second year in a row. The contest promotes food education among children and is run by the vocational college specializing in nutrition.
