YOKOSUKA REGGAE BASH, a leading outdoor reggae music festival in Japan themed "Planting Seeds for the Future," will take place September 12 (Saturday) and 13 (Sunday) at the Natsushima Ground special venue. The festival runs from 12:00 to 20:00 each day with gates opening at 10:00, and features a lineup of 44 artists. Admission is free.
