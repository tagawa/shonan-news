Singer-songwriter Tetsurou, active mainly in the Shonan area, will hold "Tetsurou SeptemberLIVE" on September 12 at Art Gallery Los Pinos & Cafe in Fujisawa City (Minami-Fujisawa 7). The event is presented as an intimate acoustic performance at the gallery-cafe venue. Further details such as start time, ticketing, or program contents were not provided in the brief notice.
