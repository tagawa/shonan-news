The Autumn Bon Odori event, presenting traditional local performing arts from Yokosuka's West District, will take place on September 12 (Saturday) at Nagai Uminote Park "Soleil no Oka". It is organized by the Okusu, Takeyama, and Nagai combined neighborhood association together with the Nagai Community Management Council. In addition to dances and other community performances, the program will include a 300-drone light show and other attractions aimed at drawing residents and visitors. The event highlights efforts to keep local arts alive while offering new spectacles for the community.
