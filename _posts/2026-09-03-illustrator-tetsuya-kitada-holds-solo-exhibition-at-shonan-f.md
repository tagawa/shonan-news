Illustrator Tetsuya Kitada, who is from Chigasaki City, is holding a solo exhibition titled "Illustrator Tetsuya Kitada Exhibition" at Shonan Fujisawa Tokushukai Hospital in Fujisawa's Tsujido Kandai 1. The show was inspired by his experience with illness and is currently open to the public at the hospital venue.
