Many medals awarded before and after the war are lying unused in homes. The buyback shop "Uru Navi" on the second floor of Ito-Yokado Shonandai store is running a readers-only purchase campaign until September 30, 2026. The campaign invites people who have medals to bring them in for appraisal and purchase. Details and any restrictions are available from the store.
