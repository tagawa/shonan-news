In a J2 League fourth-round match between Shonan Bellmare and Omiya Ardija, the game ended 2-2, leaving Omiya unable to secure a victory that might have decided the league lead. The decisive sequence came in the 83rd minute when Shonan's Artur Silva delivered a feed that led to a scramble involving Omiya defenders and a pass from a recovered clearance. The match was described as a painful split for both sides, with neither able to claim the full three points in this top-of-the-table contest.
