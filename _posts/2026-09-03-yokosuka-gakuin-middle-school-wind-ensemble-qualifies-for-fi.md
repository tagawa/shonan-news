Yokosuka Gakuin Middle School (Yokosuka City, Inaokachō) wind ensemble won the 'Gold Prize and Representative' in the middle school B division at the 75th Kanagawa Prefecture Wind Ensemble Competition held this summer. Their prize earned them a spot at the East Kanto Tournament, to be held on September 19 in Chiba Prefecture, marking the program's first-ever advancement to that tournament. The qualification is described as the culmination of 32 years of effort by the school's ensemble.
