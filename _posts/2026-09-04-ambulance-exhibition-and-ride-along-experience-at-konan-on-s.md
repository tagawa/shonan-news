Ofuna Fire Station will hold an "Emergency Fair" at Konan Kamakura Ofuna store on September 9, aligned with Emergency Medical Care Week running September 6 to 12. The event will include hands-on CPR training using mannequins, an ambulance vehicle exhibition, and ambulance ride-along experiences. The fair aims to raise public awareness of emergency medical care and teach basic lifesaving skills.
