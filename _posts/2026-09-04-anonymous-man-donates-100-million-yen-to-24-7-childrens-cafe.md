The donor used the pen name "宮城昭" and included a message of support for the cafeteria, which operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. The donation will support the continued operation and activities of the children’s cafeteria YOKOSUKA NANAGAYA in Ikegami, Yokosuka City. Staff described the gift as unexpected and expressed gratitude, noting it will help sustain services for local children and families.
