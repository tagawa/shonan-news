Kaneko, a judicial scrivener known for clear explanations and broad expertise, cautions that some heirs leave inherited real estate in a deceased parent's name because they do not understand the new mandatory inheritance registration rules. He explains that failing to register can cause problems such as issues with property rights, difficulty selling or using the land, and potential penalties under the new law. Kaneko outlines practical points heirs should check—such as confirming ownership, completing necessary documentation, and consulting a professional—to avoid future disputes and sanctions.
