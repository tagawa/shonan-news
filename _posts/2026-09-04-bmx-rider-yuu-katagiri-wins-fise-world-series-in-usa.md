Yuu Katagiri, a 21-year-old BMX flatland rider from Samukawa, won the FISE World Series held in Birmingham, Alabama, from August 7 to 9. The victory came at the international competition in the United States, where Katagiri competed against top riders from around the world. His win highlights the rising profile of Japanese BMX athletes on the global stage.
