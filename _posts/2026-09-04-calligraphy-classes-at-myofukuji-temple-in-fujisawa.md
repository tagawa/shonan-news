Myofukuji Temple in Uchimodori, Fujisawa, opens for calligraphy lessons on the first and third Friday and Saturday each month. Participants may enter anytime between 2:00 and 17:00 on class days, with the temple closing at 18:00. The classes are taught by an active Buddhist priest skilled in calligraphy who emphasizes disciplined practice and clear instruction. Interested people can attend during the posted hours.
