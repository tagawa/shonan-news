Chigasaki Beer will offer a limited-time all-you-can-drink buffet of freshly brewed craft beer at its brewery restaurant during Silver Week. The event runs from September 19 (Saturday) through September 27 (Sunday), with time slots available each day. Reservations are being accepted for the special buffet.
