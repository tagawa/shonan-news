The My Number Card, usable for identity verification and as a health insurance card, remains a priority for Chigasaki City. To reduce the burden on applicants, the city will once again operate outreach application support this fiscal year, but applicants must attend in person and proxy applications are not accepted. The service assists with initial applications and reapplications, and is intended to help residents who need hands-on support completing their forms.
