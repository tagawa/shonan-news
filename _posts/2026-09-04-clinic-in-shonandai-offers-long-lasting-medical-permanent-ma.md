Shonan-Fujisawa Cardiovascular Clinic in Shonandai is providing medical art makeup, promoted for saving time in busy mornings, allowing people to enjoy sports and hot springs without worry, and easing anxiety from disease-related hair loss. The clinic markets the service as a durable cosmetic solution tailored to patients seeking low-maintenance appearance improvements. It is presented as both a convenience for active lifestyles and a therapeutic option for those affected by medical hair loss.
