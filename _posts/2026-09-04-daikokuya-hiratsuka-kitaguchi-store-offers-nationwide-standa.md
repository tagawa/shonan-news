In the competitive buyback market, accurate market information is crucial. Daikokuya Hiratsuka Kitaguchi Store leverages its nationwide network to provide appraisal power that attracts many repeat customers. The shop accepts items including Rolex and Omega watches, Chanel bags, and alcoholic beverages, and offers trial appraisals so customers can check current market prices before selling. Their nationwide-standard assessment helps ensure fair, market-aligned offers.
