The event titled BASEBALL LIVE & PLAY BayStars in Yokosuka will be held on September 13 (Sunday) at Yokosuka Arena in Fuirutocho. Former professional pitcher Miura Daisuke will give live commentary as attendees watch a large-screen broadcast, and the event aims to let visitors experience the fun of baseball. Additional activities and details about participation were announced by the organizers to promote local interest in the sport.
