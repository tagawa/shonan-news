The All-Japan Beach Volleyball U15 Championship took place at Kugenuma Beach on August 22 and 23, where local team Fujisawa Club earned third place. The team, made up of second-year students, had earlier secured a sixth consecutive prefectural title in July. Their national podium finish showcased the skill and potential of the younger squad at a high-level tournament.
