Pukapuka Fireworks Night was held three times at Port House Terugasaki in Oiso Town on July 25, August 7, and August 23, offering viewers the chance to watch large fireworks bursts from a pool. The event aimed to create summer memories in Oiso by combining fireworks viewing with the experience of floating on water, giving attendees a unique seaside summer evening.
