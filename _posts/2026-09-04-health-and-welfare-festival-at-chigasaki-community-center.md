The Chigasaki District Community Center is hosting the "コミセン健康福祉フェスタ" on Saturday, September 12. The event runs from 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., with registration closing at 3:30 p.m., and admission is free. Activities will focus on health and welfare themes for the local community.
