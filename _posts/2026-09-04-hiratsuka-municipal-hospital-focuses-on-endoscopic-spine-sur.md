Hiratsuka Municipal Hospital is promoting endoscopic surgery for lumbar spinal stenosis, a condition in which age-related spinal changes narrow the spinal canal and cause symptoms. The endoscopic technique uses a very small skin incision and is designed to minimize stress on the lower back compared with conventional open surgery. The hospital says this approach can shorten recovery and may be suitable for older patients or those concerned about surgical impact on daily life.
