Kamakura City Family Support Center seeks "support members" who will provide paid volunteer assistance to childcare households. Training sessions run October 7 (Wednesday) from 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and October 8 (Thursday) from 9:00 a.m., covering practical caregiving skills and program rules. Participants who complete the course can register as paid volunteers to help local families through the Family Support Center. Contact information and application details are available from the center.
