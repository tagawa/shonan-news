Kanagawa Densetsu (Chigasaki-shi Hagi-en) ran a hands-on electrical work experience at Tsurumine Nishi Community Center on August 25. Participants were students from Ichijuku Elementary School and Hamanogo Elementary School, from grades 1 through 6. Company staff taught basic electrical tasks and safety, giving children practical exposure to careers in electrical work.
