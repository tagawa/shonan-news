The Kanagawa Kenmin Hall's year-end staple, the 'Fantastic Gala Concert,' is scheduled for December 27 (Sunday) at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall's large hall, an eight-minute walk from JR Chigasaki Station north exit. The program is presented as a festive year-end performance by Kanagawa Kenmin Hall, continuing its tradition of seasonal concerts for local audiences. Further details such as ticketing and performers were not specified in the brief announcement.
