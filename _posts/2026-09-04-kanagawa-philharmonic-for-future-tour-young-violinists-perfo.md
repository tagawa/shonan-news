The Kanagawa Philharmonic Orchestra is holding its For Future touring concert series. The performance will take place on October 3 (Saturday) at Chigasaki Civic Culture Hall, an eight-minute walk from JR Chigasaki Station North Exit, with a 2:00 p.m. start. The program features young violinists playing the famous "Fate" motif from Beethoven’s works. Tickets and further program details were announced by the orchestra.
