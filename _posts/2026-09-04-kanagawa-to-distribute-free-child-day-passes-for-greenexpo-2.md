GREEN×EXPO 2027, the International Horticultural Exposition, is scheduled for March 19–September 26, 2027, at the former Kamiseya Communication Facility in Yokohama. Kanagawa Prefecture will distribute "こども招待１日券" (child invitation one-day tickets) so children can experience flowers and greenery from around the world. The announcement was made in the context of prefectural assembly questions advocating local engagement and promotion of a new era for Kanagawa starting from Fujisawa. The measure aims to encourage family attendance and broaden public access to the expo.
