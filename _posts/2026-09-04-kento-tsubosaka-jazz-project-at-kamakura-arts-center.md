The program, part of the Kanagawa Prefectural Hall regional project C×JAZZ titled "Kento Tsubosaka × George Gershwin," will take place December 5 (Saturday) at the Kamakura Arts Center small hall, about a 10-minute walk from JR Ofuna Station. The performance is scheduled to start at 3:00 p.m. Additional details about tickets and performers were not provided in the notice.
