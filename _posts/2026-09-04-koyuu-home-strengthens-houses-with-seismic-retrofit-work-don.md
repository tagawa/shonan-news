Koyuu Home emphasizes the importance of improving and maintaining housing seismic performance to protect life and property from earthquakes. While newer homes already meet higher seismic standards introduced around 1981, many older houses still need reinforcement, so the company offers in-home construction to retrofit and strengthen existing structures. The service provides reassurance to homeowners by performing reinforcement work without requiring residents to leave their homes, addressing both safety and convenience.
