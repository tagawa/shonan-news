Kurashi Net Kanagawa is offering consultations for people facing difficulties with aging, empty-nest or inherited houses, including options to sell, rent, or retain properties. The sessions will cover inheritance, legal matters, sales, taxes and other real estate concerns, and aim to guide residents on who to consult and what options are available. Consultations are scheduled for September 12 and 19 and will take place on the third floor of the Kamakura Oji Building.
