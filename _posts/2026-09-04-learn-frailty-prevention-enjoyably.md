The event, titled “フレイル予防セミナー〜人生１００年時代を創造しよう〜,” takes place on September 12 (Saturday) at LUSCA Chigasaki, sixth-floor hall (元町１の１). It runs from 10:30 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. The seminar focuses on enjoyable approaches to preventing frailty; further participation details were provided in the original notice.
