Meiji Jisho Co. is strengthening its property sales in the Shonan area by providing sellers one free choice from seven services designed to enhance a property's appeal. The complimentary options aim to raise final sale prices and speed up transactions. The program is presented as part of the company’s sales support efforts to improve marketing and presentation for listed properties.
