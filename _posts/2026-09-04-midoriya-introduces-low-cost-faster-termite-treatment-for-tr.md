Midoriya Co. has launched the Clean Barrier Method, a termite-control service tailored to traditional Japanese houses whose underfloors are bare earth. The company says the method reduces both cost and construction time compared with conventional measures while addressing hidden termite damage signs such as slightly spongy floors or sighting of winged ants. The service targets homes prone to trapped moisture and offers a focused alternative to more extensive renovation or fumigation.
