Miwako Mashimo, an 11-year-old student at Kamakura Municipal Onari Elementary School, participated in the inaugural Inclusive Runway 2026 held August 26 at the Tokyo National Museum. The event showcased children with disabilities and serious illnesses, and Mashimo appeared as a kids model using a wheelchair. Organizers aimed to promote diversity and visibility for people with different abilities through fashion and performance. Her appearance highlighted inclusion efforts in local and national cultural events.
