A dementia consultation session is scheduled for September 16 (Wednesday) at the Nakai Town Health and Welfare Center, 1st-floor Community Comprehensive Support Center, Hinakubo 104-1. The event runs from 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., participation is free, and family members may also receive consultations.
