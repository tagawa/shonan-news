Ofuna Central Hospital is hosting a public lecture on September 19 (Saturday) in its lecture room beginning at 2:00 p.m., with doors opening at 1:30 p.m. The roughly one-hour session will feature a certified nurse who will explain heart failure prevention in accessible terms. Attendance requires a reservation, and the program is part of a well-received series offering clear, specialist medical information to the public.
