The Chogo Civic Center hosted a neighborhood exchange event called "1coin Noryokai" on the 21st of last month, attracting over 20 local residents. The venue was lit with colorful lights different from its usual appearance, creating a bright, festive atmosphere. Attendees enjoyed music and interaction in the transformed space, which temporarily operated like a live house for the evening. Organizers aimed to foster local connections through the low-cost community gathering.
