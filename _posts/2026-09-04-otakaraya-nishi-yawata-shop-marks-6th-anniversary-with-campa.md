Otakaraya Nishi-Yawata Shop is holding a six-year anniversary campaign that gives customers household items when they sell goods worth ¥5,000 or more. The shop encourages bringing brand-name items and precious metals for appraisal and offers free assessments, making it a good opportunity for first-time visitors. Female staff members are always on site to provide a sense of security for customers.
