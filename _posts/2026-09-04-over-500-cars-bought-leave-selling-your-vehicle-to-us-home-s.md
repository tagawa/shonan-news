Happy Cars Fujisawa, a local car-buying company, says it has purchased more than 500 vehicles and stresses honest customer service and high Google ratings. The shop offers free on-site appraisals and can complete sales at the seller’s home, and it will pay at least 20,000 yen for cars that run even if other companies previously valued them at 0 yen. The store positions itself as a trustworthy, community-focused option for people considering selling their vehicles.
