Many people pack emergency food and water but often forget oral-care items, according to Wada Wataru, director of Fujisawa Wataru Dental Clinic located a seven-minute walk from Fujisawa Station. He warned that disasters can cause water outages and disrupt regular oral hygiene, increasing the risk of aspiration pneumonia, especially among the elderly. Wada recommended keeping toothbrushes and liquid toothpaste in disaster kits and maintaining routine oral care to reduce infection risks during emergencies.
