The Kanto regional Barber and Beauty Championship, aimed at improving skills among barber and beauty students, was held in July in Chiba City, Chiba Prefecture. Ishizaki Nana, 30, who attends Shonan Beauty College in Akashicho, entered the competition in two categories, including eyelash extensions. Her participation advances her toward the national tournament while showcasing the college's technical training.
