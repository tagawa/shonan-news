Tatsuya Onoue was appointed chair following the association's regular general meeting in late July. The group supports police officers who handle dangerous duties and helps maintain public safety, with Onoue pledging to promote a secure environment where businesses can operate with confidence. He will lead efforts to encourage officers and cooperate with the community on crime prevention and local development.
