The Amateur Kickboxing Korakuen Hall National Tournament 2026 took place on August 17 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo. Three members of Taniyama Gym, based in Isehara and including residents of Hiratsuka City, produced strong results, with one fighter winning a championship and two finishing as runners-up. The results highlight Taniyama Gym's growing presence in amateur kickboxing competitions.
