Tipness Fujisawa, located three minutes on foot from Fujisawa Station South Exit, is promoting a membership campaign. The club offers a well-equipped gym, a variety of lessons, an adults-only pool near the station, and a spa. It uses analysis data to support member programs and emphasizes being a place where people can continue exercising in a way that suits them. The campaign aims to attract new members by highlighting these facilities and the club’s supportive environment.
