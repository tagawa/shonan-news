Kanagawa Toyota Motor Company held a health and driving support event for veteran drivers on August 27 at the Toyota Mobility Kanagawa Tsujido store. The event offered ability measurements and guidance to help older people continue to enjoy driving safely. Staff provided simple physical and cognitive checks and advice about vehicle features and driving habits tailored for seniors. The initiative aims to promote safe mobility and peace of mind among elderly drivers in the region.
