Local real estate firm Usui Home Kurihama Branch, which marks its 50th anniversary, reports growing demand for used detached houses and condominiums because of rising new-construction costs. The company has substantially strengthened its services for selling and buying secondhand properties, encouraging owners to see the current market as a good time to sell. The move aims to meet concentrated buyer interest and to support smooth transactions amid the shifting market dynamics.
