Yokosuka City has announced it will change the name of Verny Park in Honcho to Verny-Oguri Park ahead of the 2027 NHK Taiga drama. An amendment to the city urban park ordinance was submitted to the September council meeting, and a ceremony is scheduled for Saturday, November 14.
