Working Day Wakaba operates a day service that practices social participation for its users through park maintenance activities and aims to help people keep meaningful roles as they age. To respond to increasing user numbers, it is recruiting care staff for day-service work and a seikatsu soudanin (life counselor). The announcement emphasizes supporting users’ continued social engagement and daily living through hands-on activities in the community. Contact and application details are provided by the service for interested candidates.
