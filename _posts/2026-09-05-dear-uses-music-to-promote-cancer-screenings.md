The unit dear, composed of Tomohiro Yoshizawa, who works at a financial institution in Fujisawa City, and Itsuki Takimoto, a personal trainer living in Katase, is accelerating awareness of cancer screenings through musical activities. They aim to reach men who are less likely to attend screenings by combining live performances and outreach that emphasize the importance of early detection. Their efforts grew out of university connections and community ties in Fujisawa City.
