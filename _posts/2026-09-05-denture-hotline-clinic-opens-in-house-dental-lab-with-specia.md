Facing a super-aged society where people may live to 100, the clinic says dentures are increasingly important because implants can be unsuitable for many patients aged 80 and over due to bone, cost, and health issues. To improve outcomes and extend healthy life expectancy, the clinic has established an on-site dental laboratory and employs a denture specialist technician to make and adjust higher-quality dentures. The initiative aims to provide faster, more personalized denture care and to promote oral function as a key to healthier aging.
