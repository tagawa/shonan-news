After earthquakes and typhoons, immediate safety and restoring living conditions take priority, but subsequent recovery uncovers various legal issues; judicial scrivener Sakane Office provides continuous support from emergency consultations at shelters to long-term reconstruction. The office helps with procedures such as property title changes, insurance claims, loan negotiations, beneficiary designation, and procedures for deceased victims, aiming to reduce legal uncertainty that hinders rebuilding. Sakane staff coordinate with local governments, lawyers, and non-profit groups to offer on-site consultations and document preparation tailored to each victim’s circumstances. Their role emphasizes practical, empathetic assistance to help residents move from short-term recovery to full restoration of their lives and homes.
