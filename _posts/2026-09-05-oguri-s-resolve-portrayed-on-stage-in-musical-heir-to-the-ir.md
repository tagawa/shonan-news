The musical "Heir to the Iron Will" portrays the life and ideals of Oguri Uenosuke Tadanori, a Bakumatsu-era retainer who oversaw construction of the Yokosuka Iron Works and helped drive Japan's modernization. Performances began on October 10 and continue on subsequent dates. The production explores Oguri's leadership and commitment during a turbulent period of industrial and political change.
