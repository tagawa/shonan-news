The Hiratsuka Chamber of Commerce and Industry Youth Division (YEG) is accepting participants for the 第36回ＹＥＧベルマーレカップ２０２６小学生駅伝競走大会, scheduled for November 28 (Saturday) at Lemon Gas Stadium Hiratsuka in Hiratsuka City Comprehensive Park. The event is one of the largest elementary school ekiden races in the Kanto region and is organized to promote youth sports and community engagement. Organizers are recruiting teams and individuals to take part in the race; entry details and deadlines are available from the YEG office.
