Mitsubishi UFJ Trust and Banking Fujisawa branch conducted a training exercise simulating a bank robbery on the 19th of last month at its Minami-Fujisawa branch. The drill was held with cooperation from Fujisawa Police Station under Chief Jiro Asada. The branch routinely carries out such exercises to prepare staff for robbery scenarios.
