A seminar titled "If a seismic intensity 7 earthquake occurs in Fujisawa: Considering regional infrastructure and disaster preparedness with Professor Kato of the University of Tokyo" is scheduled for October 1 (Thursday) at Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce Minapark (Fujisawa 607). The event will discuss impacts on local infrastructure and steps residents and authorities can take to prepare, with insights from Professor Kato of the University of Tokyo. The notice is aimed at raising awareness and strengthening community readiness for a major earthquake.
