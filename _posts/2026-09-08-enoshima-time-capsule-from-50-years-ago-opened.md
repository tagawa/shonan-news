A time capsule buried to mark the 50th anniversary of the Enoshima Tourist Souvenir Association was opened at Enoshima Shrine on the 1st. Items retrieved included family trees, photographs and personal mementos that brought back half-century-old memories for those involved. Organizers and locals gathered for the unveiling, marking a reunion with the past and renewed interest in community history. The event highlighted the sentimental value of preserving local memories across generations.
