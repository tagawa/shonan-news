Kugenuma Junior High School's guitar ensemble took first place, receiving the Special Gold Award at the All-Japan Student Guitar Festival on August 18. The report notes individual achievements by young guitarists, including third-year Rokukai Junior High student Reo Kojima, who also participated in student guitar events earlier in August. The story highlights the strong performance and growing presence of youth guitarists in regional and national competitions.
