The Meiji University Mandolin Club will perform on October 24 (Saturday) at Yokosuka Bayside Pocket in Honmachi. The concert marks the 65th edition in Yokosuka and follows a two-year suspension due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The program celebrates autumn and resumes the club's long-standing local tradition.
