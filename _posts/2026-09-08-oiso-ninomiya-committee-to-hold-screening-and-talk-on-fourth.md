A meeting to consider "第四の被曝" ("Fourth Exposure") will take place on September 26 (Saturday) in the large conference room of Oiso Town Library, located at Oiso 992. The event opens at 1:00 p.m., with the program starting at 1:30 p.m. A materials fee will be charged.
