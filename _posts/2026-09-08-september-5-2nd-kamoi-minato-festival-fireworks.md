The 2nd Kamoi Minato Festival fireworks took place on September 5 at Kamoi Port beach (near 3-6 Kamoi, Yokosuka). The event was organized mainly by local youth working with community members; fireworks began at 18:30, with food stalls and kitchen cars operating from 15:00 to 19:30. The festival proceeded in light rain but would have been canceled in severe weather. For inquiries, attendees were directed to the event office at 080-5413-7878.
