The exhibition, titled "Oshima Taiki and Maya Yamagata: Oshima Sibling Exhibition," features works by brothers and sisters Oshima Taiki and Maya Yamagata. It opens September 10 and continues through September 13 in Exhibition Room C at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall. The show highlights the artistic bond between the siblings, aged 88 and 77, and presents their paintings together for local residents and visitors.
