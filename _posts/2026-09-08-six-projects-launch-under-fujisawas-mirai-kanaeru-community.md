Fujisawa City announced this year’s projects for the Mirai Kanaeru Activity Support Program, which helps diverse groups including civic organizations collaborate to solve community problems. The city presented six initiatives, with a focus on supporting the launch phase of activities. The program aims to foster sustained partnerships among stakeholders to address regional challenges in Fujisawa.
