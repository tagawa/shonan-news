Yui Wada, 39, a resident of Yokosuka City, is exhibiting her first solo show titled "yui and hachi Illustration Exhibition" at the used bookstore Furuhon Isado Totorado in Zushi, about a six-minute walk from JR Zushi Station. The exhibition displays numerous gentle, heartwarming illustrations featuring dogs, cats, monkeys and children. The show is Wada's debut solo exhibition and is being held at the small local shop in Zushi City.
