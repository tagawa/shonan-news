Councilor Fujii Tatsunori of Nagaoka City and Councilor Sumiyasu Koichi of Ojiya City visited a community center on August 20 to observe the 'Odekake Wagon' operated by the Shonan Area Machidikara Council. The visit was part of their study of the service's operations and community support functions. They examined how the mobile wagon serves local residents and considered practices that might be applied in their own municipalities.
