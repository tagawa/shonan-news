Kamakura Kadan has opened submissions for the "21st Kamakura Kadan Sakigake Minamoto no Sanetomo Commemorative Tanka Gathering," to be held November 26 (Thursday) at Kirara Kamakura. Entrants should submit one new tanka written neatly in block-style characters on B5, 200-character manuscript paper, and send it by mail; further submission details and any additional requirements were provided in the notice.
