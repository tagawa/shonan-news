The 66th National Junior High School Swimming Championships were held August 17–19 in Hiroshima Prefecture. Hayashi Swim School of Chigasaki, which included Takahama Junior High third-year student 八幡晟, took first place for the first time in the boys' 400-meter medley relay. The team also competed in freestyle events. The victory marked Hayashi Swim School's first national relay title at the junior high level.
