The Kamakura UNESCO Association's International Exchange Committee has organized a 'Ring the Bell of Peace' event for September 26, starting at 1:00 p.m., to encourage reflection on peace. The event commemorates the 75th anniversary of the Japan National Commission for UNESCO and invites the public to join discussions and ceremonies focused on peace.
