Kanagawa Eisei Gakuen Vocational School, which trains acupuncturists, massage therapists and nurses, held its annual Yoko Festival on October 3 at its Yokosuka campus in Sanocho 2-34 from 10:00 to 16:00. Students showcased what they had learned through exhibits and activities, and the event included popular massage trial sessions that drew positive feedback. The festival, held every year, highlights the school’s educational outcomes and provides the public with opportunities to experience healthcare techniques.
