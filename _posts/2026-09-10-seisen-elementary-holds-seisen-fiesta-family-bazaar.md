Seisen Elementary School (鎌倉市雪ノ下３の11の45) will host the Seisen Fiesta, Family Bazaar on September 19 (Saturday) from 9:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Admission is 500 yen. The event will feature performances including flamenco and choral singing along with the family bazaar activities.
