The Fujisawa Corporate Association Women’s Division, chaired by 小林寿美恵, held the opening ceremony for the 13th Fujibo Ladies Academy on October 5 at the Fujisawa Corporate Hall. The event included a talk by the Fujisawa Tax Office director and was scheduled to begin in the afternoon. The program is organized by the Fujisawa Corporate Association Women’s Division to provide educational sessions for its members.
