Takaki Saito, executive at Chogo Seipan in Takakura, Fujisawa, was appointed a regional ambassador for Atotsugi Koshien, a program run by the Small and Medium Enterprise Agency of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry. On September 8 he paid a courtesy visit to Fujisawa Mayor Tsuneo Suzuki to report his appointment and discuss support for local successors and future collaboration. The meeting covered ways to assist regional business succession and coordinate efforts going forward.
