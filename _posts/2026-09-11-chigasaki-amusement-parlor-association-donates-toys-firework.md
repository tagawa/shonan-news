The Chigasaki Amusement Parlor Association, whose members include pachinko parlors in Chigasaki City and Samukawa Town, visited three children's residential facilities in Chigasaki on August 6. Led by association head 小巻嵩典, they donated items such as toys, fireworks, sweets, drinks, and bicycles. The visit was part of the association's local outreach to support children in care, providing both recreational goods and practical items.
