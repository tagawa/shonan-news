At a regular press briefing on August 28, Chigasaki City said it will present 22 proposals, including a supplementary budget, and six reports to the city council for the September regular session held from August 31 to September 30. The supplementary budget strengthens measures against rising prices by implementing premium shopping vouchers for residents. The city framed the move as part of broader efforts to ease the burden of inflation on households.
