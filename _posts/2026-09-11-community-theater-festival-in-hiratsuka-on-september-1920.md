The 25th 湘南ひらつか市民演劇フェスティバル will be held September 19 (Saturday) and September 20 (Sunday) at 平塚市中央公民館大ホール. Doors open at 1:00 p.m. and performances begin at 1:30 p.m. Admission is 500 yen.
