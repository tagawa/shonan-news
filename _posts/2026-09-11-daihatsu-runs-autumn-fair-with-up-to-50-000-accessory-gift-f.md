Daihatsu Hiratsuka Sakuragaoka and Ninomiya stores are running the "ダイハツ秋の選べすぎ！フェア" from September 5 (Saturday) to September 13 (Sunday). Visitors to the event and customers who sign for a new car during the period can receive gifts, including up to ¥50,000 in accessories when buying new models such as the Move and Tanto. The promotion also includes additional offers running through September 30.
