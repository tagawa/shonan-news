As chairman of the temple support association, Onoda Yasunari announces that Ofuna Kannonji will hold the Dream Kannon and Mitama Festival on September 26 (Saturday). The event will include equinoctial memorial services plus community activities such as monkey performances, stage programs, and food stalls to promote regional exchange. Onoda invites local residents to attend and participate in the festival.
