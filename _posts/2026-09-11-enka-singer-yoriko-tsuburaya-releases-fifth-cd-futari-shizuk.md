Yoriko Tsuburaya, an enka and kayō singer who lives in Zushi-shi Shinjuku, has released her fifth CD titled '二人静' (Futari Shizuka). Known for a bright, vigorous voice that shows no signs of decline even as she nears her 77th birthday, she enjoys singing simply for the love of it. In addition to performing, she teaches singing classes at the Ikego Senior Center.
