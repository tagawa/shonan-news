A free seminar titled "End-of-Life and Inheritance" will take place on September 30 (Wednesday) at Hirashin Hiratsuka Cultural and Arts Hall. Attendance is limited to the first 10 people who apply. The event begins with Part 1 at 13:00, covering end-of-life planning and inheritance measures. Further program details and any additional sessions were not provided in the brief notice.
