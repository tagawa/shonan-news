As part of celebrations for the 10th anniversary of the city mascot “ふじキュン♡,” Fujisawa City has renewed its local marriage registration form for the first time in six years. The new design, tied to the mascot’s anniversary commemorative projects, began distribution from the 1st of this month. The redesign aims to promote the mascot and local ties through a popular municipal document.
