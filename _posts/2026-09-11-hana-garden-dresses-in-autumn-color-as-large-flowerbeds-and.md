At 花菜ガーデン in Hiratsuka City’s Teradanawa area, the panoramic main flowerbed is in bloom with celosia, zinnia, and gomphrena, while lycoris (red spider lilies) are also at peak viewing. The garden uses the slope of Mikazukiyama immediately inside the entrance to create seasonal displays that highlight autumn colors. Visitors can enjoy dense, colorful plantings across the site as part of the garden’s autumn presentation.
