Hatsuki Ohashi, who lives in Hiratsuka and trains at the Miyamae Boxing Gym Shonan Ryuken, won the Japanese light-flyweight title by TKO against reigning champion Daiki Kameyama on August 16. The victory made Ohashi the new national champion in the division. The bout took place under the scheduled title match rules, and the stoppage was recorded as a technical knockout.
