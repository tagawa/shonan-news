Jorenji Temple in Hiratsuka City opened an individual-type burial hall called “Annon” near the city center that does not require a donation, and strong demand led to the first phase selling out quickly. The temple says the facility eases future worries by offering secure, private placement of remains and also handles full grave closures known as “haka-jimai.” A limited number of spaces remain in the second phase, so the temple recommends prospective visitors view the facility early. The site is noted for its quiet location despite being close to central Hiratsuka.
