The popular Shonan-area food and spot influencers known as Todomaguro, husband and wife Wataru Maekawa and Keiko Maekawa, have been appointed PR ambassadors for the “Kyun to Suru Fujisawa Shopping Street.” The couple will promote the shopping street’s restaurants and attractions through their channels. Local organizers expect the pair’s popularity to raise the profile of the shopping street and attract more visitors.
