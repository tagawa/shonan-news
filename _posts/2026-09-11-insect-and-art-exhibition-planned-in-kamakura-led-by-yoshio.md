An interactive exhibition titled "ムシヨロズ展-養老孟司と巡る鎌倉虫散歩" is being organized in Kamakura with anatomist and insect enthusiast 養老孟司 (Yoshio Yoro), a Kamakura resident, taking a central role. The show will use sites such as 建長寺 (Kencho-ji) in 山ノ内 and is scheduled to open next June. The organizers have launched a crowdfunding campaign to support the project.
