The event, aimed at addressing problems such as what to do with an old family home after aging or nuclear family changes—whether to sell, rent, or retain it—will offer consultations on inheritance, legal matters, sales, and taxes. Organizers note people often do not know whom to ask about different kinds of real estate concerns, so specialists will be available to give tailored advice.
