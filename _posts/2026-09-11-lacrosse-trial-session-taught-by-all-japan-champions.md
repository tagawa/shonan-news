A lacrosse experience event is scheduled for September 19 (Saturday) at Shiokaze Sports Park, reflecting growing interest after the sport was added to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Instruction will be led by members of the team that won the All-Japan Championship in February. The session offers local residents an opportunity to try lacrosse under guidance from top-level players.
