Mayumi Yanagawa, a 37-year-old newcomer and native of Chigasaki, has revealed her intention to run in the Chigasaki City Council election during the unified local elections in spring 2027. She has received official endorsement from the Democratic Party for the People. Yanagawa’s background includes studies at Aoyama Gakuin, and she is positioning herself as a fresh candidate in the local race.
