Megane no Araki is running a "hearing aid consultation" event through September 23 to assist people who find conversations hard to hear or who are told their TV is too loud. The event offers professional guidance and support for daily hearing concerns, with personalized consultations about hearing aids and listening issues. Customers can visit Megane no Araki stores during the campaign period for assessments and advice from trained staff.
