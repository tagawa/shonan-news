The Zushi branch of eyewear retailer Megane no Araki is offering a hearing aid consultation event to help people who struggle with conversation or have been told their TV is too loud. The event provides professional guidance, hearing checks, and personalized advice to address daily hearing concerns. It runs until September 23 and is intended to support customers worried about their hearing and improve their daily communication.
