The regional revitalization event “2026 N-Fes” will take place on September 19 (Saturday) at Nagai Fishing Port in the Nagai district on the western side of Yokosuka City. The festival highlights local food and culture from the fishing-town area, and a truck stage is expected to draw strong attention from attendees. The event aims to promote the attractions of Nagai and support community activity.
