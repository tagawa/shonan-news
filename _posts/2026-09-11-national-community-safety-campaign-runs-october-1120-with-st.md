The nationwide campaign called 全国地域安全運動 runs from October 11 to 20 and aims to promote safer communities through coordinated local crime-prevention activities. Authorities and organizers are calling for wider participation in neighborhood efforts, including use of stickers to deter special fraud and other crimes. Many自治会 and町内会 have already introduced sticker programs as a simple, visible deterrent to suspicious approaches and to raise resident awareness. The drive emphasizes community vigilance and cooperation to create a safer living environment.
