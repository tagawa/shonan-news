September is designated Disaster Prevention Month as typhoons and water-related hazards increase, making it an ideal time to review personal readiness and evacuation systems. Tsuyoshi Hoshino, a Liberal Democratic Party member of the House of Representatives, calls for learning from past disasters and strengthening coordination among local governments, police, fire departments, the Self-Defense Forces, and community members. He emphasizes reviewing evacuation plans and raising everyday awareness to better protect lives and livelihoods.
