When severe cavities reach the nerve, patients are often told extraction is the only option, but Oral Care Clinic Fujisawa says it may be possible to save those teeth. The clinic, located a three-minute walk from Fujisawa Station, emphasizes collaboration among specialists to provide meticulous root canal treatment. Their approach aims to extend the functional lifespan of teeth that might otherwise be lost, offering patients an alternative to extraction.
