Otakara-ya Nakahara Store is celebrating its 6th anniversary with a special promotion that raises appraisal amounts by 35% for customers who say they saw the Town News, with some exclusions. The store, known for polite, careful service and positive word-of-mouth, also has a national chain backing and is airing a television commercial. The promotion is time-limited in September and certain items are excluded from the increase.
