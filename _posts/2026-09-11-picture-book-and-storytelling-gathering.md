The event, titled "絵本とおはなし会" (Picture Book and Storytelling Gathering), will take place on September 15 (Tuesday) at the gallery on the sixth floor of Hasegawa Shoten Nespa Chigasaki store near JR Chigasaki Station North Exit. It will begin at 3:00 p.m. and is part of the store's ongoing reading-promotion activities.
