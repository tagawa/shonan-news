Raffle, a women-only health mahjong school located a six-minute walk from Chigasaki Station South Exit, is offering a newly launched beginner course aimed at novices. The program reframes mahjong from its former gambling image into a fun, healthy pastime that has become very popular among women. The school emphasizes a friendly, supportive environment and is currently accepting applications for the class.
