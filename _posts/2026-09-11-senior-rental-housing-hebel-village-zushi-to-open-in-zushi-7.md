Asahi Kasei Homes has completed construction of a senior-targeted rental housing project called Hebel Village Zushi in Zushi 7-chome, aimed at people aged 60 and older. The development promotes smaller, easier living while enhancing freedom and safety for seniors through design and services tailored to their needs. The complex will be part of the company’s broader senior housing program in the Tokyo metropolitan area and is scheduled to begin occupancy this autumn.
