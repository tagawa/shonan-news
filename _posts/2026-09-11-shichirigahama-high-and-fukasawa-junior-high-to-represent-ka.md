The 75th Kanagawa Prefectural Wind Ensemble Contest was recently held, and two ensembles from Kamakura won gold: Kanagawa Prefectural Shichirigahama High School and Kamakura Municipal Fukasawa Junior High School. Both groups earned the prefectural gold prize and will advance as Kanagawa representatives to the East Kanto competition.
