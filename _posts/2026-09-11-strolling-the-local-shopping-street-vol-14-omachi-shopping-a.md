The Omachi Shopping Association has a history exceeding 70 years and lies southeast of Kamakura Station along Wakamiya Oji toward the eastern exit. Hidesaku Maze became the association's chairman about 15 years ago and oversees a district that includes long-established shops with histories exceeding 100 years. The piece highlights the association's deep local character and the continuing presence of these historic businesses in the neighborhood.
