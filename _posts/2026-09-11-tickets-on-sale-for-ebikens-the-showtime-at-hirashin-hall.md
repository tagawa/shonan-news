The sound, light, and dance production "THE SHOWTIME!" featuring EBIKEN in collaboration with AIRFOOTWORKS will be held on October 24 (Saturday) at ひらしん平塚文化芸術ホール. Tickets are currently on sale for the performance. The event combines music, lighting effects, and dance in a stage entertainment format.
