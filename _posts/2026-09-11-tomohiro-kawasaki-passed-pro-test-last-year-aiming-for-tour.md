Tomohiro Kawasaki, 24, who lives in Nakajima, Chigasaki City, passed the professional golf test last year on his third attempt and now works at GDO Chigasaki Golf Links at Hishinuma Beach. He practices on the course every day after work until dusk, aiming to secure playing rights on the tour next year. Kawasaki balances his job and intensive practice while focusing on improving his game to meet the tour's standards.
