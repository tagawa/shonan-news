Shingo Hinata was chosen as chair following the chair and vice-chair elections at the Kamakura City Council special session held on August 20. The Town News Kamakura editorial team interviewed him ahead of the September regular session that began on September 2. In the interview, Hinata discussed his priorities for making the council more open and responsive to residents. He outlined steps he wants to take to improve transparency and communication between the council and the public.
