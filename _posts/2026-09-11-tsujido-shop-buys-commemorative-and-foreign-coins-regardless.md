Many people leave commemorative coins from events like the Olympics and expos unused because they cannot be spent. The shop 買取つなぐ屋, located two minutes on foot from Tsujido Station, is buying Japanese commemorative coins, old coins, and foreign coins regardless of whether they are still usable as currency. The store asks owners not to leave such coins unused and offers to purchase them.
