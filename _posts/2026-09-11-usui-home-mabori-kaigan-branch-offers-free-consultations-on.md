Usui Home's Mabori Kaigan branch is providing free consultations for residents facing common real estate issues such as considering a move from a house to a condominium or dealing with an inherited home left vacant. The service aims to help people understand the value of their homes and plan for the future, including options for sale, transfer, or renovation. Consultations are being offered at no charge to encourage local homeowners to seek advice tailored to their circumstances.
