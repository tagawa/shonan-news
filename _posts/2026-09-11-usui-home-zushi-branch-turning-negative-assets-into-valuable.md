Residents worried about paying annual property taxes and maintenance for unused family homes can consult Usui Home's Zushi branch about options to monetize those properties before they become financial drains. The firm addresses concerns such as ongoing upkeep costs for gardens and other maintenance that cause money to leak out, and promotes solutions for better utilization of inherited houses and the broader empty-house problem. Their approach aims to transform properties that might otherwise be abandoned or costly to maintain into immediate, tangible value for owners.
