September 10 is World Suicide Prevention Day set by the World Health Organization, and the national government designates September 10–16 as Suicide Prevention Week. In Hiratsuka, the city held awareness activities during the week, including a counseling event on the first floor of city hall where staff offered support and information aimed at protecting lives. The local initiative emphasized listening to those in need and connecting people to help and resources.
