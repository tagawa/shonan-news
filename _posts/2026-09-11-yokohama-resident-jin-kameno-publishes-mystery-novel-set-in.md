Jin Kameno, 53, of Yokohama, has published a mystery novel titled 『幽世（かくりよ）までの寄り道 介護施設・灯影庵（ほかげあん）の謎日誌』 with Takarajimasha Bunko on August 5. The book weaves everyday "what if" scenarios into a story set near the Shonan coastline and in Nishikamakura, and centers on a care facility called Hokagean. It was released by 宝島社文庫 and presents a local mystery grounded in familiar neighborhood settings.
