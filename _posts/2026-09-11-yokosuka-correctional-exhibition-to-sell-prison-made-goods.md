The Yokosuka Correctional Exhibition will take place on October 18 at Yokosuka Prison Branch (Nagase 3-12-3). The event aims to explain the importance of social reintegration support and preventing reoffending provided by correctional facilities such as prisons and juvenile detention centers, and will include sales of products made through prison work programs. Organizers expect the exhibition to increase public understanding of correctional activities and offer a chance to purchase goods produced by inmates.
