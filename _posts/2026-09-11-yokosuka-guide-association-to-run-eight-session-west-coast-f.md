The NPO法人よこすかシティガイド協会 (NPO Yokosuka City Guide Association) is organizing an eight-session walking tour series that moves north along the Miura Peninsula west coast to capture scenic views of Mt. Fuji. The tours will focus on themes such as local history and nature and are part of the association’s ongoing efforts to offer guided walks around the peninsula. Participants can expect guided stops chosen for their vantage points of Mt. Fuji and for cultural or natural interest.
