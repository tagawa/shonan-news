Yoriko Tsuburaya, 76, who lives in Shinjuku in Zushi and was featured in the paper's personals column, released her fifth CD, titled '二人静' (Futari Shizuka), in August. Tsuburaya made her professional debut in 2014 and is known for a mature, restrained vocal style that conveys adult romantic feelings. The release was accompanied by a photograph in the original report; further details about the tracks were not provided.
