Yui Wada, 39, a resident of Yokosuka City, is exhibiting gentle, comforting illustrations in her first solo show titled "yui and hachi イラスト展" at Furuhon Isado Totora, a used bookstore about a six-minute walk from JR Zushi Station in Zushi City. The exhibit features Wada's warm, soothing artwork that aims to put viewers at ease and is drawing local attention. Visitors can view the works at the shop during its business hours; the article highlights the intimate setting of the small bookstore as a fitting venue for Wada's debut solo show.
