Chigasaki has revamped the reservation system for public facilities such as community centers and gymnasiums so users can now book and pay online. The change is intended to make access more convenient, but it requires users to update IDs and PINs. Officials note the system may be difficult for people who do not use the internet, and measures to assist those users are being considered.
