The Shonan Kamishibai Festival, an event to enjoy nostalgic yet fresh kamishibai picture-story shows, was held for the first time on the 6th in the second-floor nursery room of Chibikko Hoikuen Fujisawa-en in Asahi Town. The festival was organized by an executive committee of kamishibai performers from across the prefecture and featured eight performers, including Nagano and BUN-chan, who gave live performances. About 100 people attended the event.
