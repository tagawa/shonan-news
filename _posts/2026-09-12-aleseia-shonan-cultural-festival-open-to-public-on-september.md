Aleseia Shonan Junior and Senior High School will host its cultural festival, called the “Peace Festival,” on Saturday, September 12. The event runs from 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., with last admission at 2:30 p.m. Activities include stage performances and interactive workshops, and the festival will be open to visitors during those hours.
