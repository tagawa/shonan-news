The Chigasaki Shonan Rotary Club is marking its 50th anniversary with a hands-on disaster-preparedness event titled "ちがさき防災クエスト〜家族で挑む！震度７のミッション" on November 28 at the Kanagawa Prefectural General Center. The program invites families to experience simulated earthquake scenarios and learn practical safety and response skills through interactive missions. Organizers are currently accepting participants for the event and emphasize family participation to improve community readiness.
