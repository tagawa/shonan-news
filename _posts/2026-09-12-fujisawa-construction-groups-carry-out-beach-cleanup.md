Fujisawa City Construction Industry Association youth group Fujuyukai, led by President Nishiyama Kota, conducted a beach cleanup at Katase Nishihama and Kugenuma bathing beaches on the 20th of last month. The association’s women's division, Fujisawa Build♡Kyun, chaired by Takanashi Akaho, joined the effort. Participants collected litter and worked to keep the coastline clean as part of ongoing local community and environmental activities.
