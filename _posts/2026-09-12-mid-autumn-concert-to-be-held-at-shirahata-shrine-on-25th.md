The annual autumn dedication event "Mid-Autumn Concert" will take place at Shirahata Shrine (Fujisawa 2-4-7) on Friday the 25th. The performance will feature Wagakkai "Nobori," a taiko group active across Kanagawa and Tokyo with more than 15 teams, among other acts. The event is a traditional offering to the shrine and part of local seasonal festivities.
