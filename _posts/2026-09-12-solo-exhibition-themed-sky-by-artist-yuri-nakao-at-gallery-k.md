The exhibition, themed "Sky," is held at art café Gallery Kinomi (茅ヶ崎市芹沢２１２９) from October 29 (Thursday) through November 9 (Monday), with a closure on November 4 (Wednesday). The show features works by Chigasaki resident artist 中尾有里 (Yuri Nakao). Visitors can see the artist's pieces at the gallery during the listed dates.
