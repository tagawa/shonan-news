The inaugural Enoshima Taiko Festival, organized by Shonan Taiko Kenkyukai Enoto and Wadaiko-shu Yuraku, was held on Saturday the 12th at Enoshima Samuel Cocking Garden. Visitors enjoyed taiko drum performances and a bon odori dance, with other related events planned for the day. The festival offered a chance to experience traditional Japanese percussion and community summer festivities on Enoshima Island.
