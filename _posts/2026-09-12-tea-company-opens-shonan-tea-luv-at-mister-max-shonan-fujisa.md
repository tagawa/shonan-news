A tea-leaf company has opened its first shop, a tea café called SHONAN TEA Luv. (湘南ティーラブ), on September 4. The café is located inside Mister Max Shonan Fujisawa Shopping Center at 4 Tsujido Shinmachi, Fujisawa City. The opening was reported by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
