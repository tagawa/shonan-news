Triangle Co., which operates the Sarushima ferry route, is offering a plan to land on the uninhabited island Sarushima to watch the Yokosuka Kaikoku Fireworks Festival, one of the largest-scale events on the Miura Peninsula. The event will take place under clear autumn night skies and includes a special fare called the "Yokosuka Citizen Discount" for local residents. The plan aims to provide an exclusive viewing experience of 10,000 fireworks launched during the festival.
