Yokosuka City introduced a disaster-response kitchen car and toilet car to boost local resilience against increasingly frequent large earthquakes and record rainfall. The vehicles are intended to provide hot meals and sanitary facilities at evacuation sites when normal infrastructure is disrupted. City officials described the units as part of broader efforts to prepare for disasters that exceed past assumptions and to strengthen community support during prolonged emergencies.
