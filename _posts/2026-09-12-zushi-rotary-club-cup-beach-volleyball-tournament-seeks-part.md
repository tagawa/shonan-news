The Zushi Rotary Club is organizing the Zushi Rotary Club Cup Zushi Beach Volleyball Tournament 2026 on October 4 at Zushi Beach East Shore near the Nagisa Bridge public restrooms. The club is recruiting teams and individual participants, with an application deadline of September 15. Event details, participation categories, and entry procedures are being managed by the Rotary Club; interested players should contact the organizers before the deadline.
