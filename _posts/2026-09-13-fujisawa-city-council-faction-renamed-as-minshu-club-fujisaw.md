Fujisawa City Council announced on the 11th that its second faction, previously called "民主クラブ" (Minshu Club), has changed its name and membership. The faction will be called "民主クラブ藤沢" (Minshu Club Fujisawa) and the listed members include Masao Takemura (group leader), Toru Oya, and Ayu Yanagida. The notice reflects an internal reorganization of the faction's composition and title within the council.
