Hiratsuka City is staging a "Kaunas Week" from September 26 (Saturday) to October 2 (Friday) to celebrate the third anniversary of its sister-city partnership with Kaunas, Republic of Lithuania. The event will broadly promote the attractions of Kaunas and is organized by the city; additional details about specific programs, venues, and participants were not provided in the notice.
