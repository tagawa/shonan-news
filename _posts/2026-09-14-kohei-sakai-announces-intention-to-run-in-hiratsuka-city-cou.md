Kohei Sakai, a 46-year-old company employee from Oimatsu-cho, has declared his intention to run as an independent in the Hiratsuka City Council election next spring, which will fill 26 seats when terms expire. He told reporters on September 7 that his platform will focus on education reforms, including moral education. The announcement was made in response to inquiries from the local paper.
