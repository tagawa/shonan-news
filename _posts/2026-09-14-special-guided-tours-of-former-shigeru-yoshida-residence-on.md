As part of the Showa 100th special program, "Former Shigeru Yoshida Residence Special Garden Guide" will take place September 19 (Saturday) and September 20 (Sunday) in the Former Shigeru Yoshida Residence area of Oiso Shiroyama Park. Reception runs from 10:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. each day. The event offers guided visits of the garden and related spaces; visitors should go to the designated reception during those hours to participate.
