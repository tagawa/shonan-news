The popular event, which drew more than 15,000 visitors last year, returns on October 11 (Sunday) to Daiichi Cutter Kiiro Park (Central Park) in Chigasaki. Over 80 food trucks will gather and visitors will vote to choose the No.1 kitchen car. The event aims to offer a wide variety of foods and recreate last year’s large turnout.
