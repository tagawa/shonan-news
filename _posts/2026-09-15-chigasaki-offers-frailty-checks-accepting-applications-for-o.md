Chigasaki City is running a frailty check program that measures muscle mass with body composition scales, assesses oral health, and includes explanations from support staff. The city is now accepting applications for sessions taking place from October onward. The program aims to identify risks of frailty and provide guidance to help residents maintain health and prevent decline.
