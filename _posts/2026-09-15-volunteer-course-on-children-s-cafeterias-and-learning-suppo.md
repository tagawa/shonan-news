Hiratsuka City Council of Social Welfare is organizing a volunteer course on 'children's cafeterias and learning support' on October 26 (Monday) at the Hiratsuka City Welfare Hall, 2nd floor, Third Meeting Room. The afternoon session starts at 13:30 and aims to raise interest in creating places where local children can feel welcome and supported. The program targets residents who want to help children in the community and provides practical guidance for running a children's cafeteria or learning support activities. Participants are asked to register in advance with the Hiratsuka City Council of Social Welfare.
