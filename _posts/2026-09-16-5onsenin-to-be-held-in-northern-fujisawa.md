Participants will walk through nature, food, and culture in Fujisawa City during the event, which focuses on satoyama scenic areas. The program includes nature observation and opportunities to sample local gourmet food in the Endo area. The event is the fifth edition of the ONSEN Gastronomy Walking series in Fujisawa.
