The event, timed with World Alzheimer’s Day, is scheduled for September 21 at Zushi Civic Exchange Center near Keikyu Zushi-Hayama Station. Organizers aim to deepen understanding of dementia and foster a supportive local network through workshops, performances, and interactive activities that combine learning with enjoyment. The festival is billed as a community-building opportunity where residents can learn how to support people with dementia while connecting with local services and volunteers.
