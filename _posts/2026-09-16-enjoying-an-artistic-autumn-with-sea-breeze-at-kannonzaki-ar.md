The event offered visitors an autumn arts experience by the sea, featuring live painting, accessory-making workshops, and other hands-on activities. Held at Yokosuka Museum of Art's 'Yama no Hiroba' (Kamoi 4-1), the festival aimed to engage people of all ages in creative activities and celebrating local art. Organizers presented a relaxed outdoor atmosphere where participants could enjoy both artistic expression and coastal scenery.
