The one-day event 'Big Hayama Market' promotes Hayama's industries and culture and showcases local attractions. It is organized by the Hayama Town Chamber of Commerce and will take place in the precincts of Morito Daimyojin (Morito Shrine) in Horiuchi, Hayama Town. Visitors can enjoy gourmet food stalls, demonstrations of artisan skills, and a lottery-style prize draw among other activities.
