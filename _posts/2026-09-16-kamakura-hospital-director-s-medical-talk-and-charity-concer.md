The charity event titled 'Dr. Mimatsu Kodo's Medical Lecture: Kamakura Medical Ensemble Concert' is organized by local cafeteria Yuki no Shita and takes place on Sunday, September 27 at Catholic Yukinoshita Church in Komachi 2, Kamakura City. The program combines a medical talk by the hospital director with performances by the Kamakura Medical Ensemble to raise funds for community activities. The event aims to bring health information and cultural enjoyment to local residents while supporting the organizer's services.
