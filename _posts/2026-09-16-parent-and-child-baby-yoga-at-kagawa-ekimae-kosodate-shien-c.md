A parent-and-child yoga class aimed at one-year-old children and their parents will take place on September 24 (Thursday) at Kagawa Ekimae Kosodate Shien Center, located at 茅ヶ崎市香川５の３の17. The session runs from 10:30 a.m. to 10:50 a.m. Participants should bring a bath towel and drink; the class is free and limited to the first 10 parent-child pairs to register. For inquiries or to sign up, contact the center directly.
