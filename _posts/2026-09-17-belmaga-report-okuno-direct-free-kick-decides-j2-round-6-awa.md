With the additional time board showing 4 minutes and the clock already past, Shonan continued pressing and won a free kick deep in the opponent's half after 'Tamura Sousei' was fouled. Goalkeeper 'Sanada Kota' joined the attack, and 'Okuno' struck a decisive direct free kick to secure Shonan's 1-0 away victory over Vanraure Hachinohe. The dramatic finish came in stoppage time and handed Shonan all three points.
