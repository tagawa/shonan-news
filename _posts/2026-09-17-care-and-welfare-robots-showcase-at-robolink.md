The event titled '介護・福祉ロボット大集合' will take place on the 25th (Friday) at Robolink, located at Fujisawa 559, running from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. Visitors can experience the latest technologies in the care and welfare field, and admission is free. The program will include demonstrations and explanations of newly introduced devices for caregiving and welfare settings.
