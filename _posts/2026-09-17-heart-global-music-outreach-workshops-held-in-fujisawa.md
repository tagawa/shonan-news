A music workshop program focused on expressive education called 'Heart Global Music Outreach in Fujisawa' ran for three days from September 21 to 23 at the Shonandai Cultural Center Citizen Theater in Fujisawa (Shonandai 1). The program was presented by a U.S. foundation and offered outreach-style lessons in musical expression. The event was reported by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
