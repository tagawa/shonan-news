The national middle school karate championships were held late last month in Shizuoka Prefecture, where members of local dojo 'Shikukai' (head instructor Kobayashi Shimimitsu) achieved strong results. 'Yoneida Kosei', a third-year student at Chogo Junior High, won the boys' individual kata title. The girls' team kata from 'Shikukai' finished in fifth place.
