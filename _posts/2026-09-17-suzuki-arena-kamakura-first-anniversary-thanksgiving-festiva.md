Suzuki Arena Kamakura, operated by (株)スズキ自販神奈川 at address 鎌倉市大船６の９の35 in front of Ofuna Police Station on Kamakura Kaido, marked its one-year renewal opening with a four-day thanksgiving festival. The event took place September 19 (Saturday), 20 (Sunday), 21 (Monday, holiday), and 22 (Tuesday). The festival offered activities and promotions to thank customers following the showroom's renewal reopening.
