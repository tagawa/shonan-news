The event, titled ‘第４回ずしはやま未病フェス’, will take place on September 27 (Sunday) in the precincts of Kameoka Hachimangu in Zushi City. It runs from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and offers participants hands-on experiences in mental and physical care and health promotion. The festival aims to help people address conditions that are 'less than illness' through workshops and activities focused on well-being.
