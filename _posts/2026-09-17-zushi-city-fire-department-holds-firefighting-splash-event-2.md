The Zushi City Fire Department hosted 'Firefighting Splash Event 2026' on August 30 at its headquarters, aimed mainly at elementary and preschool children. Participants learned about fire safety and prevention through hands-on activities, including demonstrations related to safe handling of fireworks and an opportunity to experience being sprayed with water from firefighting equipment. Organizers said the event was intended to make fire services feel more familiar and to reinforce practical prevention knowledge among young residents and their families.
