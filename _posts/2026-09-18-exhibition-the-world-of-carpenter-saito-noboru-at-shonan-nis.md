The Shonan Nishiwaki Gallery, located at Kugenuma Matsugaoka 4-17-15, is hosting a curated exhibition titled 'The World of Carpenter Saito Noboru' that showcases the warmth and grain of wood. The show is the 18th event by carpenter Saito at this gallery and runs until 27 September. Visitors can expect pieces that emphasize wood texture and craftsmanship in a gallery setting. Admission details were not provided in the report.
