The Samukawa Town Tourism Association held the first 'Try It! Tourist Guide Experience Tour' on 1 September around Samukawa Shrine. The event featured the volunteer group 'Samukawa Tourism Guide Club', which explained local history and attractions to participants. Organizers aim to foster interest in community heritage and train more local guides through future similar tours.
