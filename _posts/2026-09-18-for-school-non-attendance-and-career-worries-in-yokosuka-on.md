A consultation session for children and students struggling with school non-attendance, their guardians, and teachers is scheduled for 3 October at Yokosuka City General Welfare Hall, located at Honcho 2-1. Representatives from Yokosuka City, including staff from evening and correspondence schools, will be present to provide advice on schooling and future pathways. The event aims to offer one-on-one consultations and information to help families and educators address educational and career concerns. Attendance details and any required reservations were not specified in the brief notice.
