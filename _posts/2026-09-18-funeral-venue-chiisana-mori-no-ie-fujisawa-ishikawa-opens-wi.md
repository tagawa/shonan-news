A new family funeral venue called '小さな森の家 藤沢石川' at Fujisawa-shi Ishikawa 3-22-8 will hold an open event on 26 September and 27 September from 10:00 to 16:00 with no reservation required. Activities include an all-you-can-pack vegetable giveaway and other attractions, and visitors who join membership on the day can participate in a large raffle with no losing tickets. The facility is positioning itself as a neighborhood option for family-style funerals and is inviting local residents to attend the opening weekend. Admission is free and the event runs across both days during the stated hours.
