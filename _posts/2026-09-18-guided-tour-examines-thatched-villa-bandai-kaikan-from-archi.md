A public guided tour will take place on 17 October at the thatched villa 'Bandai Kaikan', designated as an Important Cultural Property of Yokosuka City and located at Tsukui 2-15-33. The event will explain the building from architectural-history and wooden-construction viewpoints. The tour is intended for visitors interested in the remaining historic wooden architecture in the city. Further details such as participation requirements and exact schedule were not provided in the brief notice.
