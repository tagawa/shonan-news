Kazuhiko Nakanishi, who worked on the national role-playing game series 'Dragon Quest,' gave a lecture on 10 September at Fujisawa Shoko Kaikan Minapark. The talk was organized by the Fujisawa Nishi Rotary Club as an open meeting of the service organization, and Nakanishi spoke about the mindset and practices needed in the game industry under the theme 'creating a world from zero.' He emphasized practical lessons for developers and shared experiences from his work on the long-running franchise.
