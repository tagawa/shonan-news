The official junior team of Kamakura Public Golf Course, 'KPGJ' (Kamakura Public Golf Junior), went undefeated in the Kanagawa Division qualifying matches held between July and August and earned the prefecture's spot. Team members competed at the course in Imaizumi and will represent Kanagawa in the PGA Junior League East Japan tournament. Coaches and organizers praised the players' teamwork and consistency that led to the clean sweep in division play. The team now prepares for the regional competition against other prefectural qualifiers.
