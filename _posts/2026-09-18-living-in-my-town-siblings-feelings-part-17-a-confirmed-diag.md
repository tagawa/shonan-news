This installment of the series focuses on the relationship between Urara and her older brother Ren, and on Ren's reflections as a sibling of a child born with a disability. Author Ai Gobongi, representative director of general incorporated association 'sukasuka-ippo,' writes that receiving a confirmed diagnosis — Angelman syndrome — created 'emotional space' for the family, helping them understand and adjust to their situation. The piece explores personal episodes, the challenges siblings face, and how clarity from a diagnosis can change family perspectives and caregiving approaches.
