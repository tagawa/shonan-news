Hayama resident and artist '八詠', using a professional name, opened a first solo exhibition titled '旅する日本画 光のゆくえ' on the second floor of Hayama Town Library's 'Hayama Machizukuri-kan' at Horiuchi 1874. The show combines traditional Japanese painting and calligraphy, exploring light and travel themes, and runs until 30 September. The venue is free to visit during library hours, inviting the local community to view '八詠''s distinctive fusion of painting and written art.
