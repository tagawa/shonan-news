The 'Koide River Higanbana Festival' is scheduled for 26 September, running from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., and is organized by the Koide River Higanbana Group Council. The festival site stretches from Daikoku Bridge to Kan... where the higanbana are expected to be in bloom. Visitors can see bright red spider lilies along the riverbank and attend the seasonal event arranged by local volunteers.
