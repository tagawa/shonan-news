The second 'Onarimatsuri' will be held at the Onarimachi parking area, a one-minute walk from Kamakura Station West Exit, on 19 September and 20 September. Event hours are from 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Local and nearby food and drink vendors will participate, along with other attractions for visitors. Organizers aim to showcase Kamakura-area eateries and provide a community festival atmosphere.
