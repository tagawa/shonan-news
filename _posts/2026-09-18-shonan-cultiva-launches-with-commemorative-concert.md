A new cultural exchange platform called 'Shonan Cultiva,' led by representative Fukayama Tomoya, has started in the northwestern part of Fujisawa City. The group held an inaugural commemorative concert to introduce its goal of connecting diverse organizations and activities across the area. 'Shonan Cultiva' aims to build ongoing ties among local cultural groups and residents through events and collaborative programs. Organizers say this launch is a first step toward a more connected cultural community in the region.
