Umi Kaze Terrace will host the 10th edition of its 'singing salon' on 15 October. Guest 'Akika' will lead the program; she works as a voice coach, event instructor, and performs as a stage singer and pianist. The event invites participants to sing familiar Showa-era tunes together in a relaxed salon setting. Additional program details and participation information were provided by the venue.
