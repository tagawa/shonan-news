An event called 'Showa Chat Café' will take place at the NPO Takusukeai Workers Ozora, located at Nakazato 731-3 in Ninomiya, on 29 September, from 1:00 p.m. to about 3:00 p.m. Participants will pay a small fee to cover tea. The gathering invites people to talk about life and memories from the Showa era in an informal café setting.
