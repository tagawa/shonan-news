The national autumn traffic safety campaign runs from 21 September to 30 September. In connection with the campaign, Kamakura Police Station will hold an autumn traffic safety campaign on 24 September at Kamakura Seaside Park in the Yuigahama area, featuring a performance by the police music band. The event aims to raise awareness of road safety during the campaign period. Further details such as exact times and program content were not provided in the report.
