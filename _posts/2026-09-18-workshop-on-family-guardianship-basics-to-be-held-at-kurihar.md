The Hiratsuka City Adult Guardianship Support Center is organizing a family guardianship training titled '親族後見人の仕事のきほんを学ぶ〜大切な家族と未来を守るために〜' on 30 September at Hiratsuka Kurihara Home's third-floor large conference room. The session is aimed at relatives who may act as guardians and will cover practical responsibilities and legal basics to help protect loved ones and their futures. Participants should contact the support center for registration details and any participation requirements.
