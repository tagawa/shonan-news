The course, titled ‘Time to Organize Your Life and Mind’, is organized by ‘Your Town Health Room’ to provide a casual space for people to talk about health, daily worries, and living matters that do not require a hospital visit. It will take place on 27 September at Hayama Town Library and covers practical tidying and ‘shukatsu’ — preparing affairs for the end of life. The event aims to help participants simplify their homes and minds while offering information and conversation in an approachable setting.
