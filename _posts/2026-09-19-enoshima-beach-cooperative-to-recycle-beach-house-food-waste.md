The Enoshima Beach Cooperative started a project on 9 September at Katase Nishihama and Kugenuma bathing beaches to process food waste from 'umi no ie' beach huts. The cooperative is working with the company 'J Bio Food Recycle' to use fermentation technology to enable local recycling and contribute to renewable energy.
