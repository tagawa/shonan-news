An event for parents and children to observe nearby nature, titled 'Parent-and-child fun! Nagakubo Park Ikimono Tankentai', was held on 13 September at Nagakubo Park (Fujisawa, Tsujido Taiheidai 2). The activity focused on watching local nature and learning about biodiversity.
