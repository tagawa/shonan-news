Sachie Yamamoto, age 81, a resident of Oba, serves as a morning 'traffic guide' and is the oldest such volunteer within the Fujisawa Kita Police Station area; she directs schoolchildren and other pedestrians at street crossings. The report notes her role in protecting local traffic safety and shows her performing the guiding gesture described as a single swing.
