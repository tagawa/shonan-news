Sachiko Kiyota of Zushi, who will turn 100 this year, sang at the Sakae Civic Culture Center in Yokohama on 12 September. The report describes her voice as both powerful and delicate, and notes she survived harrowing war experiences and overcame cancer before delivering the performance.
