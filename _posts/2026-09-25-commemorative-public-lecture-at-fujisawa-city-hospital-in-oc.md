Fujisawa City Hospital's Emergency and Critical Care Center, marking its 20th anniversary, will hold a commemorative public lecture on October 1 (Thu). The event will run from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the East Building fourth-floor auditorium, is free to attend, and requires no prior registration.
