The steam locomotive 'Shiokaze' at Kugenuma Undo Park (commonly called Happo Park) in Fujisawa, located at Kugenuma Kaigan 6-12-1, will reach 50 years since it was brought to the park next month. An 'SL matsuri' will be held on 12 October (Mon) in the park as an anniversary celebration.
