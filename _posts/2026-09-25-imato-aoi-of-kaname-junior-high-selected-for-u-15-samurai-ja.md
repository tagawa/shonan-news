Imato Aoi, a third-year student at Hiratsuka Municipal Kaname Junior High and a member of Shonan Boys in Fujisawa, was chosen for the U-15 Samurai Japan squad for the WBSC U-15 Baseball World Cup in Mexico beginning 25 September (Fri). The tournament is described as the WBSC U-15 Baseball World Cup 2026 in the source text.
