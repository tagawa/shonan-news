Ad-Mind Co., Ltd. operates the senior computer class Fujitsu Open College Yokosuka Campus at Kurihama 4-3-6, a one-minute walk from Keikyu Kurihama Station. The program is described as a place where older residents can casually gather and interact, offering conversational and social opportunities alongside computer lessons.
