The steam locomotive 'Shiokaze' at Kugenuma Undo Koen, commonly called Hachibu Park, will reach its 50th year in the park next month. On October 12 (Mon) the park will hold an 'SL Matsuri' featuring preservation activities and a mini railway operating event.
