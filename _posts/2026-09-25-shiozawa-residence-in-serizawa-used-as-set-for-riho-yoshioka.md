The movie 'Shadow Work,' starring 'Riho Yoshioka,' was shot at the Shiozawa Residence, an old private house in Serizawa, Chigasaki City, and is scheduled for release on 25 September. The site has recently hosted a string of drama and film shoots, and people involved said this has contributed to the revitalization of northern Chigasaki City.
