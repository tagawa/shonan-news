Takao Morizono, 45, a Kamakura City resident, is serving as executive chair of 'Oktoberfest Kamakura' to be held on October third and fourth at Kamakura City Hall. The event, titled 'Oktoberfest Kamakura 2026', will bring together breweries from within Kanagawa Prefecture and across the country, and will feature a variety of food and stage performances. Morizono is involved through his affiliation with the Kamakura Chamber of Commerce and Industry.
