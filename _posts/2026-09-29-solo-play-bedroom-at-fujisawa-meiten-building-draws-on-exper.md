Hodaka's solo play, titled 'Hodaka Solo Play: Bedroom,' will run from 2 to 4 October at the Rooftop Theater atop Fujisawa Meiten Building, 2 Minami-Fujisawa, Fujisawa City. The play takes the experience of school refusal as its motif.
