On July 25, 2026, Southern Beach Festa will run from 16:00 to 20:30 at Southern Beach Chigasaki, offering a magical beach atmosphere with events such as a street piano, a future "hand" exhibition, bamboo lantern making, lighting of the shoreline and Eboshi Rock, and candle art. The event is free to enter, though workshops are paid; it will be canceled in case of severe weather.
