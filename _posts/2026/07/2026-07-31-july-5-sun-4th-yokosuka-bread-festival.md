About 10 popular bakeries from Yokosuka will gather for a must-visit event for bread lovers. The event runs from 11:00 to 14:00, but items may sell out so early arrival is recommended; admission tickets will be distributed at the main entrance from 10:00 and near the venue after 10:30. The event takes place at Ichigo Yokosuka Port Market indoor stage, and inquiries can be made to the management office at 046 (823) 1015.
