The Yakumo Festival, an early-summer tradition of Misaki, will be held to pray for the end of epidemics and for good health, continuing an ancient shrine ritual. Events begin at 10:00 with the mikoshi being raised and carried through the town, and the mikoshi is scheduled to return to the shrine around 22:00 at Kainan Shrine, Misaki Shitamachi 7; inquiries: ☎046（881）3038 Kainan Shrine.
