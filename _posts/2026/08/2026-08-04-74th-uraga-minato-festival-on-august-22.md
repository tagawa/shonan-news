The festival on August 22 runs from 16:00 to 20:00 at Nishi-Uraga Minato Ryokuchi and the surrounding Uraga Port area. Events include ritual dances and festival music to honor the dead, as well as food and game stalls. The evening concludes with about 500 fireworks launched from Uraga harbor, scheduled from 19:30 to 20:00; the event will be canceled in case of severe weather. For inquiries, contact Yokosuka City Call Center at 046（822）4000.
