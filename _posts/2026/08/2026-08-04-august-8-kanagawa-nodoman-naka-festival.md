The Nodoman Naka Festival will be held on August 8 (Saturday) at Samukawa Central Park from 16:00 to 20:00, and will be canceled in case of rain. The event features a fair atmosphere with festival stalls, dance and ohayashi (traditional festival music) stages, and popular kitchen cars. Local shops will run workshops, and a large raffle sponsored by companies offers prizes to create lasting summer memories. For inquiries, contact Samukawa Town Chamber of Commerce Youth Division at 0467-75-0185.
