On August 5, Afternoon Tea Living on the first floor of Terrace Mall Shonan will release collaboration items with the NHK art-themed program “びじゅチューン!” (Bijutune!). The lineup includes two new items featuring the “Pearl Earring Kunoichi” motif and six reissued items. The release takes place at Terrace Mall Shonan in Fujisawa City.
