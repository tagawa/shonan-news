BS日テレ's program "友近・礼二の妄想トレイン" will broadcast on August 4 at 9:00 p.m., featuring actor 手塚理美 traveling the Enoden railway. The episode highlights the line's new rolling stock introduced after 20 years and showcases photogenic spots in 鎌倉市 and 江の島 that surprised 中川家・礼二. Viewers can expect scenic views and recommended photo locations along the Enoden route.
