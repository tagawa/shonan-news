The exhibition displays carnivorous plants that trap and eat insects. Visitors who collect stamps and answer a quiz can bring their results to the shop register to receive a gift. The event is recommended for school research projects; admission is 400 yen for those 20 and older and free for junior high school students and younger. For inquiries call 0467-46-2188 (Ofuna Flower Center).
