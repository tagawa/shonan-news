Enoshima Electric Railway is offering a limited edition "Reiwa 8 Year (2026) August 8 Commemorative Ticket" starting August 8, 2026. The release is limited to 2,000 sets and each set contains six hard tickets mounted on a card featuring photos of the nostalgic 800 series that ran from 1971 to 1986. The full six-station set is priced at 800 yen, tax included.
