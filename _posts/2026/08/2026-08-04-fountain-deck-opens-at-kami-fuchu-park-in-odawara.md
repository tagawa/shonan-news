Nakamura Seisakusho installed a fountain facility called the Fountain Deck in the waterside plaza of Kami-Fuchu Park in Odawara City. The renovated area reopened on 2026-07-04 and is promoted as a new summer spot where even toddlers can play safely and securely in the water. The facility is intended to provide a family-friendly water play area for local residents and visitors.
