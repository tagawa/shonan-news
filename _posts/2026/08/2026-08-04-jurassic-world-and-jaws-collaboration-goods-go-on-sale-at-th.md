Thank You Mart inside Lalaport Shonan Hiratsuka in Hiratsuka City will begin selling collaboration goods featuring the films Jurassic World and Jaws starting Friday, August 7, 2026. The items will be released sequentially and are described as a lineup of limited, summer-appropriate goods aimed at fans. Shoppers can expect a variety of themed merchandise available at the store in the shopping complex.
