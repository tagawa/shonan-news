Kamakura City, in partnership with en Corporation, has opened an independent nationwide recruitment for public elementary and junior high school teachers. Successful hires will be employed under city-funded contracts with salaries and treatment equivalent to prefectural recruitment. The application period runs from 2026-08-03 to 2026-08-30. The initiative is part of Kamakura's broader effort to pursue education reform and has drawn public attention.
