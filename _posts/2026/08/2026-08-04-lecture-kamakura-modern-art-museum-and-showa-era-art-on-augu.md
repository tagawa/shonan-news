A special lecture by former director Tadayasu Sakai will be held on August 15 from 14:00 to 15:30 (doors open 13:45) at the Kanagawa Prefectural Museum of Modern Art, Hayama. The talk is connected to the museum's ongoing Showa 100th anniversary collection exhibition; admission is free and no advance reservation is required, limited to the first 60 attendees on the day. For inquiries, contact 046 (875) 2800. The museum's holdings include Shunsuke Matsumoto's 1935 oil on board "Building."
