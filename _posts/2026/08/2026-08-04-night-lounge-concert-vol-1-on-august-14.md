A new casual evening series, NIGHT LOUNGE CONCERT vol.1, will take place on August 14 from 19:15 to 20:15, with doors opening at 18:30. Young musicians from Fujisawa on piano, violin and cello will perform at Fujisawa City Shonandai Cultural Center, Citizen Theater. Admission is 1,500 yen, general seating; advance booking is available through the listed ticket site.
