Strong Heart, which operates a Shonan office in Chigasaki City, is sponsoring the Gold's Gym–hosted 2026 Muscle Gate Shonan competition on August 29 at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall. The company is supporting bodybuilders by providing its additive-free protein bar product, Strong Bar, at the event. The sponsorship reflects Strong Heart's local presence and engagement in fitness events in Chigasaki.
