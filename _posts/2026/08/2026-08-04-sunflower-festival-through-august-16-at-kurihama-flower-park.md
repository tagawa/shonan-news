Visitors can enjoy Pioneer sunflowers, valued for their green manure effect on cosmos, blooming across 18,000㎡ in the poppy and cosmos garden at Kurihama Flower Park. The festival is open from 10:00 to 16:00, admission is free, and popular herb soft-serve ice cream is available. For inquiries, contact the park at 046 (833) 8282.
