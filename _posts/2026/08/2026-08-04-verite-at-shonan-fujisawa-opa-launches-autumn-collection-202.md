Jewelry shop Verite on the 2nd floor of Shonan-Fujisawa OPA in Fujisawa City is presenting its Autumn Collection 2026 to mark its 35th anniversary as a listed company. The collection features new pieces using Akoya pearls and the rare White Butterfly gold pearls, and the store is also holding a consultation event for jewelry remodeling. The lineup emphasizes seasonal pieces and specialty pearls aimed at customers seeking anniversary and gift purchases.
