Courceka Bayside Stores will run a campaign from July 31 to August 30, 2026, giving a total of 3,000 yen in electronic coupons to customers who sign up for a new Epos Card. New cardholders can choose from popular collaboration design cards as part of the offer. The promotion is limited to the campaign period and applies at the Courceka Bayside Stores location in Yokosuka.
