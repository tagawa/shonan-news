The professional 3-on-3 basketball league 3x3.EXE PREMIER will stage Round.7 of its 2026 Japan Men's series on August 15. The event is scheduled at Tokkei Security Hiratsuka General Gymnasium in Ohara, Hiratsuka City, and will feature the local team playing a home match. Organizers expect fans to attend the single-day tournament in Hiratsuka.
