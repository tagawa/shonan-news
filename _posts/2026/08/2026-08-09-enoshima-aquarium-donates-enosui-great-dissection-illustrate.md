Shin-Enoshima Aquarium, located at 2 Katasekaigan in Fujisawa, published "Enosui Sea Picture Book 'Enosui Great Dissection Illustrated Encyclopedia'" on July 3. The aquarium donated copies of the book to Fujisawa City public elementary, junior high, and special-needs schools through the Fujisawa City Board of Education. The gift aims to support local marine education by providing illustrated, educational materials about sea life to students across the city.
