The inaugural Stars Cup, a 3x3 (three-on-three) basketball tournament for elementary school students, is scheduled for September 20 (Sunday) at Chigasaki City General Gymnasium, Second Gym. Organizers are inviting teams of elementary-age players to participate and experience the fast-paced format used internationally. The event aims to promote youth sports and give children a chance to learn and enjoy 3x3 rules and play. Additional details such as registration, game times, and eligibility are being provided by the event organizers.
