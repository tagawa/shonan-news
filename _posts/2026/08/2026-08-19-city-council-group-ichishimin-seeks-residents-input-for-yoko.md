The Yokosuka city council group Ichishimin is gathering input from residents to shape policy proposals to submit to the city. They say they are drawing on experiences from local activities, citizen consultations, and town meetings to identify priorities. The group invites residents to share their concerns and suggestions so those voices can be reflected in Yokosuka's policies.
