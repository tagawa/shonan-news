The documentary "Yokosuka 1953" follows Yoko Kiga, a GI baby born in postwar Yokosuka, as she searches for her biological mother. The film will be shown on August 24 at Zushi City Cultural Plaza. The screening highlights the chaotic post-World War II period in which many children of American servicemen and Japanese women were born and explores personal and historical roots.
