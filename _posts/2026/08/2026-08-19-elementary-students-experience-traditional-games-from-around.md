The Tamagawa Youth Center hosted a workshop on July 28 titled “Let’s Try! Games of the World,” its fifth edition. Elementary students experienced a variety of games from the Americas, Asia, and Europe, including China’s to-hu (投壺) and board games from Israel. Organizers aimed to broaden children’s cultural understanding through hands-on play and demonstrations.
