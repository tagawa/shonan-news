The German beer festival Yokosuka Oktoberfest 2026 opened on April 17 at Verny Park along Yokosuka Honko in Yokosuka City’s Shioiri-cho 1. The event celebrates German beer and brings the Oktoberfest atmosphere to the local waterfront. Organizers expect visitors to enjoy beer offerings and festival attractions during the event period.
