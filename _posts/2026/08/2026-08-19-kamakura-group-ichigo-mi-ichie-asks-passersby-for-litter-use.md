The group 'Ichigo Mi-Ichie' frames its work as receiving, not picking up, litter to foster conversation and deeper communication with people. On March 15, members walked around Kamakura Station using a garbage-sorting machine developed by high school students involved in manufacturing. The activity aims to engage tourists and passersby through interaction while promoting proper waste separation.
