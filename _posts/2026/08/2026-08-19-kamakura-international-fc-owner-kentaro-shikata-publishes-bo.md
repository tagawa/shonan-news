Kentaro Shikata, owner of Kanagawa Prefecture amateur first-division club Kamakura International FC (known as Kamakura Inter), has written a book titled "越境人～飛び出した人からうまくいく～." The book was published by Sunmark Publishing on June 10. The article was reported by Kamakura Keizai Shimbun.
