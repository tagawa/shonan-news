The Cresta Summer Festival took place on August 11 (Tuesday, public holiday) at Gold Crest Stadium Kamakura, the home ground of Kamakura International FC. The event was a regional exchange festival that included appearances by players from Kamakura International FC. Activities aimed to bring the local community together at the stadium.
