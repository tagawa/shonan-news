Kamakura Keizai Shimbun reported that its most-viewed article for the first half of 2026 covered the "Kamakura Station Front Ajisai Night Market." The story described an event that closes the road in front of Kamakura Station East Exit to vehicular traffic to hold a night market. The ranking was part of the newspaper's 2026 first-half pageview tally.
