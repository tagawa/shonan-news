The club entered its second year with Teru Miyazawa taking over as the second president from the founding president, who was also an organizer. Miyazawa said his initial focus will be on strengthening the club's foundation by increasing opportunities for members to interact, because "members' bonds have not yet fully deepened." The aim is to build cohesion among members before expanding other activities.
