A 'Nodojiman' singing contest will take place October 4 (Sunday) in front of Sun Beach Oppama (Oppama Honcho 1-28-5) in Yokosuka City's Oppama shopping district. Comedian and Oppama native 'Make Debut Yamada' will serve as master of ceremonies. Organizers are recruiting participants for the event; interested people should contact the local shopping street association for entry details.
