Residents in their teens and twenties from Yokosuka have collaborated on a voter education video titled "届け！ Z世代の声" to encourage turnout for the June 22 voting for the city's mayoral and city council by-elections. The videos are being released on YouTube and shown on 10 street signage locations around the city as part of the public outreach.
