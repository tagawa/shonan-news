The article urges owners of seldom-used expensive items—such as Rolex and Omega watches and Chanel bags—to consider selling them rather than leaving them unused. Daikokuya Hiratsuka Kita-guchi store offers appraisal and purchase services and highlights that many people realize they last used such items years ago. The piece frames selling as a way to put dormant valuables to better use and recover cash from items that no longer fit everyday life.
