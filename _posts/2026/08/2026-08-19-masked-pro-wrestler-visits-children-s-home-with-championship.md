The organization Machi Emi Pro Wrestling, led by Hitoshi Ejima, brought masked wrestler Andy Woo to a child welfare facility on August 3 to give children experiences of excitement, challenge, and inspiration through pro wrestling. The visit featured demonstrations and interactions centered on a championship belt, which fascinated the children and encouraged them to dream. The group aims to continue similar visits to support and motivate kids in care.
