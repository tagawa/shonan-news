The exhibition, called "MasuG (Masujī) Photo Exhibition," honors Masuda Masayoshi, who spent years documenting civic activities in Yokosuka. It opened on July 5 at the beachfront cafe Blue Moon, located at 1 Tsukuihama, Yokosuka. The show presents his long-running photographic record of local community events and civic life.
