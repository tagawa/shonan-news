The Yokosuka Circular Plant, a used-plastic recycling facility in Shinmei-cho, Yokosuka, developed a high-performance recycled material called CirculeX. The plant succeeded in creating the new material and started mass production in February. The project aims to advance recycling technology and produce high-function recycled plastics for broader use.
