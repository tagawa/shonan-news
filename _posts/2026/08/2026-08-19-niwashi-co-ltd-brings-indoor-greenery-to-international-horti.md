Niwashi Co., Ltd., a landscaping firm based in the Shonan and Yokohama area, is one of the exhibitors at the flower and green festival opening next March in Seya Ward, Yokohama. The company plans to present examples of indoor greening that highlight skilled craftsmanship and the appeal of plants in interior spaces. Their exhibit aims to demonstrate practical techniques for bringing greenery into homes and commercial buildings, reflecting regional landscaping traditions adapted for modern indoor environments.
