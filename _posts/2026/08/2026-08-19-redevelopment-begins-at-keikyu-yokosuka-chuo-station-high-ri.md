Redevelopment in front of Keikyu Yokosuka-Chuo Station has entered a full-scale phase. A groundbreaking ceremony for construction of a new commercial facility was held on January 21. The project envisions a high-rise mixed-use building as part of the station-front redevelopment. Local officials and stakeholders marked the start of construction at the site.
