The oceanographic research vessel Mirai, which retired in December of last year, held a retirement commemorative event on January 25 at Yokosuka Shinko Pier. The event included a public opening of the ship for visitors to tour the interior. The activity was reported by Yokosuka Keizai Shimbun.
