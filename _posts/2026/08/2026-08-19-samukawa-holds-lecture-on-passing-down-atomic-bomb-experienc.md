Samukawa will host a "Hibaku Taiken Densho Kokwa" (lecture on transmitting atomic-bomb experiences) on August 20 (Thursday) from 10:00 to 11:30 at the town hall east branch office, Meeting Room No. 1, with remote streaming available. The program will feature Seiji Morikawa, a second-generation hibakusha, who will speak about experiences and memories of the atomic bomb; attendees can participate either in person or online.
