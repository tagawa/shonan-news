The 27th Chigasaki Peace Film Festival takes place on August 26 at the Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall small hall. The film to be shown, 'Kurokawa no Onnatachi,' depicts women in Manchuria after Japan's 1945 defeat who became victims of sexual violence while trying to return home. Director Fumie Matsubara will give a greeting at the venue.
