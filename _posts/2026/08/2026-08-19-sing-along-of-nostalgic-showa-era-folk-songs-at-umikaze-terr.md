An event inviting participants to hum along to Showa-era folk songs with guitar accompaniment will take place September 5 (Saturday) from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, with doors opening at 1:30 PM, in the Basement 1 Meeting Room at Umikaze Terrace. The participation fee is 50 (text truncated in the source), and attendees will gather to enjoy familiar folk tunes from the Showa period in a casual group setting.
