The Yokosuka Chamber of Commerce and Industry, which promotes itself as a town that supports entrepreneurship, held a “Petit Opening School” in its hall on August 2. About 70 local residents, including those interested in side businesses, attended to learn practical steps for starting their own ventures. The event is part of broader efforts in Yokosuka City to encourage new business creation and self-directed entrepreneurship.
