Inaoaka Housing is offering Sunny BOX self-storage units at nine sites across Chigasaki City to help residents manage limited home storage. The service targets people needing temporary storage during renovations or moves and those who want to store ski and snowboard gear, winter clothing, or hobby collections. The company highlights convenience for residents struggling with crowded homes and seasonal equipment.
