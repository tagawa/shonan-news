Two consecutive weekend concerts are scheduled in Kamakura. On August 11, a program titled "真夏の演奏会、バイオリンとピアノが織りなすプレリュード" will take place at 今泉台町内会館 (吉ガ沢公園・今泉台４の６). On August 22, another concert will be held at a different local venue. The events aim to offer live music for visitors enjoying their holidays in Kamakura.
