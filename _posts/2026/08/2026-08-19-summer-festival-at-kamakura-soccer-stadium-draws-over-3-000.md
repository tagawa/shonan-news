The event, organized by the soccer club Kamakura International FC (known as Kamakura Inter) to strengthen ties with the local community, took place at Gold Crest Stadium Kamakura in Kamakura city’s Dai neighborhood. More than 3,000 attendees joined festivities that included a communal dance called "Minna de Odorou Hato Ondo." The festival is part of the club’s ongoing efforts to engage residents through local events.
