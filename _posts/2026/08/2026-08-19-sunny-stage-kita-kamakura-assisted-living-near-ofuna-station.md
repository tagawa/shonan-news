Sunny Stage Kita-Kamakura presents its mission as weaving each person’s wishes and connecting their past and future to create moments when residents feel glad they chose the facility. Located within walking distance of Ofuna Station, the paid nursing home emphasizes care with services designed to let residents pursue activities they want to do. The facility promotes daily life that respects individual desires and continuity of identity, framed as offering time that makes residents say, "I’m glad I’m at Sunny Stage."
