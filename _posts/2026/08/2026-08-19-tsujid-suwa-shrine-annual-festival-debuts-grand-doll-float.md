The Tsujidō Suwa Shrine annual festival took place July 26 and 27 at the shrine precincts and nearby streets. In addition to two mikoshi portable shrines and four taiko floats, four neighborhood associations from east, west, south and north of the shrine presented a large doll float based on the shrine as a reference, and Fujisawa City designated it as a tangible folk cultural property. Visitors enjoyed the parade of these traditional floats and portable shrines during the two-day event.
