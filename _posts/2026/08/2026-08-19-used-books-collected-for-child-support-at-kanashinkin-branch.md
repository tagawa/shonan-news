Regional financial institution Kanagawa Shinkin Bank is running a donation program called "こどものみらい古本募金" that uses donated, read books to raise funds for children. Collection boxes have been placed at the bank's head office and branches across Kanagawa Prefecture. The program is organized through the shinkin bank's central body to convert donated books into support for child-related services.
