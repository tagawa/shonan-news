The citizen theatre group SUKAミュー (横須賀市民ミュージカルを作る会) is marking its 25th anniversary and aims to stage a summer performance in August. To fund the production, the group has launched a crowdfunding campaign and is calling for public cooperation and donations. The appeal emphasizes the community nature of the company and its goal of continuing local musical theatre in Yokosuka.
