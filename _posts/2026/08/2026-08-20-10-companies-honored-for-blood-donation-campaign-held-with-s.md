The Kanagawa Red Cross Blood Center presented letters of appreciation to 10 companies that contributed to a blood-donation campaign held alongside the 31st Grand Sumo Fujisawa Tournament. The awards were handed out by Center Director Rie Okubo at the center in Kohoku Ward, Yokohama City. The campaign took place on July 31 at the center.
