Masayuki Nakano, president of the Samukawa Rotary Club, unveiled the club’s theme for the year: “We Can Do It〜with a smile.” He said the club will focus on bringing in new members so it can be firmly passed on to the next generation. Nakano intends to make this year one of growth and continuity to ensure the club remains needed in the community.
