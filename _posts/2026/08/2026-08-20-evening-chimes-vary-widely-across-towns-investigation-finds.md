The survey checked timing and songs of evening chimes heard while walking towns at dusk and found they reflect each municipality's history, notable local figures, and unique local touches. Broadcast times and melodies differ by locality, with some communities choosing tunes tied to historical events or local personalities and others using distinctive arrangements or timing. The findings highlight how a common practice — broadcasting evening chimes — carries diverse local meanings and craftsmanship across the paper's coverage area.
