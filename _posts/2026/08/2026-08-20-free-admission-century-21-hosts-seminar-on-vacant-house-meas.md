Century 21 Miraizu, a real estate company in Odawara City, will host a seminar titled "Vacant House Problems & End-of-Life Housing" on September 12 at the Odawara City Kawahigashi Town Center Marronnier, Nakazato 273-6. The event offers free admission and will provide professional advice on dealing with vacant homes and planning for the end of one’s housing needs. The seminar is intended for local residents concerned about vacant properties and housing arrangements as they age.
