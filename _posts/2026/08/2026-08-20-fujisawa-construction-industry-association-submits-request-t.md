The Fujisawa City Construction Industry Association, led by Chairman Murakami Susumu, submitted a written request to the city on the 30th. It asked for measures addressing worsening business conditions for local construction firms amid rising material costs, higher labor expenses, and elevated crude oil prices. The association sought changes related to workstyle reform and the public bidding system to help stabilize the industry's finances and operations.
