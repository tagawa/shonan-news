Fujisawa Minami Rotary Club, led by President 羽原淑恵, held its fifth annual event on July 24 at the beach hut at 辻堂海水浴場 where children were invited to paint freely while learning about marine conservation. Participants decorated the hut in colorful artworks and received instruction on protecting the ocean, aiming to raise environmental awareness among young residents. The club plans to continue the program in future seasons to keep engaging children with hands-on environmental activities.
