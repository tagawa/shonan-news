When choosing a care facility, considering what daily life will be like is as important as location. Habitas Karma, a residential paid nursing home in Nishi-Uraga with views of Tokyo Bay, emphasizes a relaxed living environment where residents can enjoy life. The facility promotes a home-like lifestyle and also accepts short-term stays, including during consecutive holiday periods, to support families and provide respite care.
