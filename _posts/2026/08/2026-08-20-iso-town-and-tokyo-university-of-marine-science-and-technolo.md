On July 12, Ōiso Town concluded a partnership agreement with the national university Tokyo University of Marine Science and Technology to collaborate on seaside community development using coastal regional resources. The agreement aims to enhance local value by leveraging the attractions and resources of the sea and coastline, and to promote sustainable use and revitalization of coastal areas. The collaboration will involve joint initiatives between the town and the university to support projects that integrate research, education, and community planning.
