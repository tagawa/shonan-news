Rihito Masuyama, a 48-year-old artist living in Kamakura, released his debut picture book "ベベタンとぺヨタン 星のうまれるところへ" on July 26. The book explores themes of life’s cycles and interconnectedness through a story and illustrations that reflect Masuyama’s artistic sensibility. It represents his first foray into children’s publishing after being featured in the local biographical column 人物風土記.
