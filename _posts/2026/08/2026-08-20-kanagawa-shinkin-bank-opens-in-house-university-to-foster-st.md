Kanagawa Shinkin Bank, headquartered in Yokosuka, has established an internal educational institution named Kanashin Graduate University (Shohaku Juku) to cultivate pride and skills among its staff. The bank held an entrance ceremony on August 6 for 14 first-year students. The program is intended to provide structured training and foster a shared corporate identity among employees.
