The purchase salon "買取サロン メゾンスタア" near Fujisawa Station North Exit, a four-minute walk from 江ノ電藤沢駅, is holding an August-only "貴金属高価買取キャンペーン." Under the offer, customers bringing in five or more gold or platinum items, or three or more diamond or colored-stone jewelry pieces, can receive increased appraisal amounts. The campaign aims to encourage selling while market conditions are favorable; additional details and any limits are available at the salon.
