A special event called the Shonan Kamishibai Festival, aimed at both children and adults, will be held on September 6 (Sunday) in the second-floor nursery room of Chibikko Hoikuen Fujisawa-en, located at Asahi-cho 13 no 5. The festival runs from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. and features kamishibai storytelling, blending nostalgia with fresh programming for all ages. The announcement describes it as a first-time event intended to provide enjoyment across generations.
