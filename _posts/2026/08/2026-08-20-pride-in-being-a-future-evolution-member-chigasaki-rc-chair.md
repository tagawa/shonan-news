Keiji Tomita, chair of the Chigasaki Rotary Club, said the club—whose history spans 66 years—has set this year's theme as "Future Evolution" to strengthen members' pride and make the organization shine. He emphasized that members should take pride in having once led the community and work to revive the club's prominence. The theme is intended to help the Rotary Club evolve and continue contributing to the local community.
