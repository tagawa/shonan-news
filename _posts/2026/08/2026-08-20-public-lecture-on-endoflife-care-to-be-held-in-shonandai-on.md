The second public lecture, titled "What Is End‑of‑Life Medical Care: From the Perspective of Home Visits to Elderly Care Facilities," is scheduled for September 5 at the Shonandai Civic Center basement hall. The event is organized as a Fujisawa city commissioned project and will address medical care in the final stage of life, drawing on experiences from physicians who make home visits to elderly care facilities. The talk is aimed at local residents interested in understanding end‑of‑life care options and practices.
