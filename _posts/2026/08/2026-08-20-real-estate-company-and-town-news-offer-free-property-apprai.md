A collaboration between a real estate company and Town News is offering free home appraisals to residents of Hiratsuka, Oiso, Ninomiya and Nakai. The offer targets homeowners considering downsizing after children leave or planning inheritance measures as local property values rise. Applications are accepted until September 4. The initiative aims to help people prepare for retirement and succession by clarifying current property values.
