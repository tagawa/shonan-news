Hiratsuka Hachimangu Shrine, led by chief priest Junhiko Takuno, held its reitaisai from August 14 to 16. On August 14 activities included the misogi purification ritual at 6:00 a.m. at Oogimatsu Beach, an evening shrine ceremony at 5:30 p.m., and a bon dance at 6:00 p.m. Other festival events took place through August 16 across the shrine grounds.
