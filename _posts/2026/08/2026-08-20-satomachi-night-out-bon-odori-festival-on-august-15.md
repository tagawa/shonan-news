The event called "Satomachi Night Out: Nakai Everyone's Bon Odori" took place at Nakai Central Park's Nakai Satomachi♡CAFE (Hinekubo 580) from 5:00 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. Admission was free and the event would be canceled in case of rain. The festival offered a community Bon Odori gathering for local residents.
