Tetsuya Okubo, a 190cm-tall soccer player nicknamed “Jumbo” from Nagahuracho, Yokosuka City, spoke at a lecture held on August 1 at Werk Yokosuka in Hinode Town. The event was organized by a general incorporated foundation and drew local residents interested in his career and experiences. Okubo discussed his journey as a professional athlete and reflected on aiming for “one more goal” as he continues his travels and public appearances.
