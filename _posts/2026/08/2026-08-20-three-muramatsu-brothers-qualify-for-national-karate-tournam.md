Ibuki Muramatsu (sixth grade), Towa Muramatsu (fourth grade), and Sora Muramatsu (second grade) from Kosaka Elementary advanced past prefectural qualifiers and will compete in the national karate tournament held in Tokyo from August 13 to August 15. The brothers expressed that their long-held dream came true and they aim to support each other while giving their best performances. Their school and local community celebrated the achievement, noting the rarity of three siblings reaching the national level together.
