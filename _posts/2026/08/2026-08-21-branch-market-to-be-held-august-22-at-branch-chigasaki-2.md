Handmade group SHIGONIN is hosting the BRANCH Market on August 22 at BRANCH Chigasaki 2 on the second floor. The market runs from 10:00 to 15:00 and will be held the same day as the neighborhood summer festival. Attendees can expect stalls featuring handmade goods and activities typical of a local mar¬ché.
