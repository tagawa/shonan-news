Yuharu Atsuta was a key cameraman at Shochiku Ofuna Studio who helped shape the look of many masterpieces by directors such as Yasujirō Ozu and influenced filmmakers worldwide. His daughter and son now manage his archive and promote his visual philosophy through exhibitions, screenings, and discussions in Kamakura, the town once known as a center of Japanese cinema. The family’s activities aim to deepen public understanding of Atsuta’s techniques and the studio’s historical role while passing his artistic values to new generations.
