The event, called the '1coin Noryokai' regional exchange meeting, is organized by the Chogo District Local Development Promotion Council’s Regional Human Resource Discovery and Development Subcommittee. It is aimed at residents of the Chogo district and will transform the Chogo Civic Center into a live-house style venue for the occasion. The program is intended to foster local connections and support community talent development.
