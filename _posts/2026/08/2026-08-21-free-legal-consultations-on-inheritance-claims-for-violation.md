Many people face inheritance problems such as family disputes or complex relationships. The law firm "弁護士法人湘南ＬＡＧＯＯＮ" (Shonan LAGOON) is providing free consultations with lawyers who specialize in inheritance matters, including guidance on inheritance division and claims for violation of the reserved portion (遺留分侵害額請求). The service is intended to help residents understand their legal options and seek appropriate legal assistance.
