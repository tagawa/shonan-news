The Koide District Community Center at 茅ヶ崎市堤１９４８の１ is hosting "Comi Piano," an event where visitors can freely play the piano. The event runs until August 20 and is open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Visitors of all ages are welcome to stop by and play during those hours.
