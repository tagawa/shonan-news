A Town News investigation cataloged evening chimes and melodies heard while walking around municipalities, noting broadcast times and songs. The findings show local governments choose tunes tied to their history, notable residents, or unique community practices. In Fujisawa City, the evening chime confirmed by the survey is “夕焼け小焼け.”
