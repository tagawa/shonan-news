The Fujisawa City Development and Management Public Interest Incorporated Foundation is offering all eight lots exclusively to Fujisawa residents. The site in Shonandai is a 14-minute walk from the station and is being promoted for its strong transportation convenience, nearby shops, parks, and a children's center. The program aims to encourage a second Fujisawa lifestyle for city residents by selling well-located building-ready plots.
