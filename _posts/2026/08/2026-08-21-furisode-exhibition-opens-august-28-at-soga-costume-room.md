The exhibition takes place on the third floor of Samukawa Shrine’s assembly hall in the SOGA costume room from August 28 (Friday) through September 7 (Monday), with September 1 closed. Visiting hours are split into two sessions, 10:00–12:00 and 13:00–15:00. The display showcases furisode, traditional long-sleeved kimonos, allowing visitors to view and select what they call their “fateful” garment.
