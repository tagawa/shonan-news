The Fujisawa Corporate Association is recruiting participants for a generative AI seminar titled Basics and Risk Management to be held September 7 (Monday). The event will take place in the second-floor conference room of Fujisawa Hojinkai Hall, located at Fujisawa 86, Fujisawa City, from 5:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. The seminar will cover information security and other risk-related topics alongside foundational use of generative AI.
