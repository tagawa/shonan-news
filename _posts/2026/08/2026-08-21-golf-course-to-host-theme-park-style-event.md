The event “Endless Summer” will be held on September 26 (Saturday) at Kamakura Public Golf Course at Imizu 5-1003, and organizers are currently recruiting participants. The program will feature Asia champion Hiroyuki Kuwata and other attractions as part of the day’s lineup. The golf course will be presented with theme park elements to create a festival atmosphere for visitors.
