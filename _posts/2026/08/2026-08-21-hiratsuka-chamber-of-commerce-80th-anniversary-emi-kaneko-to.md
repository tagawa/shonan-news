The Hiratsuka Chamber of Commerce will mark its 80th anniversary with a joint lecture organized by the Chamber's Cultural Information Services, Finance and Insurance, and Commerce divisions. Emi Kaneko is scheduled to speak at the event on October 10 (Saturday) at Hirashin Hiratsuka Cultural and Arts Hall. The lecture is part of the Chamber's anniversary program and is intended for members and the local business community.
