A kamishibai (illustrated story performance) teaching what to do during earthquakes and fires will be staged at the Yokosuka Citizen Activity Support Center in Honchō on August 22. The performer is Hiruta, a firefighter who works at the Yokohama City Fire Bureau and lives in Heisei-chō. The event is designed to make learning disaster response enjoyable for participants.
