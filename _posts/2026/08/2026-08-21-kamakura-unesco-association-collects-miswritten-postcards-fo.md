The NPO Kamakura UNESCO Association, which engages in charitable and international understanding activities, is continuously accepting miswritten postcards, unused stamps, and prepaid cards. Donated items are converted into funds to support the association's projects. The association is asking residents to contribute these items as an easy way to support its work in the community and internationally.
