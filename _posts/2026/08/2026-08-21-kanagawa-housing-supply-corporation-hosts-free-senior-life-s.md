The seminar, aimed at people aged 65 and older and their families, covers preparing for future long-term care and planned changes to long-term care insurance and senior housing set for 2027. Organizers note Kanagawa's elderly population is expected to peak around 2040 while the total population turns to decline, highlighting the growing importance of planning. The event is free to attend and designed to help seniors and their families understand options and financial and practical preparations for aging in place or moving to elder-specific housing.
