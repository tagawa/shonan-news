An event titled "Learn About Police Work with the Nanko Resident Officer!" will take place on August 26 (Wednesday) at Nanko Community Center, located at Chigasaki City, Nanko 6-15-1. The program runs from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. and is aimed at elementary school students, offering a chance to learn about the duties of police officers directly from the local officer. Interested participants should contact the community center for sign-up details and any eligibility or capacity limits.
