Fuyo-so, a gallery using a vacant house, opened on August 7 as a highlight of the Miura Beach Art Festival held at the summer swimming beach in Miura. The space features works by artists and creators who are reimagining the coastal landscape and activating an unused local property. Organizers say the project aims to attract visitors, revitalize the neighborhood, and present art in an accessible, everyday setting by the sea.
