Prefectural Ofuna Flower Center (Okamoto 1018) will extend its hours for four days, on August 15, 16, 22 and 23. Normally closing at 5:00 p.m., the garden will remain open until 8:30 p.m. so visitors can enjoy evening blooms and illuminated flower displays. Admission and event details follow the center’s usual rules; visitors should check the center for any additional information before attending.
