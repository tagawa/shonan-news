The series recounts episodes from Ren, the brother of Urara who was born with a disability, describing his perspective and feelings as a sibling. This installment, featuring Ai Gobongi, focuses on balancing the needs and peace of both the person with a disability and their family, and includes a scene set in a computer room. It highlights the challenges and emotional experiences siblings face while advocating for supportive community structures.
