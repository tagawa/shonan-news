The Chigasaki Workers' Civic Hall (新栄町13の32) will host the "Chigasaki Startup Support Lecture" on August 27, Thursday, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. The event is aimed at people considering starting a business and will feature talks by experienced entrepreneurs. Participation details and any application requirements were not specified in the brief notice.
