The J2 League 2026-27 season has begun, and Shonan Bellmare will host their first home match on Saturday, August 15. The club has adopted the slogan "ALL IN," expressing a determination for every member to give everything in pursuit of promotion to J1. Coaches and players emphasized unity and full commitment as they prepare for the opening match and the season ahead.
