Kodai Nakano of professional basketball club Wolga Shonan, which is based in three cities and one town including Fujisawa, served as a traffic safety ambassador. In recognition of his work, Fujisawa Kita Police Station chief Hiroshi Kojima presented Nakano with a letter of appreciation on the 6th. The police expressed hopes that Nakano will continue promoting traffic safety in the community. The presentation acknowledged the club’s contributions to local traffic-safety awareness.
