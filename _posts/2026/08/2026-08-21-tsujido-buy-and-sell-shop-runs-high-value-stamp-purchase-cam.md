Items such as long-stored stamps and stamps found during estate cleanouts can sometimes fetch unexpectedly high appraisals even if thought worthless. The Tsujido Buy-and-Sell Shop, located a two-minute walk from Tsujido Station South Exit, is running a campaign to buy a wide range of ordinary and specialty stamps at premium prices. The shop accepts many types and encourages customers to bring in stamps for appraisal rather than assuming they have no value.
