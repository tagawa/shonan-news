Wakana Fukushima, a first-year student at Yokohama Shuyukan High School who lives in Ikeda Town, Yokosuka City, represented Kanagawa Prefecture at the 59th National High School Part‑Time and Correspondence Schools Athletic Meet table tennis tournament held in Tokyo from August 4 to 6. She took part in both individual and team competitions. The article reports her participation on the national stage as a prefectural representative, highlighting the achievement for her local community.
