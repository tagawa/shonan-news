The event, organized by the West Coast Shopping Street Promotion Association under chairman Shinya Nozaki, is titled "Fureai Kodomo no Yoichi" and runs from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Saturday, August 22. This year's night market is designed for children to enjoy, featuring activities and attractions suitable for young visitors along the shopping street. The association aims to create a family-friendly evening that invites children to gather and participate in the community event.
