General Incorporated Association Yahho, which operates the community space Yahho for children in Oiso and is led by Representative Director Ke Nakamura, has launched food assistance for financially struggling households. The program started recently and is being carried out in cooperation with Food Bank Shonan and other partners. Distribution and coordination are handled from Yahho’s Oiso base, expanding local support for families facing hardship.
