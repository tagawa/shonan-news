Yokosuka City received the Japan DX Grand Prize 2026 regional award for a health support program that uses AI to anticipate residents’ needs and provide early interventions. The initiative combines digital tools and data analysis to improve efficiency in municipal health services and to deliver targeted, proactive care. The award recognizes the city’s successful use of digital transformation to enhance community health outcomes and public service delivery.
