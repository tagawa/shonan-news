The group Yokosuka Shimin Musical o Tsukuru Kai, known as SUKAミュー, marked its 25th anniversary with shows on August 15 and 16 at Yokosuka City Cultural Hall in Fukadadai 50. The production, titled “EM Club Era,” combined lively singing and dancing to recreate the energy and chaos of that period. Longtime members and new performers shared the stage, blending community enthusiasm with amateur theatrical passion. The performances attracted local audiences and highlighted the group's quarter-century contribution to Yokosuka's cultural life.
