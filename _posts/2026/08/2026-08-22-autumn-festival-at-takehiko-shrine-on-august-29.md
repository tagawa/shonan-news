Takehiko Shrine, a beloved village guardian shrine at 3580 Tsutsumi in Chigasaki, will hold its autumn festival on Saturday, August 29. Festivities run from 5:00 p.m. to about 9:00 p.m. and will feature a song show and a raffle, with food stalls offering yakisoba, yakitori, frankfurters and more.
