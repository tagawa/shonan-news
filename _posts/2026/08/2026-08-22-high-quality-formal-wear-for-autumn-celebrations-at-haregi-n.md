Autumn is a season for life milestones like weddings and Shichi-Go-San, when choosing high-quality clothing enhances commemorative photos. Haregi no Marusho Hiratsuka, a specialty rental shop with origins dating to Showa 40 (1965), draws on decades of trust and experience to provide a wide selection of formal wear. The store emphasizes carefully selected fabrics and styling to suit occasions and clients of all ages, helping families create lasting memories.
