Hiratsuka City is inviting submissions for the municipal culture festival exhibition to be displayed at the city art museum in October. Entries are accepted in three categories: painting and sculpture, photography, and calligraphy, and must be previously unpublished works. The call targets eligible applicants specified by the city; further details and application instructions are available from Hiratsuka City.
