Entering its 71st year, Nadeshiko Kindergarten began operating as an '幼保連携型の認定こども園' in fiscal 2026, marking a fresh start. The school added a new childcare building to accept infants from age 1 and implemented meals and care overseen by a nutritionist to support working parents. The changes aim to provide more comprehensive early education and childcare services for families balancing work and childrearing.
