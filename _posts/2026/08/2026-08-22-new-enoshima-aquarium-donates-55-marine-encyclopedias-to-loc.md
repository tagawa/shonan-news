New Enoshima Aquarium (President Naofumi Sakiyama) presented 55 copies of its book "えのすい海のずかん えのすい大解剖図巻" to Fujisawa City elementary, middle and special-needs schools. The donation took place at a presentation ceremony held on the same day. The gift is intended to support marine education for local students.
