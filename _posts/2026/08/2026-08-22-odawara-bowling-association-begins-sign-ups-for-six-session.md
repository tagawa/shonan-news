The Odawara Bowling Association has opened registration for its popular "Health Bowling Course," aimed at beginners and held at Corona Cat Bowl in Odawara. The program consists of six sessions and carries a participation fee of 1,000 yen. Organizers promote bowling as a lifelong sport that combines physical activity with the enjoyment of striking pins, and they are encouraging locals to enroll.
