A picture-card storytelling session focused on learning about and discussing the world and peace will take place on the 25th (Tuesday) at Eco Store Papalagi, located at 鵠沼石上１の３の６. Local author Keiko Kochiya will perform. The event runs from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., and organizers are inviting participants to join.
