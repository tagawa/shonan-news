The event, titled the 1st Chigasaki Rakugo Meeting: Third-generation Katsurakadanji Solo Performance, will take place on September 19 (Saturday) at Chigasaki Keisen Church, located at Kyoue 2-7-1. The performance starts at 2:00 p.m. The program is notable for staging rakugo, a traditional comic storytelling art, in a church setting, offering an unusual venue for the form.
