The Shonan Regional Laborers' Welfare Council, chaired by Hiroyuki Miyagi, donated a total of 450 towels on the 5th to social welfare organizations across its jurisdiction covering two cities and one town: Fujisawa, Chigasaki and Samukawa. The donation came from a group made up of labor unions and related organizations in those municipalities to support local welfare facilities. The towels were distributed to social welfare-related groups and facilities in the three municipalities to assist daily care and service activities.
