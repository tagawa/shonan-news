The season-opening league match was played at the away venue Clasdos Dome Oita, where humid dome conditions and a temperature of 29 degrees made it feel like midsummer. Shonan claimed a 1-0 victory over Oita Trinita in the first round. Homegrown goalkeeper Sanada, a product of the club's development system, finished the opening match without conceding a goal.
