Town News reported on August 7 about a special campaign offering free bulk property appraisals, which has drawn strong public interest. The program is run with three prominent local firms—Meiji Jisho Co., Ltd.; Mitsui Fudosan Realty Chigasaki Sales Office; and one other regional specialist—and targets residents seeking market information for real-estate transactions. Applications are being accepted free of charge, and the deadline for submissions is September 11.
