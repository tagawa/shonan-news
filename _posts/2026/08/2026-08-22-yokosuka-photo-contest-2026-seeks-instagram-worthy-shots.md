The Yokosuka Photo Contest 2026 invites participants to express the charms of Yokosuka through Instagram-ready photographs. The event is organized by a group including Yokosuka City and aims to promote tourism and publicize the city's appeal. Entrants submit images via Instagram to highlight picturesque spots and experiences in Yokosuka.
