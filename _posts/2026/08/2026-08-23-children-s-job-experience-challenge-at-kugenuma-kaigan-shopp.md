Organized by the Kugenuma Kaigan Shopping Street Promotion Association, chaired by Iida Masaaki, the "Children's Job Experience Challenge"—which began last year—has started again. Local elementary school students who applied take part by trying out jobs at businesses that are members of the association. The event gives kids hands-on experience in a variety of shop tasks and continues through the end of August.
