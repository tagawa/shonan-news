The event, titled "みんなでつくろう子育てマップ" (Let's Make a Childcare Map), will be held on September 19 (Saturday) in the second-floor community hall of Shonan Mall Fill. It will offer opportunities for parents and children to interact, play, and consult about childcare, starting at 10:00 a.m. Organizers from Fujisawa Junior Chamber (藤沢ＪＣ) aim to compile and share local childcare information through participant contributions.
