Organizers of the annual Fujisawa Wine Festival 2026 have added a new high school band audition to this year’s program. The Fujisawa Wine Festival Executive Committee launched the initiative to energize the autumn festival and gave students a chance to win a performance slot. The audition is billed under the name "Fuji Pop Festival" and aims to showcase local youth talent during the popular community event.
