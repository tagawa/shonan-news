On the 18th, Kenta Ogawa, head of the NSK Labor Union Fujisawa branch, and Yoshito Okibe, head of the Kirihara branch, presented 100 crossing flags to Fujisawa Mayor Suzuki Tsuneo. The flags are part of an effort tied to the national mutual aid coop for protecting children from accidents, and the donation will be used to enhance pedestrian safety for schoolchildren. City officials accepted the donation and will distribute the flags where needed.
