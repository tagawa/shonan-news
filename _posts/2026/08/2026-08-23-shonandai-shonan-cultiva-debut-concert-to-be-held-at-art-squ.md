An event titled 'Shonan Cultiva Debut Commemoration' will take place on the 31st at the Shonandai Station underground Art Square, starting at 4:30 p.m. Admission requires a 1,000 yen seat fee. The event highlights the new Shonan Cultiva project and involves performers and activities tied to the city’s northwest area and its diverse cultural scene.
