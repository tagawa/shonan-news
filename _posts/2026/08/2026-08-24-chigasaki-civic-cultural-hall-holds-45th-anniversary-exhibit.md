Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall (Chigasaki 1-11-1) opened a commemorative display titled "45 Years of Progress — Up to Now, and From Now On" in the first-floor exhibition room. The show runs through August 23 and features historical materials including past performance posters, programs, and stage photos illustrating the hall’s events and community role. Admission is free, and visitors can view panels and artifacts that trace the venue’s programming and changes over its 45-year history.
