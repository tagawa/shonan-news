Chigasaki City plans a commemorative project for its 80th anniversary on October 1, 2027, by applying a special one-year wrap to the community bus known as the Eboshi-go. The city has launched a public design contest for the bus livery; selected artwork will be used during the anniversary period. Details on eligibility, submission procedures, deadlines, and selection criteria will be provided by the city as part of the募集.
