Umikaze Terrace operates a shared bookshelf called Kurukuru Library in the building's first-floor community space. Anyone can freely borrow books, take them home permanently, or donate titles they have finished reading. The initiative aims to encourage reading and community sharing by allowing residents and visitors open access to the shelf.
