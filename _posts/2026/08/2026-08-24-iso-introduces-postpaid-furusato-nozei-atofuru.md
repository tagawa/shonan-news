Ōiso Town began offering a new postpaid furusato nozei donation method called 'あとふる' on July 24, the first municipality in Kanagawa Prefecture to do so. Under the system, travelers can make donations after using local services such as lodging and dining, with payments handled later rather than at the time of service. The town aims to boost local tourism and spending by making it easier for visitors to support Ōiso through donations tied to their travel experiences. The program is intended to attract more visitors and increase use of local businesses by linking donations to actual consumption during stays.
