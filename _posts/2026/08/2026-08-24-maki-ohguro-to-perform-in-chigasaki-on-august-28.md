The AUN J Classic Orchestra and Maki Ohguro will hold a special collaboration live concert titled "ＡＵＮ Ｊクラシック・オーケストラ大黒摩季スペシャルコラボライブ" on August 28 at the Chigasaki Civic Cultural Center Large Hall. Doors open at 18:00 and the performance begins at 18:30. Further details about ticketing and the full program were not included in the short notice.
