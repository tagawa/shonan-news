Ushida, president of Ryoshin Reform and known for the company's "good-faith response," continued his popular advice series with tips on water-related renovations that please grandchildren and increase comfort for homeowners. He discussed making the home comfortable when grandchildren visit during summer vacation and focused on practical, family-oriented improvements to bathrooms and kitchens. The piece emphasizes balancing safety, accessibility and enjoyment so both grandchildren and grandparents benefit from the upgrades.
