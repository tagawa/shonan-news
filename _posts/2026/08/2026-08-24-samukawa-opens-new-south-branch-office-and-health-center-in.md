Samukawa Town has completed the South Branch Office (Health Management Center) in Miyama and will begin using the facility in September. The town plans a completion ceremony and public open house on the 23rd. The center is expected to serve as a local hub for disease prevention, health maintenance, and child-rearing support. Town officials hope the integrated services will better meet residents' community health and family needs.
