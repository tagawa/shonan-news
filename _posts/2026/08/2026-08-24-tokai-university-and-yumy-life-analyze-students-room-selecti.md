Seven students from Tokai University’s media project Spinach!! presented midterm results of a housing conditions survey on July 29 at Tokai University Shonan Campus, conducted in collaboration with Yumy Life. The industry-academia joint study analyzed what students fear when selecting rooms and factors affecting their decisions. The presentation is part of ongoing research into student housing needs and will inform further investigation and collaboration between the university and Yumy Life.
