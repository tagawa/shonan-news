Umikaze Terrace will host a Feldenkrais trial session on October 4 (Sunday). The Feldenkrais Method works on the brain and nervous system and is used worldwide in education, healthcare, and the arts to improve movement, awareness, and function. Participants will experience gentle, guided movement lessons designed to increase ease and expand physical potential. The event aims to introduce local residents to the method’s practical benefits.
