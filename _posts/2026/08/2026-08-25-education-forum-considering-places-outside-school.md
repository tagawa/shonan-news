The forum, titled 教育フォーラム　学校外の居場所を考える, is aimed at sharing information adults should know to protect the rights of children who choose not to attend school or who have weakened ties to their community. It will take place on September 6 (Sunday) and is organized by a group focused on truancy and social withdrawal issues. The event intends to help caregivers, educators, and community members understand how to support these children and secure places for them outside the formal school setting.
