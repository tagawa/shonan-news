Himitsukichi Mori Gakuen, an alternative school in Horinouchi, Hayama Town, displayed materials at the town library to inform residents about its flexible curriculum based on a unique educational philosophy. The exhibit explained learning options outside formal schooling and introduced the school's programs and activities. Organizers aimed to raise awareness of alternative education choices for families and community members. The display also served as an opportunity for residents to ask questions about enrollment and the school's approach.
