A craft beer brewery that opened on Minaminaka Street in Fujisawa reached its one-year anniversary and is being noticed as a new resource for regional revitalization in the Shonan area. The brewery, operated by people who moved into the industry from other sectors, reported they feel encouraged by customer response and growing local interest. The attached restaurant has helped attract visitors and create a community hub, contributing to the area’s economic and cultural activity.
