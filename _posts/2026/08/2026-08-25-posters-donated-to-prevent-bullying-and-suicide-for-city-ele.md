The National Campaign Headquarters for the Prevention of Bullying and Suicide donated posters on August 18 to Masahiro Yoshino, the city education superintendent, to raise awareness about bullying and suicide prevention. The Hiratsuka city council faction Komei Hiratsuka, led by Masahisa Akizawa, acted as an intermediary to facilitate the donation. The posters are intended for distribution to elementary and junior high schools within the city to support preventive education and awareness efforts.
