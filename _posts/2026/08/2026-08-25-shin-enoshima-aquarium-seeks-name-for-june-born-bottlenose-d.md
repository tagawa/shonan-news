Shin-Enoshima Aquarium (Katasekaigan 2, Fujisawa) announced on August 24 that it has opened a public call for name suggestions for a baby bottlenose dolphin born in June. The aquarium is inviting the community to submit candidate names for the calf as part of its outreach efforts.
