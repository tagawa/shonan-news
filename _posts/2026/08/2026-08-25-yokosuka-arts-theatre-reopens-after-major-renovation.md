The Yokosuka Arts Theatre large hall completed a 2-year, 2-month major renovation and held an open-day event on August 22 to unveil the refurbished space. Approximately 1,900 residents who had been eagerly waiting for the reopening attended, enjoying views from the audience area toward the stage. The ceremony highlighted renewed facilities aimed at improving the audience experience and restoring local cultural offerings.
