The Zushi Children's Rakugo classroom held its presentation performance on August 23 at Zushi Bunka Plaza Sazanami Hall, starting at 3:00 p.m. with doors opening at 2:30 p.m. The program marks the fifth edition since the class began in fiscal 2019 and showcased 10 children taking the stage. The event was presented under the title "Warabe Banashiya Hare no Yose."
