The 60th Kanagawa Prefectural Junior High School Comprehensive Athletic Meet and 78th Kanagawa Prefectural Junior High School Volleyball Tournament were held on July 29 and 30 in Yokohama. Fujisawa Club, which is based in the city, reached the prefectural tournament's first-ever best four. By finishing among the top teams, Fujisawa Club earned a spot to compete in the Kanto regional tournament.
