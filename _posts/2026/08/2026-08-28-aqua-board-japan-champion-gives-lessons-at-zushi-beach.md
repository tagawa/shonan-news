Aqua board, also known as flyboard, uses jet-ski water pressure to lift riders into the air. This summer at Zushi Beach, staff giving trial instruction included the standout talent of Japan’s aqua board champion, who drew attention for her exceptional skills. The presence of a national champion helped raise interest in the sport and provided high-level coaching to participants.
