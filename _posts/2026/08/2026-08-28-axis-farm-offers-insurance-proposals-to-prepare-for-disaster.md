On Disaster Prevention Day, Axis Farm encouraged people to reassess their insurance coverage to ensure preparedness for unforeseen disasters. The Chigasaki-shi Motomachi–based company positions itself as a trusted partner that works with insurance providers to offer reliable consultation and tailored policy reviews. They recommend consulting a dependable advisor to identify gaps and strengthen protection for households and businesses.
