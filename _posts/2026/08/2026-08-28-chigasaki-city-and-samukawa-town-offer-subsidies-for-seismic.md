Chigasaki City and Samukawa Town have set up subsidy programs for seismic diagnosis and reinforcement of wooden houses to prepare for large earthquakes. In Chigasaki, eligibility for a seismic diagnosis subsidy requires the house to have been started on or before 1981-05-31 under the old seismic standards. Both municipalities aim to encourage owners to assess and strengthen older wooden homes to reduce earthquake damage risk.
