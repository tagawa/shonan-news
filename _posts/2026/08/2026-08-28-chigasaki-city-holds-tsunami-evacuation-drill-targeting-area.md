Chigasaki City held a tsunami evacuation drill on November 7 targeting neighborhoods south of the JR railway line. The exercise aimed to reduce damage by ensuring residents could evacuate safely and quickly in the event of a tsunami. Participants confirmed evacuation procedures, and city and disaster-management agencies took part to coordinate response measures.
