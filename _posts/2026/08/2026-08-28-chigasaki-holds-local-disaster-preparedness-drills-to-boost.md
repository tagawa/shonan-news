Various districts in Chigasaki City are holding disaster-preparedness drills to promote self-help—protecting oneself and family—and mutual aid—communities protecting themselves. The exercises include training and activities planned at multiple neighborhood locations across the city. The city aims to improve overall disaster resilience ahead of large-scale earthquakes by encouraging residents to practice evacuation, basic first aid, and cooperation with neighbors.
