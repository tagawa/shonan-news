Chigasaki City and three professional associations—the Chigasaki Medical Association, Chigasaki Dental Association, and Chigasaki Samukawa Pharmacists Association—have a cooperation agreement for emergency medical care and relief during large-scale disasters. The city’s website now lists member facilities and uses colored flags to indicate which services they will continue to provide in a disaster. The aim is to make it easier for residents and emergency responders to find clinics, dental offices, and pharmacies that remain open for treatment, first aid, or medication supply after a disaster.
