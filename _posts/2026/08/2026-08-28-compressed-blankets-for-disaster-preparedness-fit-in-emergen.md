When disasters cut lifelines, people often prioritize water and food and delay measures for warmth and hygiene. The article highlights a 10-piece compressed blanket pack as a reassuring item to keep on hand for cold and sanitary needs in emergencies. These compressed blankets save space in disaster backpacks while providing immediate protection against hypothermia and improving hygiene when heating and washing are unavailable.
