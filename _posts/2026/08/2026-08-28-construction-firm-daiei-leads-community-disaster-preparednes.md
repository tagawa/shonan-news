The construction company DAIEI, based in Chigasaki (円蔵２２２１), is focusing on improving regional disaster-response capabilities by preparing equipment and materials to assist with debris removal and supply transport if needed. The company says it can deploy resources to back up local efforts and has already engaged in support activities in Kumamoto. DAIEI emphasizes collaboration with municipal authorities and community organizations to enhance readiness for emergencies.
