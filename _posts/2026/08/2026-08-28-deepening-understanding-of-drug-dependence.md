The Kanagawa Prefectural Mental Health and Welfare Center is hosting a family lecture on drug dependence on September 3, from 2:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m., at the Kamakura Health and Welfare Office (Yuigahama 2-16-13). The session is aimed at family members affected by drug dependence and will cover causes, coping strategies, and available support services. Participants can expect guidance on how to handle addiction within the family and information about counseling and local resources.
