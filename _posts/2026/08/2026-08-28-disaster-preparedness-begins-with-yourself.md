September 1 is designated as "Disaster Prevention Day." In addition to large-scale events like earthquakes and tsunamis, sudden water outages and blackouts can strike at any time, so individual preparedness is crucial. The Kamakura City Comprehensive Disaster Prevention... emphasizes that personal measures are the first step in reducing harm when disasters of any size occur.
