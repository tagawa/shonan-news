FireOne is a company focused on comprehensive disaster-prevention services centered in Chigasaki City. Its representative, Yusuke Komuro, served as a firefighter for 12 years after university and has dealt with many disaster responses and prevention duties. Leveraging that experience, he now leads FireOne in conducting equipment inspections and other preventive measures to strengthen local disaster readiness.
