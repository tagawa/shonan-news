Lumine Hatano Eye Clinic is hosting a free seminar on glaucoma and macular degeneration for the general public on September 8 (Tuesday). The event will take place at Sunpearl Hatano Eye Clinic Glaucoma Clinic on the third floor of Saikaya Fujisawa. Director Hiroshi Hatano will speak at the seminar, which is open to anyone who wishes to attend.
