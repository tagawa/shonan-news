Shopping Plaza HAYAMA STATION in Hayama Town, a local tourism hub featuring direct-sale vegetables and regionally linked gourmet food, marked its 10th anniversary on September 1. The complex, located at Nagara 1583-17, has attracted about 6,000,000 visitors since opening. The facility serves as a community-oriented commercial center that highlights local produce and cuisine.
