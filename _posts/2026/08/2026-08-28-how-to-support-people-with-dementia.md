Zushi City hosted a dementia lecture on October 3 from 1:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. in the fifth-floor meeting room of Zushi City Hall. The speaker was Masahiro Shigeta, professor emeritus of Jikei University School of Medicine and Tokyo Metropolitan University, who spoke on understanding and properly supporting people with dementia.
