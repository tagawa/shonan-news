Faced with unpredictable large-scale disasters, Kamakura City has concluded multiple cooperation agreements with companies and groups to safeguard citizens’ lives and livelihoods. These agreements cover tasks such as procuring supplies, operating evacuation centers, and sharing expertise and resources during emergencies. The city aims to create a resilient network of partners that can quickly support relief operations and community recovery when a disaster strikes.
