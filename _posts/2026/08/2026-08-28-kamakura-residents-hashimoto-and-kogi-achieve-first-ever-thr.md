Anzu Hashimoto (25) and Mayuko Kogi (26), who live in Kamakura, took first place in the Pair Free event at the UNICON22 unicycle world championships held in Steyr, Austria on August 5. Their victory marked the first three-peat in the competition's history. The pair performed a routine that secured unanimous high marks from the judges, cementing their reputation in international unicycling.
