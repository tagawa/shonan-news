The Hiratsuka campus of KG High School, a support school affiliated with correspondence high schools in the Kashima Group, reached its 15th anniversary after opening recruitment in December 2011 and enrolling three students in its first year. Since then the campus has graduated a total of more than 400 students and expanded course offerings, including a broadcast course and career support tied to local companies. The school holds monthly consultation sessions for prospective new first-year students for the 2026 school year, with inquiries accepted by phone and email, and also runs free trial lessons and events to help applicants decide.
