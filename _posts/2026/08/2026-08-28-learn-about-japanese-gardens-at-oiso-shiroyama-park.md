The Japanese garden study session titled "Former Shigeru Yoshida Residence Garden" will take place September 20 (Sunday) from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at Oiso Shiroyama Park. Admission is free. As a special project for Showa 100, the program will explore the garden ideals pursued at Yoshida Shigeru’s residence.
