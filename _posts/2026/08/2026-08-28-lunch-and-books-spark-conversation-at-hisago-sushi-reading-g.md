Hisago Sushi Yokosuka-Chuo held an "oshaberi dokushokai" reading group where attendees brought a favorite book and enjoyed lunch together near Yokosuka-Chuo Station. The event was intended as a place to make friends through books, with participants discussing titles that moved them or that they wanted to recommend to others. The format combines casual conversation about books with a communal meal to encourage relaxed social interaction.
