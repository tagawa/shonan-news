The Kanagawa Prefectural Civic Hall regional project "Organ Journey Through the Prefecture in Ninomiya" takes place December 24 (Thursday) at Ninomiya Town Lifelong Learning Center Radian, a seven-minute walk from Ninomiya Station, with a 14:00 start. Performers Kuribayashi and Nakata will present a program for organ; additional program details and ticket information were included in the original announcement. The event is part of an effort to bring cultural performances to communities across the prefecture.
