A parent-child event titled "Disaster-Preparedness Night Cinema" was held at Chogo Civic Center on the 21st, aimed at families belonging to Chogo district neighborhood associations. Around 15 pairs took part, using the activity to make summer break memories while learning about coping with power outages. The program was organized for residents who are members of local neighborhood associations.
