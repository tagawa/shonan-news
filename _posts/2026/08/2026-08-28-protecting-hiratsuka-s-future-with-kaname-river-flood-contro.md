City Councilor 野崎しんや (Shinya Nozaki) published an opinion advertisement urging stronger flood control measures for the 金目川 (Kaname River) to protect Hiratsuka. He cited the July 28 "令和８年熊本地震" and the "令和８年８月千葉豪雨" that began August 13 as examples of recent severe natural disasters that highlight the need for preparedness. Nozaki argued that proactive investment and planning are essential to prevent catastrophic damage to the city in future emergencies. He called on local authorities and residents to support comprehensive flood-control projects and readiness efforts.
