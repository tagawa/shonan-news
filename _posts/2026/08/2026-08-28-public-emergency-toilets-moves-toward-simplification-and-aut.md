Toilets draw attention whenever disasters strike across Japan, and Fujisawa City has been using its website to educate residents on personal preparedness for emergency toilets. The city is examining how well evacuation centers are equipped and exploring trends toward simplifying and automating public disaster toilets to make them easier to use and maintain. Efforts aim to balance individual readiness with improved facilities at shelters to better handle future emergencies.
