Samukawa Town held a “shelter experience” event on September 5 at the Shinko Sports Samukawa Arena sub-arena (Miyayama 275) from 9:00 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. The town demonstrated its actual stockpiles and shelter layout, and also held a disaster-prevention lecture. The event aimed to help residents learn practical shelter procedures and preparedness.
