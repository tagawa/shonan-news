The event, co-hosted by Kamakura Chuo Rotary Club, which aims to return sea turtles to Kamakura’s waters, and the NPO AFMET, which supports marine and sanitation projects in Timor-Leste, gathered local residents who love the sea. The program fosters connections among participants and promotes conservation efforts, including the goal of reintroducing sea turtles to Kamakura. The article highlights community engagement and collaboration between local and international groups at the event.
