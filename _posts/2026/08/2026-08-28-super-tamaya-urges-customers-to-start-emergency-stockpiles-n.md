Super Tamaya recommends that disaster preparedness begin immediately with simple stockpiling. The store says households should prepare about one week’s worth of food and daily supplies, assuming lifelines like water and electricity could be cut. The guidance highlights that supplies must be usable when needed, implying regular checks and rotation of items to avoid expired or unusable goods.
