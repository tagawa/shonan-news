Usui Home is a community-focused real estate firm celebrating its 50th year. Its Nobi branch manager, Sugita, was born and raised in Yokosuka and has 22 years of industry experience, making him a local expert. Sugita's roots run through the area’s schools, from Kamoi Kindergarten through elementary, junior high, Iwato High School, and university, underpinning his deep local knowledge.
