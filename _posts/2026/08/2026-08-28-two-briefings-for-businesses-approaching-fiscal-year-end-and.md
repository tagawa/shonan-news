A briefing for companies with upcoming fiscal year-ends, titled '決算法人説明会,' will be held on September 10 (Thursday) at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall (茅ヶ崎市茅ヶ崎１の11の１) and on September 17 (Thursday) at Fujisawa Corporate Hall (藤沢市藤沢86). The events aim to provide guidance to businesses preparing financial closing procedures and to newly established corporations; organizers are recruiting participants. Further details such as participation requirements and how to apply were announced by the hosting organizations.
