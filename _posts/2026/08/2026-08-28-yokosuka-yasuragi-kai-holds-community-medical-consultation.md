The citizen group Yokosuka Yasuragi-kai, which works on cancer care and home-based end-of-life care, is organizing a health consultation session staffed by three of the group's physicians. On September 20 (Sunday), Dr. 澁谷泰介 will be one of the participating doctors. The event offers local residents an opportunity to consult with physicians about cancer care and issues around dying at home.
