Yuu Matsushita, 41, who lives and works in Ninomiya, Ninomiya Town, will represent Kanagawa at the Kanto Block Chambers of Commerce Youth Debate Contest in Saitama on September 15 and 16. The contest features youth chamber members presenting experiences from their activities and their feelings toward their communities. Matsushita advanced after participating in Kanagawa’s July tournament and says his dream is for everyone to be an Ultraman, reflecting his community-minded message.
