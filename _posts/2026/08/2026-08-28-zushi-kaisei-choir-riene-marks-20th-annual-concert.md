The choir, made up of current students, alumni, and parents of Zushi Kaisei Middle and High School, will present its 20th regular concert on September 26. The group has been preparing and polishing their singing, putting heartfelt effort into the program. The event showcases community ties among students, graduates, and families through choral music.
