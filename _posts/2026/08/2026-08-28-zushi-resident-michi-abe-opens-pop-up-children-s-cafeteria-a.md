Michi Abe, a Zushi City resident, organized a limited-time children's cafeteria inside the Princess Wave beach hut on Zushi Beach on August 16 and 23. The initiative provided meals and a welcoming space for children, with Abe leading the effort and local supporters helping run the event. The pop-up aimed to offer community support and a safe summertime place for children to eat and gather.
