The dialysis-specialty Chigasaki Central Clinic is a five-minute walk from Chigasaki Station South Exit and provides night dialysis starting at 6:00 PM every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The clinic staffs a dialysis specialist physician on site and supports patients who want to continue working while receiving treatment. The service aims to accommodate working patients' schedules by offering later treatment hours close to the station.
