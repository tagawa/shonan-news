Tadahiro Hasegawa, 82, and Keiko Miyaguni, 74, are former members of a group that gathered in Zushi and Hayama to publish the essay collection Magnolia. Each has now released their own essays reflecting on lives they once wrote about in that shared booklet. The pieces recount personal memories and experiences tied to their long involvement with the Magnolia publication and the local community.
