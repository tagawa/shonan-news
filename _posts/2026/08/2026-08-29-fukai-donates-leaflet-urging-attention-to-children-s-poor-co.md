Sakai-based coach and FUKAI FITNESS CLUB representative Fukai Satoshi created and donated a leaflet titled "Notice when children are unwell" to city officials and child welfare centers. Fukai, known for his "emotional disaster prevention training" that helped many athletes reach world championships and the Olympics, hopes the leaflet will help caregivers recognize early signs of physical or mental distress in children and guide them to appropriate support. The leaflet emphasizes simple steps adults can take to check on children and connect them with services when needed.
