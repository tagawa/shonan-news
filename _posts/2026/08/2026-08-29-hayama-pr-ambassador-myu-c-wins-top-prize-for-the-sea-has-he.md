The Hayama town PR ambassador, a nudibranch character named Myu-C, won the gold prize on August 7 for an entry titled "The Sea Has Heatstroke" at an event organized by the Japan Climate Leaders Partnership and others aimed at sounding the alarm on global warming. The event promoted action toward a decarbonized society and acknowledged creative efforts to raise public awareness about climate change and its impact on marine environments. The award highlights local efforts in Hayama to link community promotion with environmental advocacy.
