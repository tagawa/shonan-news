Masaya Yamagishi, a model and trainer from Chigasaki who once won the grand prix at the Mr. Japan contest, produced an event called "Muscle Beach" at Chigasaki Beach on August 29. The event showcased fitness and bodybuilding activities aimed at promoting health and local engagement. It highlighted Yamagishi's ties to his hometown and his work in fitness promotion.
