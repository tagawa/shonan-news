Shigezo Kojima joined the Oiso Lions Club in his 40s and has been active for more than 40 years, serving three terms as president. Under his leadership the club has maintained coastal murals, promoted guide dog awareness, funded Girl Scouts beach cleanups, and supported sports such as baseball and karate among many other service activities. Kojima emphasizes these projects as expressions of love for the Oiso community.
