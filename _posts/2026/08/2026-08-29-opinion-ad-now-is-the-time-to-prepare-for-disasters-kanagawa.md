Masu Shintaro expressed sympathy to those affected by the Reiwa 8 Kumamoto earthquake and the heavy rain disaster in Chiba and said these events remind us of the horror of disasters and the importance of everyday preparedness. He emphasized that during disasters human lives and daily routines are at risk, calling for renewed attention to readiness measures. The message serves as an opinion advertisement encouraging residents and authorities to strengthen disaster prevention and individual preparedness.
