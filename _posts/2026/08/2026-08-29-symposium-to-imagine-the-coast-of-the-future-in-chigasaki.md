The 5th Chigasaki Coast Grand Plan Symposium will take place on September 13 (Sunday) at Cの辺り (Naka-Kaigan 3-12986-25) from 10:00 a.m. to noon. The event is organized by the NPO Chigasaki Kaigan-zukuri Promotion Group and will bring together residents and experts to imagine and discuss plans for the future of the coastline. Attendees can expect presentations and a chance to share ideas about coastal use, conservation, and community projects.
