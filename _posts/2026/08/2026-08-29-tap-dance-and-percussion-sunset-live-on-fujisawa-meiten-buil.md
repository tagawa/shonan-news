The event, titled "フジサワ名店 夏まつり ASI-PRO ART PARK LIVE vol.5," will take place on September 5 on the rooftop of Fujisawa Meiten Building at Minami-Fujisawa 2, Fujisawa City. The show features tap dance and percussion performances as part of a summer festival series. The announcement was reported by 湘南経済新聞.
