The Zushi Chamber of Commerce and Industry is recruiting exhibitors for “Zushi Naraigoto Hakken!”, a first-time event at Zushi Arena in Ikego on November 29. The fair aims to link lesson providers, local sports groups, and community organizations with children and their families. Organizers plan hands-on demonstrations and information booths so children can discover new classes and activities in the city. Interested businesses and groups are asked to apply to participate through the chamber.
