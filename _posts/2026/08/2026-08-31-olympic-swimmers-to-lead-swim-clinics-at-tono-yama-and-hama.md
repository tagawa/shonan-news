Chigasaki City will host swim classes featuring Japanese Olympic swimming representatives at the Tono-yama Swimming Pool (とのぷー殿山水泳プール) and the Hama-suka Swimming Pool (はまぷー浜須賀水泳プール) on September 5 and 6. Classes each day begin at either 1:00 p.m. or later in the afternoon, and participants are now being recruited. The events invite swimmers of various levels to learn from elite athletes; further details on registration, age groups, fees, and exact session times are available from Chigasaki City’s pool or municipal information channels.
