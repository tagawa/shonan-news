The event is organized by “ほのぼのビーチ茅ヶ崎” (Honobono Beach Chigasaki), which has carried out cleanup activities for more than 30 years since 1990. Participants can join empty-handed and should look for the blue flag as a meeting mark. The activity runs from 9:00 to 10:00 and will be canceled in case of rain or strong winds; contact information is available on the organizer’s website.
