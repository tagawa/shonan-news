The Iicha-Icha Festival, one of the Miura Peninsula’s largest outdoor Okinawa events, takes place September 5 (Saturday) and September 6 (Sunday) in front of Yokosuka City Hall and at Ichigo Yokosuka Port Market and other nearby venues. Festival hours are 10:00–20:00, with Sunday ending at 18:00. Visitors can enjoy a lineup of Okinawan food stalls, Eisa drumming and dance, sanshin performances, and music by the U.S. Navy 7th Fleet band. For inquiries, contact 046-826-0642.
