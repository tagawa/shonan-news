The Kannon Museum at Hase-dera is holding a fall exhibition titled "郷土画家・大竹五洋と歩む観音巡礼 -深水門下の彩で巡る鎌倉三十三札所-" from August 29 to December 6. The show centers on local painter Otake Goyo and works created in the style of Shinsui’s school, presenting depictions of the Kamakura 33 Kannon pilgrimage sites together for visitors. The exhibition highlights regional artistic interpretations of the Kannon pilgrimage and offers viewers a chance to see these pilgrimage images displayed in one place.
