The traditional festival at the West and East Kanau Shrines in Uraga will be held on September 12 (eve) and September 13 (main festival). Events include kagura performances, the annual ritual ceremony, and processions of portable shrines and floats through town. The schedule lists the eve starting at 18:00 on the 12th and the ritual ceremony at 10:00 on the 13th; inquiries can be made at 046(841)0179 for the West Kanau Shrine.
