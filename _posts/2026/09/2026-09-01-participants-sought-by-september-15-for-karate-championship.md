The Chigasaki City Karate Federation is accepting entries for the 42nd Chigasaki City Karate Championship, to be held November 15 (Sunday) at Chigasaki City General Gymnasium. Eligible participants are those who live, work, or study in Chigasaki City. The entry deadline is September 15, and interested people should contact the federation for registration details.
