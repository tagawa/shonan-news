Ten chorus circles from Samukawa will present their practiced singing and harmonies at the Samukawa Town Citizens' Center Hall. The event opens at 12:30 with a 13:00 start, and admission is free with admission tickets currently being distributed; same-day tickets will also be available. For inquiries, contact Samukawa Town Citizens' Center at 0467（74）2333.
