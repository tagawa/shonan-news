The first Enoshima Taiko Festival will feature four groups, mainly performers in their 20s, delivering powerful wadaiko drumming. The event includes an open taiko jam session that anyone can join and a bon odori dance. It runs from 12:00 to 17:00 at サムエル・コッキング苑 and is free; the festival will be canceled in case of rain or storm.
