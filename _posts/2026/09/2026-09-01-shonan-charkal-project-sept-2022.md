The event, promoting local production for local consumption, SDGs, and disaster-affected area support under the coined name “Charkal” (future + local), runs from 9:00 to 15:00 and will be canceled in case of rain. This month’s program features the first-ever appearance of the mobile Wajima morning market. For inquiries, contact the organizing committee at 0466-22-8720.
