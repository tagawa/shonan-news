SHONAN STREET JAM takes place on September 27 from 10:00 to 17:00 at Chigasaki Central Park (Daiichi Cutter Kiiro Park). The event brings together about 30 American cars, 20 dance teams, and multiple kitchen cars, and includes accessory workshops, a festival-style fair, and alcohol service. Admission is free, the event will proceed in light rain and is canceled in heavy rain.
