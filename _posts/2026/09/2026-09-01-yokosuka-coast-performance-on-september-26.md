The event titled "Umikaze Festa × Yokosuka Coast Performance" is scheduled for September 26 (Saturday) from 10:00 to 16:00 at the Yokosuka Kaigandori event plaza. Attendees can enjoy dance and band performances while browsing a flea market and sampling local gourmet foods. The event will be canceled in case of rain, and inquiries can be directed to the Yokosuka City Planning and Coordination Division at 046（822）8309.
