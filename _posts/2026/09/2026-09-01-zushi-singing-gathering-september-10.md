The event runs from 10:30 to 11:30 with doors opening at 10:00. Attendees will hear Schubert's "Ave Maria" and Puccini's "O mio babbino caro" and join in singing children's songs and popular tunes. Admission is 500 yen, all seats unreserved, and children from 0 to preschool age attend free; wheelchair users are welcome. For inquiries contact utatsudoi.zushi@gmail.com.
