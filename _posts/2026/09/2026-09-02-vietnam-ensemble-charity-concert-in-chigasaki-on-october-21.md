The event, titled "Vietnam Ensemble Charity Concert IN Chigasaki 2026," will take place on Wednesday, October 21, 2026, at the Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall small hall. It is organized by the event's executive committee and is scheduled to coincide with the International Day of Peace. The concert is presented as a Japan–Vietnam friendship charity performance, with proceeds likely intended to support related causes.
