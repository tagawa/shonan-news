Aloseia Shonan Middle and High School (茅ヶ崎市富士見町５の２) will host its cultural festival, called the 'Peace Festival,' on Saturday, September 12. The event runs from 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., with last admission at 2:30 p.m., and is open to the public. The school plans various student-led exhibits and performances as this year’s theme; visitors should arrive before the final admission time.
