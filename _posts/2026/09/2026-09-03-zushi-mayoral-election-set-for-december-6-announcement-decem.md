The Zushi City Election Management Committee decided the mayoral election to fill the post at the end of the current term will be announced on December 6 (Sunday) and voted on December 13 (Sunday). The number of registered voters as of September 1 is 49,034. The schedule follows the timetable required for elections at the end of a term; further candidate and campaign details will be released closer to the announcement date.
