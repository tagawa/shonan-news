Alexander カイ, a 15-year-old resident of Chigasaki City, was chosen as a member of Kanagawa Prefecture's boys' basketball team for the National Sports Festival. He plays on the U18 team of B.League club Yokohama B-Corsairs and is considered part of the group of favorites for the tournament. The selection marks another step forward in his progression toward competing on the sport's bigger stages.
