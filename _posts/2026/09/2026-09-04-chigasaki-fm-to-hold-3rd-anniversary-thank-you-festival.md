Chigasaki FM (89.2 MHz) is celebrating its third anniversary with an event titled "Thank-You Festival 3rd Anniversary — Talk, Laugh, Sing" at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall on October 4. The program will include comedy and music live performances along with talk segments, and the station invites listeners to join the festivities. Further details about performers and ticketing were not provided in the notice.
