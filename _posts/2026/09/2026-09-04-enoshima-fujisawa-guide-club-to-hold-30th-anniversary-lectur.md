The Enoshima-Fujisawa Guide Club, chaired by Shimizu Atsuko and working with the Fujisawa City Tourism Association to promote attractions in Fujisawa and neighboring municipalities, is marking its 30th anniversary. The club will hold a commemorative lecture on the 10th (Thursday) at the Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce Hall Minapa. The event celebrates the group's three decades of guiding and publicizing local attractions.
