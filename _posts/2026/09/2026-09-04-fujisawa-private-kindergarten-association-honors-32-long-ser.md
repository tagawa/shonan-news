The nonprofit Fujisawa City Private Kindergarten Association, led by Chairman Kiyoshi Ikeda, held the 41st Fujisawa City Kindergarten Convention on the 26th at the Shonandai Cultural Center Civic Theater. Staff from member kindergartens and locally elected assembly members attended, and the association presented awards to 32 employees for long service. The event included speeches and activities highlighting early childhood education in the city.
