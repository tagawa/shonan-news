The exhibition titled “Gourd Exhibition: The Primal Vessel of Humankind — Gourd Containers from Around the World” is being held at the Kanagawa Prefectural Ofuna Flower Center (Okamoto 1018). The show runs through September 27 and is located in the main building's second-floor Flower Hall. The display brings together a variety of gourd containers from different countries to illustrate their cultural and functional uses.
