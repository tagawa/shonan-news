Kamakura City opened its September regular assembly on September 2 and submitted 23 bills, including a supplemental budget proposal, plus three reports. The general account supplemental budget is ¥12,741,100, bringing the amended general account total to ¥864,000,000 (figure truncated in original). The proposals will be considered during the September city council session.
