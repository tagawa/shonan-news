The Kamakura Chamber of Commerce Youth Division (Kamakura YEG) is staging Oktoberfest Kamakura 2026, a charity beer and food event, on October 3 (Saturday) from 10:00 to 20:00 and October 4 (Sunday) at the Kamakura City Hall parking lot. The two-day festival aims to connect people and the city through toasts, with proceeds supporting local causes. Attendees can expect a variety of beers and food offerings and community-oriented activities during the event.
