Mutsumi Sato, 59, a Tokyo resident, has taken office as the 38th director of the Kamakura Tax Office, responsible for Kamakura City, Zushi City and Miura District (Hayama Town). Sato served last year as a tax office director in Yamanashi and has worked for many years as a National Tax Agency official. He said this is his first assignment to a coastal area and emphasized the principle of not causing losses to the right people.
