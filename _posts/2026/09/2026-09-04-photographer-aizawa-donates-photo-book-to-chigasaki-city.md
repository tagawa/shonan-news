Aizawa Minoru, who runs Aizawa Photo Studio in Matsunami, Chigasaki City, donated his photo collection titled 『日本の文化を創りし人々』 to the city. The book highlights distinguished artists and cultural figures, reflecting Aizawa's work to honor and support outstanding creators. The donation was made to help the city preserve and share portraits that reveal the personal sides of cultural contributors.
