Takashi Tsuneki discovered the game of go in fifth grade when the anime Hikaru no Go was popular, and his grandmother encouraged him to attend a local classroom. His exceptional concentration allowed his talent to flourish, and after entering junior high he pursued go professionally. Now having reached the pinnacle of his career, he has returned to his hometown.
