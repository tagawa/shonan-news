Gin-no-Suzu Co.'s Manyono Flower Salon (鎌倉市佐助１の18の21) will offer a Zentangle(R) course starting in October. The class is described as easy pattern art anyone can enjoy with just a pen or pencil and paper, regardless of drawing ability or age. The program consists of multiple sessions and enrollment is limited to the first six people to apply.
