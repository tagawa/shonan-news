The registered group 'Center Street Flower Group,' part of the beautification network 'Bika Net Fujisawa,' was awarded by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism for long-term road cleaning and contributions to maintaining and improving local scenery. The group's members visited the mayor on August 25; this marks the sixth civic organization in the city to receive such national recognition. The award highlights sustained volunteer efforts that keep Center Street and surrounding areas clean and attractive.
