A disaster volunteer center, which supports identifying survivors' needs and coordinating volunteer activities, is being prepared in the Kugenuma district. Typically run by municipal government, social welfare councils, and NPOs, the center aims to operate at the community level during emergencies. Local residents are building capacity in peacetime so they can quickly assess needs, organize volunteers, and work with official agencies after a disaster. The effort emphasizes cooperation among neighbors and local organizations to ensure a faster, more tailored response when crises occur.
