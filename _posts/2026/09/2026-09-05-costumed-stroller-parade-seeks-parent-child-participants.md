A parade called the "Stroller & Kids Grand March" is scheduled for the 27th (Sunday) on Yanagi Street at the north exit of Fujisawa Station as part of this month’s Fujisawa Citizens Festival. The festival’s executive committee is currently inviting parent-child teams to join the costumed parade. Organizers provided entry details and may set participation limits, asking interested families to register in advance.
