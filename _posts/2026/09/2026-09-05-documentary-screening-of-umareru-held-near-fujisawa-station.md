The documentary film "Umareru" will be screened on September 11 and 12 at the rental space Win-d Plus (ウィンドプラス) at Fujisawa Station North Exit in Fujisawa City. The event offers a chance for attendees to watch the film and discuss themes about life. The announcement was reported by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
