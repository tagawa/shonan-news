The national high school swimming championships (Inter-High) were held last month in Kusatsu, Shiga Prefecture, where Nihon University Fujisawa High School (Kameino) captured first place in five boys' and girls' events. The team's swimmers performed strongly in both individual races and relays, contributing to outstanding results across the program. Coaches and school officials praised the athletes' depth and preparation that produced victories in both individual and team competitions.
