After about ten months of renovation, the Yokosuka Museum of Art (横須賀美術館, Yokosuka-shi Kamoi 4-1) reopened on September 5. The museum's first exhibition since reopening features the work of the founding director, a Western-style painter whose death marked its tenth anniversary. The reopening event and exhibition emphasize the museum's renewed facilities and its continued focus on regional art and cultural heritage.
