The professional and amateur longboard event "宝ビスケット×NKテクノ 鵠沼プロアマオープン2026" takes place on September 12 and 13 at Kugenuma Beach bathing area in Fujisawa City, Kugenuma Kaigan 3. The contest brings together pro and amateur riders and coordinates with local businesses and beach huts. The event is reported by 湘南経済新聞.
