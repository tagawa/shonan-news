The Fujisawa-juku All-Japan Amateur Rakugo Festival Committee is staging two rakugo performances in succession. On September 12 (Saturday) the 10th Sansan Yose will be held at Mitofuji Neighborhood Hall, Fujisawa 966. On September 19 (Saturday) another event, the 22nd Rokumon Yose, will take place at the Fujisawa Civic Hall Main Hall, with experienced amateur performers taking part in both programs.
