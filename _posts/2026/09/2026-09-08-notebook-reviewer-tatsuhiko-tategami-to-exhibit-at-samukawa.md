Notebook critic Tatsuhiko Tategami, who is from Chigasaki City, will set up a booth called "Oboeteiru Techou" at the Samukawa Orange Festa, a dementia awareness event held in conjunction with September's dementia month. The event takes place in the sub-arena of Shinko Sports Samukawa Arena (Samukawa General Gymnasium) in Miyama, Samukawa Town. The festival is part of annual efforts to raise public awareness about dementia.
