The Tsujido Morning Breakfast Market, where Fujisawa farmers sell seasonal vegetables, processed goods and serve breakfast, will be held on September 13 at Tsujido Kindai Park (Seekros Park) in Tsujido Kindai 1. Fujisawa-born calligrapher and performance artist Risako Arai will present a live shodo performance during the event.
