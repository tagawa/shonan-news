A成果 sharing meeting was held at ひらつか市民活動センター on August 28 to review collaborative projects and other activities. Presentations were made by citizen activity groups and city officials who discussed how they complemented each other’s strengths and weaknesses. The event focused on reflecting on outcomes of joint initiatives and exchanging lessons learned to improve future cooperation.
