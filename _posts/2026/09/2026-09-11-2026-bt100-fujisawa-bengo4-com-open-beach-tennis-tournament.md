The international beach tennis tournament called 2026 BT100 FUJISAWA Bengo4.com Open will be held from September 25 to 27 at Masa Kugenuma on Kugenuma Beach in Fujisawa. The event is organized locally and will feature international competitors in beach tennis matches across the three days. Spectators can attend the tournament at the beach venue in Fujisawa, and organizers are preparing facilities and schedules for the competition.
