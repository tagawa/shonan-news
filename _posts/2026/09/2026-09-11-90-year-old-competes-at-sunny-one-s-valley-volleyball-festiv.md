The festival was organized by the Chigasaki Volleyball Association, chaired by Osamu Oguri, and sponsored by Inaoka Housing. The event drew teams across age groups and noted a 90-year-old participant as the tournament's oldest competitor. Activities included friendly matches and community engagement promoting lifelong sports participation.
