The event, ONSEN・ガストロノミーウォーキング in 藤沢, is a guided walking tour that combines tasting local ingredients and enjoying scenic views. This year's route will tour the northern part of the city and involves Keio University Shonan- (慶応義塾大学湘南…) as an organizer or partner. Organizers are accepting participants for the September 20 Sunday event.
