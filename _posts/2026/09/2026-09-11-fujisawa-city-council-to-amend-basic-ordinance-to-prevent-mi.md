On September 3, the Fujisawa City Council held a full-members meeting and resolved to propose a partial revision of the Fujisawa City Council Basic Ordinance at the September regular session. The amendment will explicitly state respect for human rights and require information release to be based on facts, aiming to prevent the spread of misinformation and personal attacks. The change is intended to set clearer normative standards for council operations and communications.
