Members of the Fujisawa Community Musical, based in the city, held a rehearsal on the 5th at the Shonandai Culture Center Civic Theater as they prepared for their 12th performance scheduled for the 20th (Sunday) of this month. The group, which marked roughly three years since an unstated milestone, focused on final polishing for the upcoming production. Local supporters and organizers emphasized the show as a significant stage in the troupe’s ongoing activities.
