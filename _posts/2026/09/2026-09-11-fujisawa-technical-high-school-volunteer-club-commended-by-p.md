Fujisawa Technical High School's volunteer club runs a program called "cybercrime prevention volunteer" targeting elementary school students in the prefecture. The club's efforts to raise public awareness of norms and improve response skills were recognized by the prefectural police cyber unit, which presented an award. The activities focus on educating young children about safe online behavior and how to handle cyber risks. The commendation highlights the school's contribution to community safety and cybercrime prevention education.
