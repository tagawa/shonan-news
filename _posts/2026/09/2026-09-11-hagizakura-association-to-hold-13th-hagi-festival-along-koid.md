The Hagizakura Association will hold the "第13回萩祭り" on September 19 (Saturday) at Mukoda Ryokuchi, about 50 meters downstream from the Koide River Hagien Bridge. The event runs from 9:30 a.m. to around 2:00 p.m., and will be postponed to Sunday the 20th in case of rain. Attendees are invited to enjoy a day of autumn activities along the river.
