The Hayama Defense Association, led by Chairman Takeo Tachikawa, held a visit to the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force submarine Seiryū at Yokosuka Naval Base as part of its unit training program. The association organizes such activities to support Self-Defense Force operations and promote local exchange. Participants were given a guided inspection of the submarine and briefed on its capabilities and missions. The event aimed to deepen civilian understanding of maritime defense and strengthen ties between the community and the MSDF.
