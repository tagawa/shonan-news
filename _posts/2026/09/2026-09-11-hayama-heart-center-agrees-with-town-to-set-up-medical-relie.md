Medical corporation Tokushukai's Hayama Heart Center and Hayama Town concluded a disaster agreement on September 3 that includes installing a medical relief post on the center's premises during emergencies. The pact outlines roles and cooperation for disaster response between the hospital and the town. The agreement is intended to improve local medical support capacity when disasters occur.
