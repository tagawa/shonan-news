Hiratsuka Tennis Association, led by chairman Tsutomu Nakata, is recruiting middle-aged and senior players for the 16th Senior One-Coin Tennis Festa on October 15 at Hiratsuka Municipal Momohama Tennis Courts (Momohama-cho 34-18). The one-day doubles event uses a one-coin entry fee format and is aimed at promoting recreational tennis among older players; interested participants should contact the association for entry details and categories.
