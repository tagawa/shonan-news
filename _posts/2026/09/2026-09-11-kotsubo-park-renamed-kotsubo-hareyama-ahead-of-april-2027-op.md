The park formerly nicknamed "Hageyama" has been officially named "Kotsubo Hareyama Park" by Zushi City as it prepares to open in April 2027. The city announced the new name at its September 1 regular press conference and is continuing site preparations for the planned opening. The name change reflects a move away from the old nickname while retaining recognition of the local Kotsubo area.
