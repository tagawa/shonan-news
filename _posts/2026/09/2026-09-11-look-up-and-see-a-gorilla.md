Walking through the Sun Pearl Plaza at Fujisawa Station North Exit, visitors felt watched and discovered a striking giant black gorilla statue on a building rooftop. The unusual sculpture, shown on September 10 during an outdoor event, depicts the gorilla holding money boxes in both hands and draws attention from passersby.
