An event inviting professional players from sports such as soccer to serve as instructors will take place at Yokosuka Stadium (夏島町２の２) on Friday, October 23. The program uses the normally restricted stadium to offer hands-on sports practice for local participants. Event details including schedule, target age groups, and registration procedures were not included in the brief notice.
