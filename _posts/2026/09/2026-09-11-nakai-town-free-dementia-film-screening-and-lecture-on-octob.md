The Nakai Town Council of Social Welfare is hosting a dementia lecture and free film screening on October 3 (Saturday) from 13:30 to 15:30 at the Town Rural Environment Improvement Center. Attendance is limited to Nakai Town residents, with a capacity of 50 people accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. The event aims to raise awareness about dementia; additional participation details and registration procedures are provided by the organizer.
