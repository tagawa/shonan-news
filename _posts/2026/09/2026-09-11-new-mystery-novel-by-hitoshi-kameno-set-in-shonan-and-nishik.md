Hitoshi Kameno, 53, winner of the Bunko Grand Prix at the 19th 'This Mystery Is Amazing!' awards hosted by Takarajimasha, has published a new book titled 『幽世までの寄り道　介護施設・灯影庵の謎日誌』. The novel is set in locations including the Shonan shoreline and Nishikamakura and follows mysteries connected to a nursing-care facility called Tomokage-an. The release continues Kameno's focus on local settings and social themes that earned him literary recognition.
