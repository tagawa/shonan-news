Umikaze Terrace will host an event on October 24 (Saturday) where participants can learn beautiful posture and correct walking techniques from a walking specialist. The instructor is a Precious Walking(R) instructor. The session aims to teach practical posture and gait habits to improve everyday walking and appearance.
