The Oiso High School alumni association will hold its general meeting on Saturday, September 19, at the school, beginning at 3:00 p.m., and organizers are currently accepting registrations. Oiso High School will mark its 100th anniversary in 2027, and the alumni meeting will take place on the same day as the school's cultural festival.
