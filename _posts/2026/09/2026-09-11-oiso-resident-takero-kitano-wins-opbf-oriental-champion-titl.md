Takero Kitano, who lives in Oiso and trains with Ohashi Boxing Gym, defeated Elson Trinidad on August 19 at Korakuen Hall to capture the OPBF Oriental Pacific mini-flyweight championship. The victory fulfilled a long-held ambition for Kitano, marking his first major regional title. The win was celebrated locally in Oiso, where Kitano is a well-known figure.
