Local governments and shopping street associations in Yokosuka and Miura sold premium shopping vouchers this autumn to stimulate regional consumption. The vouchers provided a 30% bonus on the purchase price and were distributed sequentially across various shopping districts, with information compiled for each area. The program included initiatives such as those by the Oppama Shopping Street Association and other local merchant groups to encourage spending at neighborhood shops.
