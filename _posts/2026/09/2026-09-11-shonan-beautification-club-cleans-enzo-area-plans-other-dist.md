The volunteer group Shonan Beautification Club, led by Aya Naga Ogawa, conducted a cleanup of the Enzo neighborhood in Chigasaki City on August 30. The activity, coordinated with the Enzo residents' association, focused on removing litter and improving local appearance. The group said it plans to carry out similar cleanups in other neighborhoods across Chigasaki City going forward.
