The Yokosuka Arts Theater reopened after renovations on September 1 and hosted the annual US-Japan music festival on September 13. The event, which began in 1995, celebrates the city’s unique relationship with nearby U.S. military bases by featuring musical performances from both Japanese and American artists. The festival continues a tradition specific to Yokosuka that highlights cultural exchange through music.
