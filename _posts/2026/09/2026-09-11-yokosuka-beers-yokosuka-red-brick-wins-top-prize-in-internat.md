Yokosuka Beer, based in Otakicho, Yokosuka City, saw its craft beer Yokosuka Red Brick earn the highest prize in its category at an international beer contest held in the United Kingdom. The award was presented in the competition’s category rankings, bringing international recognition to the brewery’s product. The win highlights Yokosuka Beer’s brewing quality and raises the profile of local craft beer on the global stage.
