The Asano siblings from Hayama Town's Isshiki have continued a strong run in wrestling, earning excellent results at several competitions. On August 26, eldest son 稜悟 (Ryogo), a third-year student at Chuo University, and second son 称志 (Noriyuki), also at Chuo, posted notable performances, while their younger sister also achieved a good result. The family's success has drawn attention locally as all three siblings maintain high competitive standards.
