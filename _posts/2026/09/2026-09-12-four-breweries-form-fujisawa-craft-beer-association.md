The Fujisawa City Craft Beer Brewers Association was established on the 1st of this month by four local breweries to energize Fujisawa through craft beer. Makoto Oura, a member of the association, says people now associate craft beer with Fujisawa and the group aims to raise the city’s profile and attract visitors. The association plans cooperative activities and events to promote local breweries and the craft beer scene in Fujisawa.
