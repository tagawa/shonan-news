A group called Fujisawa 16mm Audiovisual Volunteers intentionally continues running 16mm film projectors, valuing the tactile sounds and visual qualities that differ from smartphone video. They hold screenings and hands-on events aimed at children to convey the excitement and communal feeling of seeing film projected. The volunteers say preserving these screenings helps transmit the medium’s unique aesthetic and the emotional impact of shared film-watching to future generations.
