Fujisawa City’s Shoyo Junior High School (Tsujido Higashikaigan) is marking its 70th anniversary and has started soliciting donations through crowdfunding to fund repairs to the school buildings and gymnasium. The target goal for the campaign is ¥5,000,000. School officials are asking alumni, parents and the local community to cooperate to preserve the school facilities for future students. The project is being promoted as a commemorative initiative tied to the anniversary celebrations.
