The Enoshima-Fujisawa Guide Club marked its 30th year since founding in May with a ceremony on the 10th at the Minapark Commerce and Industry Hall sixth-floor multipurpose hall. The event was attended by Mayor Tsuneo Suzuki and representatives of the city tourism association. Club members reflected on three decades of guiding and discussed continuing efforts to promote local tourism. Organizers plan further activities to celebrate the milestone.
