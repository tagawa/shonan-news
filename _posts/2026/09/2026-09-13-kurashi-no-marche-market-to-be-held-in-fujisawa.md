Producers and creators active in the Shonan area will sell food and miscellaneous goods at the Kurashi no Marche. The event is scheduled for September 19 at the hands-on log house and wooden home exhibition space LOGWAY BESS 藤沢 in Ōba, Fujisawa City. The market will feature local food producers and craft creators offering their products to visitors.
