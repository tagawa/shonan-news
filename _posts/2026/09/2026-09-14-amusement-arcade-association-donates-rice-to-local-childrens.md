The donation was made by Hiratsuka Yūgijō Kumiai, led by chairman 荻野将夫, as part of its community contribution activities. Each of the 20 children’s cafeterias operating in Hiratsuka received 10kg of rice. The contribution was coordinated with Hiratsuka City social services to support local child welfare efforts.
