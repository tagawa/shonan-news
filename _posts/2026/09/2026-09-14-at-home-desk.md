The publication announced it has reached issue number 2,000, marking roughly 39 years since its founding in October 1987. The editor noted a personal connection, having been born one month before the paper's first issue, which made the paper feel especially close. The column expressed renewed gratitude to readers, sponsors, and news sources for their continued support.
