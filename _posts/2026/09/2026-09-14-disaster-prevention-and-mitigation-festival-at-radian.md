The festival will take place at Ninomiya Town Lifelong Learning Center Radian on September 22. Part one runs from 10:00 to 13:00 and includes exhibits and courses; a public lecture for residents is scheduled from 14:00 to 16:00. The event focuses on community preparedness through hands-on displays and educational sessions. Attendance details and any participation requirements were not specified in the brief notice.
