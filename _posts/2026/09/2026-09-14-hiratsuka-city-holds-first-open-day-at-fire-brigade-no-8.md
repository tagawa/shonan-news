Hiratsuka City Fire Brigade No. 8 (Nesakama, led by Captain Seiichi Makabe) held its first-ever open day on August 23 to raise public interest in brigade activities. Thirteen local households attended, where residents could try riding in fire engines and observe demonstrations and explanations of the brigade's work.
