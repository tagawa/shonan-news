The event will take place at the log-house and wooden-home experiential exhibition LOGWAY BESS Fujisawa (大庭5224の1) on Saturday the 19th. Seven local Shonan producers and vendors will gather to sell goods and interact with visitors at the market. The article notes the event runs in the morning, providing an opportunity to experience local products in the exhibition venue.
