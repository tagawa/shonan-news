The Tamana Castle Site Community Council is recruiting attendees for the 'Tamana Castle Site Curator Training Course' focused on the site's history and conserving its historic green spaces. Sessions will be held once a month from October through March, 9:00 a.m. to noon, and are open to anyone. Enrollment is limited to the first 15 applicants, and interested people are encouraged to apply early.
