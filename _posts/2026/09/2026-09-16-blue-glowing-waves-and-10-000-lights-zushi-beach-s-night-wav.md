The 'NIGHT WAVE Light Wave Project' will be held on October 10 (Saturday) and October 11 (Sunday) at Zushi Beach, creating a fantastical atmosphere with blue-white glowing waves along the shoreline. The installation includes about 10,000 individually lit lights and other illuminated elements designed to evoke waves and sea light. Organizers say the event aims to offer visitors a contemplative, artistic evening by the water, with opportunities to walk along the beach and view the synchronized lighting effects.
