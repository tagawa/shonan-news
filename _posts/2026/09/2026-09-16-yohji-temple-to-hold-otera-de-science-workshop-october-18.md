Yohōji Temple ('要法寺') in Hiratsuka City will host an autumn workshop called 'Otera de Science' on October 18, running from 10:00 to 12:00. Participants will make a PET bottle microscope using glass beads and learn how to observe tiny objects, with hands-on demonstrations and guidance. The event is aimed at children and families interested in science; participants may need to bring certain materials or pay a small fee, so organizers recommend checking details in advance.
