Kanagawa Prefecture released its 2026 report on residents aged 100 and over on September 15. The report identified the oldest person in the prefecture as a 112-year-old woman who lives in Hayama Town. The announcement covers the situation of centenarians within the prefecture for fiscal year 2026.
