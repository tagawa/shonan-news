The second ‘Back Cover Poem Workshop’ will take place on September 19 and 20 at Yurindo Fujisawa store in Minami-Fujisawa and in the Book Theater inside the Fujisawa Meiten Building. Participants will tour the store and use an app to create poems inspired by book back covers and displays. The event is organized by the bookstore and reported by Shonan Keizai Shimbun.
