The event takes place at 寒川町民センターホール (宮山１６５) on September 23 (Wednesday, public holiday), with doors opening at 1:00 p.m., start at 1:30 p.m., and scheduled to end at 3:00 p.m. Admission is free. The lecture's theme is about global awareness and the future of the town, and it is presented as a 'cultural lecture' for residents interested in community and international perspectives.
