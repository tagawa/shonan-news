The event, organized by the Zushi Chamber of Commerce Women's Division under chairperson Moritani Noriko, featured products and services from women active in Zushi. Stalls offered local crafts, food and health-related goods, and the market aimed to showcase the vitality and entrepreneurship of local women. Activities included hands-on workshops and opportunities for visitors to meet the stall operators, encouraging community engagement and support for female-run businesses.
