The Samukawa Town Tourism Association is holding the 'Autumn Equinox Samukawa Diamond Fuji Week' through 26 September, offering views where the setting sun appears to sit on Mount Fuji's summit. This seasonal attraction is a local autumn tradition unique to the town, drawing visitors to experience the striking sunset alignment. The event runs until 26 September and includes designated viewing areas and related activities organized by the association.
