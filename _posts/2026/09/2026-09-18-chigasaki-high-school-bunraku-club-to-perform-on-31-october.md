The performance 'Bunraku e no Izanai' by Kanagawa Prefectural Chigasaki High School's Bunraku Club is scheduled for 31 October, a Saturday, from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., with doors opening at 1:30 p.m., in the basement multipurpose room of Umikaze Terrace. Admission is free and available to the first 150 attendees. The event offers an introduction to bunraku, the traditional Japanese puppet theatre, presented by students of the school's club. Attendees should plan to arrive early due to the limited first-come seating.
