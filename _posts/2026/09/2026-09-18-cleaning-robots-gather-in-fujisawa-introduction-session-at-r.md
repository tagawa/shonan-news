A showcase called 'お掃除ロボット大集合' will bring together the latest commercial cleaning robots on 9 October at the robot business hub Robolink, located at Fujisawa 559. The event runs from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. and offers free admission. The session is aimed at businesses and organizations interested in robot deployment and will include explanations about introducing the machines into operations.
