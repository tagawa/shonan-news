The 'Oku Chiga Craft Market' will take place from 10 October through 12 October at Irohani Woodwork, located at 1296-1 Serizawa, Chigasaki City, in the northern part of Chigasaki. The market opens each day at 11:00 a.m. and closes at 4:00 p.m., showcasing handmade goods and woodworking by local artisans. Visitors can meet craftsmen and see traditional handwork up close in a community-oriented setting. Admission details and any additional programming were not specified in the source.
