An event called 'the fifth Shonan Shuwa Market' will take place on 23 September at the plaza in front of Chigasaki City Hall, aiming to build a bridge between deaf and hearing people through sign language. The market runs from 11:00 in the morning to 17:00 in the afternoon and will feature many stalls. Organizers plan activities and interactions to promote mutual understanding and communication using sign language. Admission information and specific stall details were not provided in the brief notice.
