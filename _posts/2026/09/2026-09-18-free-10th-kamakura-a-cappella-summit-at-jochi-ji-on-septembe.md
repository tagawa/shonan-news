The 10th Kamakura A Cappella Summit is scheduled for 26 September at Jochi-ji, located at Yamanouchi 1402, with free admission. The concert will begin at 11:00 and will proceed in rain but be canceled only in severe weather. Ten groups that passed a prior screening are set to perform. Organizers emphasize that entry is free and spectators should check weather conditions on the day.
