Six-year-old Yamada Shiisa, a first-grade student at Kamakura Municipal Fukasawa Elementary School, placed third nationally in the violin division for first graders at the 51st All-Japan Junior Classical Music Competition held in Urayasu, Chiba Prefecture, on 11 September. Yamada said she enjoyed performing on the big stage and delivered an enthusiastic performance that impressed judges and audience alike. School staff and family praised her hard work and preparation leading up to the competition. The result highlights her early promise as a young musician and drew local congratulations.
