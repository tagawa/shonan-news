The summit, organized by the NPO 'Umibe Tsukuri Kenkyukai', brings together student teams from 18 schools nationwide to present their research on coastal ecosystem restoration. It takes place at the Kanagawa Prefectural College of Health and Welfare auditorium on 21 September and 22 September. The event highlights youth-led conservation efforts and aims to share practical approaches and findings among schools and community groups.
