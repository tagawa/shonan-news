A female idol group named 'Jukkenzaka 66' formed in January 2026 with all members in their 50s and 60s, based in Chigasaki City. The group aims to win fans across generations and to encourage viewers, describing their activity as a 'second youth.' They perform locally and plan events that showcase energetic performances while challenging age stereotypes in idol culture.
