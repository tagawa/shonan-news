The 'Kugenoyoi Machi-ichi' night market will be held on 19 September at the Kugenuma Beach shopping street. This is the sixth time the event has been staged, and organizers say it has attracted more than 20,000 visitors in past years. The market is a nighttime shopping-street event, and since last year the organizers have continued measures to cope with heat.
