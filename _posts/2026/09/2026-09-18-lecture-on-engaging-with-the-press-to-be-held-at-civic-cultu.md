A lecture focusing on media topics will take place on 26 September at Chigasaki Civic Cultural Hall from 1:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. Admission is free. The talk will address 'spin reporting', the manipulation of information, and will be delivered by a former news professional. The event aims to give attendees practical guidance on how to understand and interact with contemporary media coverage.
