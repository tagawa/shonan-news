A pre-candidacy briefing for the Nakai mayoral election, which will be officially announced on 20 October with voting on 25 October, was held on 8 September at Nakai Town Hall. Two campaigns attended the session, suggesting the race may become a direct contest between the incumbent and a challenger. The briefing was organized by election officials to explain procedures and requirements for prospective candidates ahead of the vote.
