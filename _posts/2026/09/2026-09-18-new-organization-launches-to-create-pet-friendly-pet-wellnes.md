The nonprofit Pet Life Innovation Organization will be established on 1 October to pursue a 'Pet Wellness City' where people and pets live healthily and comfortably together. Earlier, on 9 September, Nihon University College of Bioresource Sciences hosted related events or discussions, indicating academic engagement with the initiative. The new organization aims to coordinate efforts across research, policy, and community planning to build a more inclusive coexisting society for humans and their pets. Organizers say the group will focus on practical innovations to improve everyday life for both pets and owners.
