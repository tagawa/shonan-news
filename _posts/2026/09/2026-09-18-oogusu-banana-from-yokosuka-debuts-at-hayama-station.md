On 17 September, the Shopping Plaza HAYAMA STATION in Hayama began selling 'Oogusu Banana', the first appearance of the fruit at the tourist facility. The bananas were grown without pesticides by a local ceramicist who is known as a 'banana-loving potter' and markets the carefully tended produce under the name 'Oogusu Banana'. Visitors to the plaza found the locally grown bananas offered alongside other regional products, highlighting a rare local specialty from Yokosuka.
