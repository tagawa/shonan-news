The service-equipped senior residence 'With Living Heian Kameino' at Kameino 3286 is holding a two-day open house on 19 September and 20 September. The event includes food tasting and is by advance reservation, starting at 10:00 each day. The facility features on-site care helpers and a day-service, offering an安心 (safe and supportive) living option for seniors. Visitors are asked to reserve in advance for the scheduled time slots.
