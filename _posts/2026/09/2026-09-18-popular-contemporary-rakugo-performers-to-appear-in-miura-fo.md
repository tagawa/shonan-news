Miura Chamber of Commerce and Industry will mark its 70th anniversary with a yose variety-stage event called 'Miura Performance' on 17 October at Urari Civic Hall in Misaki, Miura City. The program will feature popular contemporary rakugo storytellers including 'Katsura Beisuke' and 'Hayashiya Sanpei'. The event is presented as a commemorative public performance to celebrate the chamber's founding milestone and offer traditional comic storytelling to the local community.
