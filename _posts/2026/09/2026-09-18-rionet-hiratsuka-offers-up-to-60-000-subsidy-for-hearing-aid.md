Certified hearing-aid specialist Kouta Yamamoto and staff at Rionet Center Hiratsuka (Nishiki-machi) are encouraging people who have become withdrawn from conversation because of hearing loss to seek help. The store, a certified hearing-aid specialist shop, will mark its 45th anniversary in April next year and is promoting a campaign that gives customers up to ¥60,000 in purchase subsidies for hearing aids. The center emphasizes restoring the joy of hearing and making daily life more vibrant, offering expert fitting and support to improve communication. Prospective customers are advised to consult the shop for details on eligibility and how to apply for the subsidy.
