The student organization 'My Own Place', founded in 2016 at Keio University, marked its 10th anniversary with a thanksgiving and activity report on 12 September at Tōshō-ji temple in Takakura, led by chief priest Kurosawa Sōgō. Members reviewed past activities supporting children's spaces and pledged to continue providing safe places for children in the community. The event combined formal reports with thanks to supporters and local partners who helped sustain the group's outreach over the past decade.
