Tomoya Miyama, a 64-year-old resident of Shonandai, has been appointed representative of the cultural and creative organization 'Shonan Cultiva'. He will lead the platform that builds networks in cultural arts and connects diverse stakeholders, emphasizing the way music brings out children's vitality. Miyama's main occupation is as a club activities instructor, and he brings more than 40 years of experience to the role.
