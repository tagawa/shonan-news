At the UNICON 22 world unicycling championships held in Steyr, Austria, from 25 July to 7 August, 21-year-old Misaki Uchida, a third-year student at Tokai University residing in Chigasaki, won the overall women's competition for the third consecutive edition. Uchida set new records in three events during the meet and secured victories across multiple disciplines. Her performance continued Japan's strong showing in international unicycling and reinforced her status as a leading competitor in the sport.
