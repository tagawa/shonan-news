The outdoor music festival, billed as the largest on the Miura Peninsula, attracted local and visiting reggae artists and fans across two days. Organizers and performers, including reggae musicians from the area, emphasized community ties and repeated their declaration of 'sowing seeds for the future' for the event's cultural legacy. The festival featured live performances and local participation aimed at supporting regional music and youth engagement.
