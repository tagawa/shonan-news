The 'Zushi Art Festival' will be held from 3 October to 25 October as a triennial art event. This year's edition, framed as a triennale, uses the theme 'はじまりの未来、はじまりの場' and will present installations and programs across the city. Local venues and public spaces will host works and participatory projects intended to immerse residents and visitors in contemporary art. Organizers expect the festival to animate Zushi and attract audiences throughout the three-week period.
