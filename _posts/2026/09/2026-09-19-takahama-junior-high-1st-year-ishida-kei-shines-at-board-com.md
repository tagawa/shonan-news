The Mind Sports Olympiad (MSO), a global competitive mind-sport festival, was held in London from the twenty-second to the thirtieth of last month for its thirtieth anniversary. Takahama Junior High first-year student Ishida Kei achieved multiple medals—gold, silver and bronze—competing against top players from around the world, overcoming language barriers.
