Seaside.Kitchen Zushi, a cafe in Ikego's Zushi Daiichi Sports Park run by Ima Akihito, launched a project to use soon-to-expire 'Zushi kurashi ouen-ken' vouchers in a unique way. The project aims to redirect the vouchers' value toward children's meals.
