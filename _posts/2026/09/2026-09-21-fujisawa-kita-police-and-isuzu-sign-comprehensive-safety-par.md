Fujisawa Kita Police Station, led by Chief Kojima Hiroshi, signed a "Comprehensive Partnership on Community Safety and Security" with Isuzu Motors on the ninth, and the agreement ceremony took place at Isuzu Plaza in Tsuchidana. At the ceremony, Isuzu presented a mascot and the event continued with further activities described by the source.
