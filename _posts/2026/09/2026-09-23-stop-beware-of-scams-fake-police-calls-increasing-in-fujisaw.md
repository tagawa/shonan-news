Special fraud victims are increasing in Fujisawa City. In the jurisdictions of Fujisawa Police Station and Fujisawa Kita Police Station, the provisional number of recognized special fraud cases from January to the end of July is 68, and the reported losses total about 600,000,000 yen, approximately 200,000,000 yen more than last year.
