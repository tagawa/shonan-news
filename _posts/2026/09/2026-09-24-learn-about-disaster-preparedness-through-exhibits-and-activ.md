The Oiso Disaster Preparedness Fair will take place on 4 October (Sun) at Oiso Undo Koen from 10:00 to 15:00 with free admission. Exhibits of disaster-preparedness goods will be displayed on the park's multipurpose plaza, and vehicles that are useful in disasters will also be present.
