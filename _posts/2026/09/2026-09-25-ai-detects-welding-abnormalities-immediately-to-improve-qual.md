Bunsekiya Co., led by president Daisaku Mizoguchi, on 15 September unveiled an AI system called 'SAMURAI Analyzer for Welding' that can be added to existing welding machines to detect welding abnormalities immediately. The announcement described the system as intended to improve quality and reduce labor, and the description in the source cuts off after naming the product.
