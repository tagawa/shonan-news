The pictured Jomon pottery fragments were discovered by local citizens in 1972 at the Ooya site in northern Chigasaki and were donated to Chigasaki City in 1973. The item is featured in the curator's series 'Curator's Recommendation' Vol.46, highlighting artifacts that reflect ancient Chigasaki.
