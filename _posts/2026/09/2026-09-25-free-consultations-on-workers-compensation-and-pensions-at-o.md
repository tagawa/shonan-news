The event is organized by the Kanagawa Prefecture Certified Social Insurance and Labor Consultants Association, Fujisawa Branch, led by branch head Yukimi Furuta. It will take place on October 2 (Fri) at the JR Ofuna Station south ticket gate east-west free passage, and run from 11:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
