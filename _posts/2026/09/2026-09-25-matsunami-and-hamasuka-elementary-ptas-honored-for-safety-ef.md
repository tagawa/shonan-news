On 9 September at Kanagawa Prefectural Office, the 'Excellent PTA Kanagawa Prefectural Board of Education Awards' recognized PTAs with achievements in promoting healthy child development. From Chigasaki City, Matsunami Elementary School and Hamasuka Elementary School were among those honored for their efforts to protect safety.
