The event will discuss how species names, such as the deep-sea fish 'Mitsukurienaga-chouchin-ankou' found in Sagami Bay, have unique origins and histories. It will be held on 4 October (Sun) at Hayama Shiosai Park within the Shiomi... location, and will also cover learning about traces of natural disasters.
