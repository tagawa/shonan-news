On 1 September the Ofuna and Kamakura police stations changed the legal speed limit for residential streets without signage to 30Km/h. Roads that carry signposted limits such as 40Km/h remain governed by those signs. The source text indicates residential streets are those frequently used by local residents but is cut off before completing the definition.
