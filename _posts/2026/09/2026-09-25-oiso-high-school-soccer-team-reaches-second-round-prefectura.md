Kanagawa Prefectural Oiso High School's soccer team qualified for the second-round prefectural qualifiers of the '2026 National High School Soccer Tournament'. The achievement is the team's first since its founding in 1973, reached in the club's 53rd year; the match is scheduled for 26 September.
