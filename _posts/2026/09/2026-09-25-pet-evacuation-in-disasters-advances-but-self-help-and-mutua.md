With frequent major earthquakes and concentrated heavy rains, measures for evacuating pets have drawn attention. Past disasters have produced troubles where owners were separated from pets and confusion over keeping animals at evacuation centers. The article says progress is being made in accepting pets at shelters, but emphasizes that 'self-help' and 'mutual aid' are important and that owners' preparedness is the key.
