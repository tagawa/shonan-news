Third-year students Kawano Shunsuke and Hasegawa Wataru received the poster presentation award at the 'Super Science High School Student Research Presentation' held in Hyogo Prefecture. Their project, developed through about one hour per week of extracurricular study, focused on restoring an extinct plant.
