The NHK Taiga drama 'Gyakuzoku no Bakushin', starring the late Edo-period retainer Oguri Uenosuke Tadanori, is due to begin broadcasting in January. In preparation for the series, promotional activities are being intensified and plans include opening a Taiga Drama Hall at 'Sanpura' related to the Yokosuka Steel Works area near the current U.S. Navy Yokosuka Base.
