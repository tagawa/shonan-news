The 'Sagami Kuni Rokusha Goshuin Meguri Shikishi' is a colored sheet for collecting goshuin stamps from six shrines participating in the Sagami Kofu Festival: Samukawa Jinja, Kawagoe Jinja, Hibita Jinja, Maedori Jinja, Hiratsuka Hachiman-gu, and Rokuso Jinja. Distribution began in September, and a dedicated goshinmon crest for the sheet was also created.
