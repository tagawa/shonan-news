The Miura City Council of Social Welfare is hosting the 'Chiiki o Tsunagu Hatsuratsu Festa' on 4 October (Sun) at the council's Anshin-kan in Hikihashi. The event runs from 10:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. and will feature welfare and volunteer activities from within the city.
