The Shitamachi Neighborhood Association of Chigasaki City was selected as an implementation area for the 'residential alarm distribution model project' run by the Japan Fire Equipment Association. On the fifteenth, at a community hall, the association received fifty residential fire alarms and fire extinguishers before the description cuts off.
