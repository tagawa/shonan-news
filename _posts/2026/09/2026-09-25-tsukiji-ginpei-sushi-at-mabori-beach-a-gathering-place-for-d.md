Tsukiji Ginpei Sushi, a compact restaurant near Mabori Kaigan Station, is marking its thirty-sixth year with a bustling atmosphere. The energetic voices and cheerful faces that fill the shop are led by students who attend the nearby Defense Academy up the hill.
