Yokosuka City opened an emergency press conference on 24 September where Mayor Kamichi Katsuaki reported on citywide damage and upcoming response measures following record heavy rain associated with Typhoon No. 25. The report said the typhoon brought its closest approach on 21 September and, with disaster response prioritized, a local fireworks festival was cancelled.
