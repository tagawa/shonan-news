The torrential rain and landslides brought by Typhoon No. 25 caused catastrophic damage in parts of Yokosuka and Miura, one of the prefecture’s leading open-field vegetable-growing areas. In Yokosuka’s Hayashi district, a farmer manages about 2 hectares of farmland.
