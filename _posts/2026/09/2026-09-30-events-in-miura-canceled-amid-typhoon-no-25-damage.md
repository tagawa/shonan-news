The Miura Night Market Executive Committee Secretariat announced on 29 September that it had canceled the '18th Miura Night Market: Lantern Night Walk,' which had been scheduled for 3 October (Sat) and 4 October (Sun) at Misaki Downtown Shopping District. The title cites damage from Typhoon No. 25 and other factors.
