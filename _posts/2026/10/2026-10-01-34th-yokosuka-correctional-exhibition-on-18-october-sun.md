The exhibition will display and sell prison-made products from across Japan, including the popular 'Blue Stick' laundry soap made at the Yokosuka Prison Branch. Visitors can tour the facility; the event runs from 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m., rain or shine, and admission is free.
