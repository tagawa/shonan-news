More than 120 types of umeshu from across Japan will be featured, along with new-style drinks and food pairings. The Autumn Rose Festa will also take place at the same time; hours are 11:00 to 18:00, except 12:00 to 19:00 on the first day and until 17:00 on the final day, and general tickets cost 1,900 yen. For inquiries, call 029 (229) 0712, Umeshu Kenkyukai.
