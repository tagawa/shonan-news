The tour will explore places linked to Oryo, who moved to Yokosuka after Sakamoto Ryoma’s death and died at age 66, including her residence and shrines. It will run from 8:30 a.m. to around 2:30 p.m., will be canceled in rainy weather, and costs 500 yen; the application deadline is October 3. For inquiries, contact the Yokosuka City Guide Association at 046-822-8256.
