The festival will feature dance performances and an exhibition of works. On weekends during the event, a flea market with distinctive clothing and general-goods shops will be held, alongside food from kitchen cars; hours are 10:00 to 18:00, with the flea market running until 16:00. For inquiries, contact Minami-shitaura Community Center at 046-887-0550.
