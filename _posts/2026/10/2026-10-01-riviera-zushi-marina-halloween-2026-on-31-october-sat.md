From 10:00 to 16:00, the event will feature a costume parade along palm-lined avenues for families and their dogs, a market with Shonan creators, and an additive-free icing cookie workshop. Advance registration for the costume parade costs 1,000 yen for participants aged 15 and under; other fees also apply.
