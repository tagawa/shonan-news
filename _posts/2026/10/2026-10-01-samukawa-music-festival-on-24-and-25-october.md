Performances will include harmonica, ocarina, acoustic guitar and bands from junior high schools in the town. Advance entry tickets will be distributed at the center and community centers from 1 October; performances begin at 1 p.m. on 24 October and 10 a.m. on 25 October, with free admission. For information, contact Samukawa Town Residents' Center at 0467 (74) 2333.
