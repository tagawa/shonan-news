The athletes, who belong to Chigasaki Taekwondo Dojo in Wakamatsucho, visited Chigasaki City Hall on 20 August and reported to Mayor Sato Hikaru on their performances at a national competition. Koizumi Ai, 18, competed in the 37th All Japan Taekwondo Championships.
