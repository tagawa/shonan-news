Yoshida Nagao, a 43-year-old Kinko-ryu shakuhachi performer who lives in Chigasaki, will take the name of his great-grandfather, the celebrated Yoshida Seifu. A commemorative concert will be held in Kumamoto on 31 October (Sat).
