On the 17th of last month, Shonan Ladybird, Fujisawa City’s only girls’ elementary school softball team, reported to the mayor on its runner-up finish in the 43rd Kanto Elementary School Girls Softball Tournament, held in Tokyo in late August. The team is coached by Katsuichi Ito.
