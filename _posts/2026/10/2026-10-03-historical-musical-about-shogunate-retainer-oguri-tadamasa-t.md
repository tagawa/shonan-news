The musical, titled 'Those Who Inherit the Iron Will: The Man of Iron Will,' depicts Oguri Tadamasa, also known as Uenosuke, who was active in the final years of the Edo period. It will run for two days at the theatre’s small hall, Yokosuka Bayside Pocket.
