Kanagawa Dental University, located in Inaokacho, Yokosuka, is participating in the Pink Ribbon Light-Up 2026 in Kanagawa awareness campaign to promote early breast cancer detection and increase screening rates. The university describes this as a contribution to society by a medical university.
