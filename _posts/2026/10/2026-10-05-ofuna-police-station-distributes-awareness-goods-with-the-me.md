On 24 September, Ofuna Police Station held a campaign alongside the Autumn National Traffic Safety Campaign and distributed awareness goods. The campaign publicized that the statutory speed limit on residential roads had become 30 km/h from 1 September and aimed to raise traffic safety awareness.
