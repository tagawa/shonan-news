The multigenerational exchange event 'Uwamatsuri 2026' will be held on 10 October (Sat) in Uwamachi, known for its retro streetscape and signboard-style buildings. It is hosted by the Uwamachi Shopping Street Federation, formed by three shopping streets in the district.
