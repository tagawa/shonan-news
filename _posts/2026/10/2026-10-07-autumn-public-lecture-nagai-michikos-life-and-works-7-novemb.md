The lecture, ‘Nagai Michiko’s Life and Works,’ will be co-hosted by the Kamakura Pen Club and Hayami Art Academy. It will be held on the fourth floor of the school’s Kamakura main campus, a three-minute walk from the east exit of Kamakura Station, starting at 3 p.m.
