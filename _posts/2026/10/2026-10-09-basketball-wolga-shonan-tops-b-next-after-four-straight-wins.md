The men’s professional basketball club Wolga Shonan, based in Fujisawa, Chigasaki, Atsugi and Samukawa, held its first home games of the season on the 2nd and 3rd at Akibadai Cultural Gymnasium. The club had won four consecutive games and led B Next.
