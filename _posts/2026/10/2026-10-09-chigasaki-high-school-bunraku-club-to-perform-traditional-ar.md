The Kanagawa Prefectural Chigasaki High School Bunraku Club will present 'An Invitation to Bunraku' from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. on October 31 (Sat), at the multipurpose room on the basement floor of the Chigasaki Park Experience Learning Center, Umikaze Terrace. Doors will open at 1:30 p.m.
