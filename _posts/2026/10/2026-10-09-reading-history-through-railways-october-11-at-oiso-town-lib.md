The Oiso Culture and Arts Festival special event, ‘Reading Local History Through Railways: Shonan Kido and Oiso and Ninomiya Stations,’ will be held on 11 October (Sunday) in the large conference room on the second floor of Oiso Town Library’s main building. It will begin at 1 p.m.
