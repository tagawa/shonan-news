About 50 members of five Lions Clubs in Fujisawa, Fujisawa LC, Fujisawa Shonan LC, Fujisawa Chuo LC, Fujisawa Life LC and Fujisawa Acty LC, were at Shonan Mall Fill on the 4th. The report describes the effort as their second support effort for Kumamoto.
