Kanagawa Prefectural Tsurumine High School’s wind ensemble will present the 'Sunday Afternoon Concert' on 25 October (Sun) in the large conference room on the second floor of Tsurumine Higashi Community Center, at 180 Nishikubo, Chigasaki. The doors will open at 1 p.m.
