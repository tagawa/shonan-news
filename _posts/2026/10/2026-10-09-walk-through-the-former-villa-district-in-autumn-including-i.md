About 110 years ago, Ito Rinosuke, the first mayor of Chigasaki Town, actively attracted villas to promote the town’s development. Nearly all the villas that once stood by the sea have now disappeared, and the event will include a walk through the former villa district, including Isamu Noguchi’s former home.
