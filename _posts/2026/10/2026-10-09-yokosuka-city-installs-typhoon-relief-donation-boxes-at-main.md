On 8 October, Yokosuka City installed the boxes at municipal facilities after Kanagawa Prefecture designated the area as eligible for allocations from the 'Reiwa 8 Typhoon No. 25 Disaster Relief Donations.' The boxes are for collecting relief donations from residents and others.
