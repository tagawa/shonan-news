The 4.5-kilometer Manazuru Road, a toll road and Route 135 bypass connecting Yoshihama in Yugawara Town, Ashigarashimo District, with Iwa in Manazuru Town, will have nighttime closures for tunnel reinforcement work. The road is also known as the Manazuru Blue Line.
