Koichi Tanaka, a 73-year-old potter who has a kiln in Katasemejiroyama, will exhibit his work from 11 October (Sun) to 13 October (Tue) at Matsu no Mori Kugenuma, a nationally registered tangible cultural property nearing its 100th anniversary.
