'Oku-Chiga Craft Market' is being held through October 12 (Mon, public holiday) at Irohani Woodworking Studio, 1296-1 Serizawa, Chigasaki. It is open each day from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., with woodworker Junko Yamashita among the participants.
