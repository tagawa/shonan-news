Since late September, the gelato shop Gelateria Konnaka in Yasuracho, Yokosuka, has offered a parfait inspired by Cherry Cheesecake, one of Yokosuka's three famous local specialties. The menu item was proposed by third-year students at Otsu Junior High School.
