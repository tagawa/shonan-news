Yokosuka Downtown Club (YDC), a group of young volunteers from four shopping streets in the Yokosuka Chuo area, will hold the event on 18 October (Sun). It will feature street-corner live music, a dance battle show, a market and a pop-up shop by Tsukimidai Market.
