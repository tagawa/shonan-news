Hiratsuka City will hold the 2026 fair on 17 October (Sat) at 'Hiratsuka no Harappa' in Hiratsuka City General Park. It aims to raise residents’ awareness of fire and disaster prevention and deepen their understanding of the city administration; attractions include ladder truck rides, exhibit booths and food trucks.
