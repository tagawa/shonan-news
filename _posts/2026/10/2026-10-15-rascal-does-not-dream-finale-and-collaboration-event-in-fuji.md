The theatrical anime 'Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend,' the final installment in the popular 'Rascal Does Not Dream' series featuring Enoshima and Shonan, is scheduled to open on 16 October (Fri). The title announces a collaboration event in Fujisawa to mark the release.
