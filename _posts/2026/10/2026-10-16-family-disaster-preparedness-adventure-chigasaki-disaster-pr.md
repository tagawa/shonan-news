As part of its 50th anniversary project, the Chigasaki Shonan Rotary Club will hold the hands-on disaster-preparedness event ‘Chigasaki Disaster Prevention Quest: A Mission for Families to Take On! Seismic Intensity 7’ on 28 November (Sat). Participants are being recruited, and bento lunches are included.
