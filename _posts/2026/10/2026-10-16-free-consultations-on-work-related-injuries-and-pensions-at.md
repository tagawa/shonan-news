The Kanagawa Labor and Social Security Attorney Association’s Fujisawa branch, led by branch chief Yukimi Furuta, will hold its 'Autumn Street Free Consultation' on 20 October (Tue) on the pedestrian deck at Chigasaki Station’s north exit. It will run from noon to 6 p.m.
