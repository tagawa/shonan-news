The Fujisawa City Disaster Prevention Fair will take place from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. on 18 October (Sun) at Fujisawa City Hall, 1-1 Asahicho, and its surroundings. It aims to give residents hands-on experience with disaster preparedness and raise awareness.
