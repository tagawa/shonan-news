Hiratsuka Junior Chamber, chaired by Keiki Ozaki, visited Hiratsuka City Hall on 30 September and handed relief donations for the Kumamoto earthquake disaster area to Mayor Katsuhiro Ochiai, head of the Japanese Red Cross Society’s Hiratsuka City District.
