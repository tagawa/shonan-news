Mitsuhiro Tsukino, 47, who runs 'Kamakura Hanko' in Onarimachi, won gold in 'mokuguchi mikoku,' described as the most technically difficult category, at the 26th National Seal Engraving Technology Competition organized by the All Japan Seal Engraving Industry Association.
