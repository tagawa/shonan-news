The annual ‘Fureai Bon Dance at Tengakuin’ was held at Tengakuin, at 1-1-1 Watauchi, on the 11th and 12th. About 5,000 people, including nearby families with children and elementary and junior high school students, gathered to enjoy the autumn evenings.
