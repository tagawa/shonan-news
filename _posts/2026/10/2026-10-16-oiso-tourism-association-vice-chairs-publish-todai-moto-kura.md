Tenki Morikawa, 51, and Kazuki Sato, 48, who serve as vice chairs of the Oiso Town Tourism Association, published the autumn issue of their newsletter, 'Tōdai Moto Kurashi,' in early October. It was the second issue, following its launch in early April.
