The Kanagawa Prefectural Federation of Corporate Associations, chaired by Nobumasa Takahashi, held the cleanup as part of its social contribution activities. About 300 members and others from 18 corporate associations across the prefecture, including the Hiratsuka Corporate Association, took part.
