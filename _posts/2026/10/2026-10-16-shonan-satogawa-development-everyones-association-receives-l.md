The association received the 2026 Water Resources Merit Award, presented by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, for achievements including river conservation. On 6 October, its chair, Hirohiro Fujino, vice chair, Shingo Komaki, and secretariat representatives were mentioned in connection with the award.
