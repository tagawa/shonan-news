The Hiratsuka City rights-protection lecture, titled 'Advance Planning to Protect Yourself and Your Family: Voluntary Guardianship, Family Trusts (Civil Trusts) and Wills,' will take place on 13 November (Fri) in the large conference room on the third floor of Hiratsuka Kurihara Home, at 31-20 Tatsunocho.
