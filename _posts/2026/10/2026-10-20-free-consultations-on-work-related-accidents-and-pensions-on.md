The Kanagawa Prefectural Social Insurance and Labor Consultant Association’s Fujisawa Branch, headed by Yukimi Furuta, will hold its ‘Autumn Street Free Consultation’ on 29 October (Thu) in the pedestrian passageway at the north exit of JR Tsujido Station. It will run from noon to 7 p.m.
