The Kamakura City Fire Department will host the 2026 Fire Department Community Plaza from 9:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. on 1 November (Sun), with 'fire department udon' also offered. The event has usually been held at a fire station, but this year it will take place at the Kamakura City Hall parking lot.
