For Kanagawa Prefecture’s ‘Safety and Security Community Development’ campaign period, postal workers from Okamoto and Ueki became one-day police officers at Ofuna Police Station on 15 October. They urged vigilance against special fraud in the shopping district in front of Ofuna Station.
