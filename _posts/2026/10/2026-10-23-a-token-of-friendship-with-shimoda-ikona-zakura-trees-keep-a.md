Donations of Ikona-zakura, known as early-blooming cherry trees, are continuing at locations throughout Hayama Town. The trees originated in Shimoda City, Shizuoka Prefecture, which has a friendship-city relationship with the town, and 10 were planted behind Goryo Shrine in Nagae on 13 October.
