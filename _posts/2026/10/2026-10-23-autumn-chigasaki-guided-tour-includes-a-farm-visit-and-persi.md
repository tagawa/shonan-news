The Chigasaki City Tourism Association is seeking participants for ‘Journey Through Autumn Satoyama: Chigasaki Beef and Persimmon-Picking Experience’ on 28 November (Sat). The tour runs from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., and the participation fee is 4,500 yen.
