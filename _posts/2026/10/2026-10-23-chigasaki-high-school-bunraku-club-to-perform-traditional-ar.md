The prefectural high school’s Bunraku Club will present 'An Invitation to Bunraku' in the multipurpose room on the basement floor of the Chigasaki Park Experiential Learning Center, also known as Umikaze Terrace, at 3-3-9 Nakakaigan, Chigasaki. The event is scheduled for 2 p.m.
