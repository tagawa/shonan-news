The Chigasaki mayoral election, prompted by the end of the incumbent’s term, is scheduled to be announced on 25 October, with voting and counting on 1 November. As of 20 October, five people, including the incumbent and newcomers, had expressed their intention to run; incumbent Hikaru Sato is among them.
