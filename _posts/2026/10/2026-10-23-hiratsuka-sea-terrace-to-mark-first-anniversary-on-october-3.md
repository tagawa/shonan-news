The seaside park Hiratsuka Sea Terrace, at 19-1 Ryujogaoka, will mark one year since opening on October 31 (Sat). The anniversary will be celebrated with the Hiratsuka Sea Terrace Festival on October 31 (Sat) and November 1 (Sun), with pupils from Hanamizu Elementary School at the event.
