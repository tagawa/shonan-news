Nakano Sekkei Komu, based in Shineicho, Chigasaki, digitized 13 items excavated from the Shimoterao Kanga Site Group in the Koide district and recognized as cultural properties, then donated them to the city on October 15. The donation was part of the company's 80th anniversary project.
