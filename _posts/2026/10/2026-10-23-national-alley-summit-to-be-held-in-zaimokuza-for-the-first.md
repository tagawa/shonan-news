The 21st National Alley Summit 2026 in Kamakura Zaimokuza will take place over two days, 7 and 8 November (Sun), at Jodo sect head temple Komyoji in Zaimokuza. The summit will provide a forum for discussion on regional revitalization and preservation through alleyways.
