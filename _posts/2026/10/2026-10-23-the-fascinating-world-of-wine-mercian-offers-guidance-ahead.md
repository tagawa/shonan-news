Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce and Industry Youth Division, known as Fujisawa YEG, held a joint meeting with Mercian's Fujisawa factory on the 19th. Ahead of the Fujisawa Wine Festival on 21 November (Sat), about 20 people, including members of the festival's organizing committee and company employees, took part.
