The briefing for prospective candidates in the Oiso mayoral election, which will be officially announced on 24 November (Tue) and voted on and counted on 29 November (Sun), was held on 20 October at the town hall’s main building. A total of two camps, one representing the incumbent and one a newcomer, attended.
