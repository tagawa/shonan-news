On 20 October, violinist Issei Kurihara, a graduate of Takeyama Junior High School, performed at his alma mater as part of the Yokosuka Arts Foundation’s artist dispatch program for elementary and junior high schools in Yokosuka. He also performed the school song with the brass band.
