Students from Nihon University’s College of Bioresource Sciences in Kameino recently created the ‘Fujisawa Sugoroku’ to showcase the city’s attractions. It includes information about areas across Fujisawa in quiz form and is to be unveiled on 24 October (Sat) at Endo Civic Center.
