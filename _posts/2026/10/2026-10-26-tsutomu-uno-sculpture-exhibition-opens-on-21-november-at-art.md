The exhibition runs from 21 to 24 November and features works by sculptor Tsutomu Uno, mainly made using lost-wax casting, which he studied in Italy. Uno created the Yuzo Kayama statue in Chigasaki and the statue of Ninomiya Kinjiro and his wife in Odawara.
