The Fujisawa SST Cultural Festival 2026 will be held on 3 November (Tue) at the P1 parking lot of [Shonan T-SITE](https://store.tsite.jp/shonan/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in Tsujidomotomachi. The event offers an opportunity to experience community-wide town development involving residents, companies and the government.
