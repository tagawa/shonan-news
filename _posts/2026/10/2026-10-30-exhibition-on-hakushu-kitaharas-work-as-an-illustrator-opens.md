The exhibition will be held from 1 November (Sun) to 29 November (Sun) at the Hakushu Memorial Museum on Jogashima, Miura, as part of the 49th Misaki Hakushu Festival, which commemorates the achievements of Hakushu Kitahara, who loved Misaki.
