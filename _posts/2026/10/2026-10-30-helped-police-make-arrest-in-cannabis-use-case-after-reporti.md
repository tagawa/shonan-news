On the 20th, Fujisawa Police Chief Jiro Asada presented a certificate of appreciation to Yoshitada Kurihara, chairman of the Enoshima Beach Association, for contributing to an arrest in an illegal drug case. Kurihara contacted authorities after noticing smoke and an unusual odor.
