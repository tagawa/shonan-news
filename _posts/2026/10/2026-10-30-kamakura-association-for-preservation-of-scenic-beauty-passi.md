This year marks the 60th anniversary of the Ancient Capitals Preservation Law and the 10th anniversary of the certification of the Historical Landscape Maintenance and Improvement Plan, milestones for Kamakura’s historic character. The city is currently holding commemorative events, including exhibitions and symposiums.
