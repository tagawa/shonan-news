At the event, one ticket costs 3,000 yen and can be used for one drink and one food item at each of three shops. A free live performance will be held at Shonan-Enoshima Station at 13:30 on November 21 (Sat). Tickets will be sold on the day at Magobemaru Parking in Koshigoe, Kamakura City.
