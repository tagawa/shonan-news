The festival will offer a full day of rockabilly, music that originated in the United States, including live performances by seven acts, merchandise sales, dance lessons, and free hair and makeup experiences. It will run from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., with free admission. For inquiries, call the organizing committee at 046 (888) 2157.
