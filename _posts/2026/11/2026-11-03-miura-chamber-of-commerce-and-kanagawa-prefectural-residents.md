The Miura Chamber of Commerce, chaired by Koji Okuyama, and the Kanagawa Prefectural Residents' Cooperative, led by president Yasuhiro Teshima, donated 461,640 yen to Miura Mayor Kaichi Deguchi on 30 October. The donation was for recovery support following Typhoon No. 25.
