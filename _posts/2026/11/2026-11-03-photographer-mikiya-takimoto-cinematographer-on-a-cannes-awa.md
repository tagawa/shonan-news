Takimoto won the Japan Academy Prize for Best Cinematography for Hirokazu Kore-eda’s film Our Little Sister, set in Kamakura. His major exhibition, ‘LUNATION: Sakubo, Reading Celestial Bodies from the Sea,’ is currently being held at Chigasaki City Museum of Art, 1 Higashikaigankita, Chigasaki.
