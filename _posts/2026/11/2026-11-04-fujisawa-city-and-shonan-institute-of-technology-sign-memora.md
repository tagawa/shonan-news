Fujisawa City announced on 22 October that it had signed a memorandum with Shonan Institute of Technology concerning cooperation on its co-creation engineering practical training program. The university will reorganize its Faculty of Engineering from the 2027 academic year and establish a new Co-Creation Engineering major.
