The 38th exhibition by Nadeshiko-kai, a group studying Japanese-style painting mainly in Hiratsuka City, will run from 12 November (Thu) to 17 November (Tue) at Hiratsuka Civic Plaza. It will be open from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., closing at 3 p.m. on the final day.
