The third market hosted by the Okura Yoritomo Shopping Association, chaired by Shoichi Matsunaga, will take place on 22 November (Sun) at the venue opposite Sanjoya Otani Shoten, at 3-6-29 Yukinoshita, Kamakura. It will include a raffle with no losing tickets; in severe weather, it will be held the following day, 23 November (Mon).
