A community exchange event aimed at helping residents feel closer to their fire department was held at Fujisawa City Fire Department’s South and North Fire Stations. The North Fire Station’s event took place on 8 August at the Fujisawa City Fire and Disaster Prevention Training Center in Ishikawa.
