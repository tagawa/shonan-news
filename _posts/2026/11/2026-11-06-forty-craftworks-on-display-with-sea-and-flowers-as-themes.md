The Shonan Craft Artists Association, chaired by Akiko Shibayama, will hold its 51st commemorative juried exhibition from 20 November (Fri) to 24 November (Tue) at Hiratsuka Civic Plaza Hall. The association is made up of about 20 people from inside and outside Hiratsuka City.
