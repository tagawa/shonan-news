Yuka Kondo, a second-year student at Kamakura Municipal Fukasawa Junior High School, won the top prize in the 'Our Cultural Properties' category of the 55th Cultural Property Protection Poster Project for junior high school students in Kanagawa Prefecture. Her poster will be displayed in an underground gallery from 10 November.
