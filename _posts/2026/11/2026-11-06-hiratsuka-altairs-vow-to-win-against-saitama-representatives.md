Hiratsuka Altairs, a rubber-ball baseball team made up of players from junior high school baseball clubs in Hiratsuka, will compete on 7 and 8 November (Sun) in Utsunomiya and elsewhere in Tochigi Prefecture. The team is represented by Tetsuya Satokura and coached by Tomohide Taira.
