‘Human Rights Gathering 2026 in Samukawa’ will be held at Samukawa Town Community Center on 28 November (Sat). Doors open at 12:30 p.m. and the event begins at 1 p.m.; Hitomi Soga, a victim of abduction by North Korea, will be the speaker.
