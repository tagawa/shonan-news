This year marks 81 years since the explosion at the former gun battery site in Kotsubo, Zushi. The annual memorial service, usually held on the anniversary of the accident, 20 October, will take place on 15 November (Sun) this year, hosted by the Kotsubo Gun Battery Accident Bereaved Families Association.
