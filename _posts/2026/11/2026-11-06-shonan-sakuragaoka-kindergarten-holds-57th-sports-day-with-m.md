The 57th sports day for Shonan Sakuragaoka Kindergarten, operated by school corporation Kubota Gakuen and led by principal Tomoko Kubota, was held on 17 October at Sakuragaoka Park in Sakuragaoka, Hiratsuka City. About 800 people cheered on the children.
