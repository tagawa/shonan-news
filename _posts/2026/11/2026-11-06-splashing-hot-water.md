Yudate Kagura, in which people divine good or bad fortune using water in a large cauldron and pray for health by bathing in its spray, was performed at Shirahata Shrine on 28 October, captivating visitors. The ritual has been passed down since the Edo period and is designated an Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property of Fujisawa City.
