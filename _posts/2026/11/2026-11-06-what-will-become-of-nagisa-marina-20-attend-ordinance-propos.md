Zushi City is coordinating plans for the tentatively named Zushi City Nagisa Marina, a facility intended as a hub for tourism and marine leisure, with operations due to begin in April 2028. At a briefing on 31 October about the proposed ordinance, some attendees questioned how the facility should be operated.
