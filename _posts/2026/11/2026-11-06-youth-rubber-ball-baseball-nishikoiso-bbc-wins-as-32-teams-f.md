The 41st commemorative Kanagawa Sagami Friendship Flag Cup, Kanagawa Shimbun Cup and Leasekin Kanagawa Cup autumn youth rubber-ball baseball league tournament was held from 12 to 24 October at Oiso Sports Park baseball field and other venues. Nishikoiso BBC won, with 32 teams from across the prefecture taking part.
