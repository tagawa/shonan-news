The Chigasaki Satoyama Marche will take place over two days, 29 November (Sun) and 30 November (Mon), at organizer Hiori Suzuki’s home at 985-2 Namegaya, Chigasaki. It will run from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. on both days.
