The museum is holding its 50th anniversary autumn special exhibition, 'Treasures Return to Their Home: Archaeological Materials Connected to Hiratsuka.' It includes a 'triangular-rimmed deity-and-beast mirror' excavated from Shindo Otsukayama Kofun and borrowed from the Tokyo National Museum.
