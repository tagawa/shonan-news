Yuji Kuroiwa, governor of Kanagawa Prefecture, will visit locations around the prefecture to exchange views as part of 'Dialogue Plaza.' The Yokosuka Miura session will be held in the sixth-floor hall at Welk Yokosuka in Hinodecho, Yokosuka.
