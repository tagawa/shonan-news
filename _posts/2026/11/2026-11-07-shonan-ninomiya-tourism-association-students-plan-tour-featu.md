The Shonan Ninomiya Tourism Association will hold the tour 'Get in Tune in Ninomiya: A Retreat for Mind and Body' on 28 November (Sat), planned from the perspective of students at SANNO University. The tour includes yoga and a wellness lecture at Umezawa Beach as part of an industry-academia-government collaboration project.
