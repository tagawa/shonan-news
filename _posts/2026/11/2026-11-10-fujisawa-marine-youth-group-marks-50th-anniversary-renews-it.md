The Fujisawa Marine Youth Group, which uses Enoshima’s natural environment to promote the healthy development of young people, marked its 50th anniversary with a ceremony at Enoshima Yacht Harbor on the 8th. Fujisawa Mayor Tsuneo Suzuki and others attended.
