The local collaborative performance, ‘6+ Cafe Year-End Yose / 44th Kanagawa Kiraku Yose,’ by the yose performing arts group Kanagawa Rakushokai will be held at Imaizumidai Neighborhood Hall, 4-6-13 Imaizumidai, Kamakura. Children will also appear.
