The Sanshuden hall at [Samukawa Shrine](https://samukawajinjya.jp/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, a five-minute walk from JR Miyayama Station, is accepting bookings for banquets, including year-end and New Year parties, spring farewell and welcome gatherings, reunions and celebrations. Its address is 3835-1 Miyayama, Samukawa Town.
