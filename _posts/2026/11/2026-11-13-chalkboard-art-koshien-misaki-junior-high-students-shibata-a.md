The winners of the 11th Nichigaku Chalkboard Art Koshien, which invites middle and high school students nationwide to submit works using chalkboards or whiteboards as canvases, were recently announced. Shibata and Watanabe of Misaki Junior High won awards in the whiteboard category for works themed around escapism.
