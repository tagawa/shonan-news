The Underwater Robot Competition in JAMSTEC 2026 was held on October 17 and 18, 2026, at the Japan Agency for Marine-Earth Science and Technology's Yokosuka headquarters. Team 'Kururin', based in Hiratsuka and made up of members aged 12 to 17, received the 'IDEA Special Award', with originality among the criteria evaluated.
