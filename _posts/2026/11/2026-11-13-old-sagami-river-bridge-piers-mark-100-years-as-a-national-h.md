The Old Sagami River Bridge Piers in Shimomachiya, Chigasaki, have reached the 100-year milestone since being designated a national historic site. Various commemorative events are being held this fiscal year, and a lecture by experts will be held in December. The piers emerged due to liquefaction in the Great Kanto Earthquake.
