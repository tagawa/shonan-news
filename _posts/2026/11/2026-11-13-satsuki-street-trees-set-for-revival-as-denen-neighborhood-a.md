The Den'en Neighborhood Association in Ofuna, chaired by Mitsuo Mizushima, donated satsuki azalea and tsutsuji azalea saplings to Kamakura City to help restore the roadside trees along Shochiku-dori, also known as Satsuki Hondori. On 5 November, planting and the donation took place at locations including the area in front of Ofuna Station’s Kasama Exit.
