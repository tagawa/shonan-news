[Shonan Bellmare](https://bellmare.co.jp/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, a J.League soccer club, produced the second edition of the 'Shonan Bellmare Arithmetic Workbook' with sponsorship from 7 local companies. The completion press conference was held on 3 November at Lemon Gas Stadium Hiratsuka, and the workbook will be distributed free to sixth graders in Hiratsuka City again this year.
