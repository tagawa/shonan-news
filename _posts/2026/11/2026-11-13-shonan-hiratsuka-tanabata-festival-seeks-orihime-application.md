Recruitment has begun for 'Shonan Hiratsuka Orihime', who will promote the attractions of Hiratsuka City through tourism events and social media, including the 75th Shonan Hiratsuka Tanabata Festival, scheduled for 2 July (Fri) to 4 July (Sun), 2027. Applications are due by 27 November.
