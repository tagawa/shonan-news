The Kanagawa Labor and Social Security Attorneys Association’s Fujisawa branch, led by Yukimi Furuta, and others will hold the ‘Autumn Street Free Consultation Event’ on the 26th (Thu) in the north-south passage at Fujisawa Station. The prefectural association will provide consultations on its professional system and labor-related matters.
