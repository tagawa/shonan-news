The ‘Reiwa 8 Hiratsuka Autumn Fire Prevention Campaign Fire Drill’ took place on 13 November at the Medical Corporation Kensuikai’s Takanedai Hospital and Asahi no Sato. Participants included facility staff, residents, and members of the Hiratsuka City Fire Department and fire stations.
