The exhibition will trace the achievements of the late Dr. Tetsu Nakamura, who was shot and killed alongside local staff while on his way to a work site in Jalalabad, Afghanistan, in December 2019, aged 73. The exhibition will be held on 5 and 6 December in front of Oiso Station.
