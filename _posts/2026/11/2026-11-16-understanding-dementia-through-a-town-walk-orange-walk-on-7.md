Hiratsuka Orange Walk aims to help build a community that is friendly to people with dementia. It will take place from 1:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m., with participants gathering in Meeting Room 519 on the fifth floor of [Hiratsuka City Hall](https://www.city.hiratsuka.kanagawa.jp/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}’s main building.
