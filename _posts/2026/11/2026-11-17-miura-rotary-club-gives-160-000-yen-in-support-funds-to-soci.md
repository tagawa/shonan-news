Miura Rotary Club, chaired by Tatsuki Yamamoto, handed 160,000 yen in support funds to the Miura City Council of Social Welfare, chaired by Minoru Sugiyama, on 10 November. The funds were intended to help residents of Miura City who suffered extensive damage from Typhoon No. 25.
