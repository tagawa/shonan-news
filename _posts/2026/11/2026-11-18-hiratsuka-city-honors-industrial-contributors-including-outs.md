On 11 November, Hiratsuka City honored people who contributed to the development of regional industry as 'Hiratsuka City Industrial Contributors.' At a ceremony at Hiratsuka City Bunka Koen Kaikan, 18 recipients across 7 categories received certificates from Mayor Katsuhiro Ochiai.
