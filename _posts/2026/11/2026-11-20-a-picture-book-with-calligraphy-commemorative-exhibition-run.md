Calligrapher and artist Sekiho Tajika, a Chigasaki resident whose real name is Yasuyuki Tajika, published 'A Picture Book with Calligraphy' in June, based on stories by Kenji Miyazawa. The exhibition will commemorate the publication and run from 26 November to 6 December.
