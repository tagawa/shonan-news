The Fujisawa Police Station, led by Chief Jiro Asada, and the Fujisawa Kita Police Station, led by Chief Hiroshi Kojima, presented commendations to two local companies on 27 and 28 October. The companies were recognized for their longstanding crime-prevention activities and contributions to a safer community.
