The American restaurant Sunny Side Kitchen Hayama in Kikoba, Hayama Town, will serve the 'Luther Burger' for the first time during the three-day period from 21 to 23 November. The burger, priced at 2,980 yen, uses doughnuts as buns and features two flavors and textures.
