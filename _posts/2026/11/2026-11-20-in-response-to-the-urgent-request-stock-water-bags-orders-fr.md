On 2 November, the Disaster Management Agency called on the public to prepare emergency supplies as part of a push to strengthen advance disaster preparedness. Town News, which handles disaster-prevention supplies, saw a surge in orders from neighborhood associations working on local disaster preparedness, including water bags.
