The award recognized longstanding efforts in blood donation by Shonan Eizen Kyokai Co., Ltd., based in Kameino, and its affiliate Shonan Architecture Co., Ltd., based in Shonandai. Shonan Eizen Kyokai is headed by Shigeo Mogami, and Shonan Architecture by Keiko Shimizu.
