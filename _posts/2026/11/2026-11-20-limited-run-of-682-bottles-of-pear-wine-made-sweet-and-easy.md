The Samukawa specialty pear wine, made with Shonan pears, was completed again this year, and sales began on 12 November. The Fujisawa Retail Liquor Sales Association’s Samukawa Branch commissions a sake brewer in Nagano Prefecture to make it, a practice that has continued for about 30 years.
