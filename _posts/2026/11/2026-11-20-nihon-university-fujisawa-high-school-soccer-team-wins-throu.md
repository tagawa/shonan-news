The final of the Kanagawa second-round qualifying tournament for the 105th National High School Soccer Championship was held at Nippatsu Mitsuzawa Football Stadium on the 8th. Nihon University Fujisawa High School secured its ninth appearance at the national tournament, and its second consecutive appearance.
