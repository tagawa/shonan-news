Pottery Workshop Kiraku, located in Tamura, Hiratsuka, was certified as a 'Hiratsuka Takumi no Mise' in recognition of its outstanding skills. An award ceremony was held on 11 November; proprietor Kentaro Ban, 49, was quoted.
