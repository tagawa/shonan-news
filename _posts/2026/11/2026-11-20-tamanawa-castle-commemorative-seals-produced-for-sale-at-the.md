The civic group Tamanawa Castle Site Community Development Council, with about 50 members, produced commemorative seals for Tamanawa Castle, built by Hojo Soun and located around Ueki and Shiromeguri. The seals will be sold at the Tamanawa Festival at Tamanawa Elementary School on 21 November (Sat).
