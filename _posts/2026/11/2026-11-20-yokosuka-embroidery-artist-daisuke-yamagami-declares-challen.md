Daisuke Yamagami, 36, is president of TAUTAU Inc., which makes and sells sukajan jackets in the Dobuita Street shopping district in Honcho, Yokosuka City. He spoke to this newspaper on 17 November and declared a challenge in next spring’s unified local elections.
