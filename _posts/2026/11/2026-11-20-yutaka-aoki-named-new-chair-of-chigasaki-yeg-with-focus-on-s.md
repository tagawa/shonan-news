The interim general meeting of the Chigasaki Chamber of Commerce and Industry’s Young Entrepreneurs Group, known as Chigasaki YEG, was held on 6 November. Yutaka Aoki, 49, was named its new chair for fiscal 2027, the group’s 44th term.
