Yasushi Ohagi and Kyuhee Park, active at the forefront of the classical guitar world, are scheduled to perform a guitar duo recital at [Zushi Cultural Plaza](https://www.bunka-plazahall.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} Nagisa Hall on November 23 (holiday).
