Tomonori Kaneko, president of KOTOBUKI, which describes itself as a 'nonprofit-type corporation,' received the 'Next Generation Leadership' special selection by the judging committee at the 5th Asia Health and Wellbeing Initiative Award (HAPI 2026). On 17 November, he visited Oiso Town Hall and reported the award to Mayor Toichiro Ikeda.
