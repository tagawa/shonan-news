The public exhibition will take place in Exhibition Room I at the [Hiratsuka City Museum of Art](https://www.city.hiratsuka.kanagawa.jp/art-muse/index.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and the Civic Art Gallery. It will be open from 9:30 a.m. to 4:50 p.m., closing at 3 p.m. on the final day.
