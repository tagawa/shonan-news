Chigasaki City is holding the experience class, titled 'Festive New Year Decorations to Bring Good Fortune,' as part of its independently planned citizen lecturer course. It will take place on 6 December (Sun) on the fourth floor of Chigasaki Citizens Gallery in Motomachi.
