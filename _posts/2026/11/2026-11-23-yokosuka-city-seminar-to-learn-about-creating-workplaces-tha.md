The seminar will deepen understanding of diverse sexualities, including LGBTQ+, and offer learning about workplace environments where everyone can work comfortably. It will be held in the first-floor multipurpose hall of the Yokosuka Chamber of Commerce and Industry, at 2-14-4 Heiseicho.
