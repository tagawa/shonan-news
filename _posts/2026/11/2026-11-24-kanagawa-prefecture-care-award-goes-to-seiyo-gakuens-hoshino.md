Hoshino Takayoshi, 48, who works at the Seiyo Gakuen residential facility for children with disabilities in Kidokoro, Hiratsuka, was among the recipients of the 33rd Kanagawa Prefecture Care Award. The recipients were announced on 2 November, and Hoshino expressed gratitude to his seniors, colleagues and juniors.
