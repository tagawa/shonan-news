The harvest festival for Yunoen Co., which grows open-field vegetables in Miyabara, was held at [Utsumochi Shrine](https://utsumochi-jinja.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in Uchimodori on the 21st. About 30 nearby parents and children took part in the event, whose theme was ‘agriculture and Noh,’ and experienced nature’s bounty.
