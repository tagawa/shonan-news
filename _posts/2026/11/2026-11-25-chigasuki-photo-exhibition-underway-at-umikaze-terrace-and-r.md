The 'My Chigasuki Photo Exhibition' brings together 60 photos of scenery, people and other subjects, under the theme 'What I love about Chigasaki.' It is being held through 1 December (Tue) at Chigasaki Park Experience Learning Centre and other venues, including Umikaze Terrace and roadside stations.
