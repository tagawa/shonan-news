'Goen no Engawa' is a gathering where residents bring ideas for activities they want to do or activities they are already doing, and connect with one another. It will be held on 22 December (Tue) in the meeting space at the Hiratsuka Civic Activity Centre, 2nd floor, 1-8-2 Mitsukecho, Hiratsuka.
