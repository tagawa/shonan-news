Ohashi Hazuki, who trains at the Shonan Ryuken boxing gym in Miyanomae, Hiratsuka, faced third-ranked Yoshida Takuya in a 10-round Japanese light-flyweight title match on 10 November. He won by decision to make his first title defence, while sticking to his own style.
