The wine and music festival was held on the 21st at [Sun Pearl Square](https://fujisawa-ekimae.com){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} by the north exit of Fujisawa Station and other venues. It was organised by the Fujisawa Chamber of Commerce and Industry Junior Division, chaired by Takeshi Namikawa, and the festival's executive committee. The event enjoyed clear skies.
