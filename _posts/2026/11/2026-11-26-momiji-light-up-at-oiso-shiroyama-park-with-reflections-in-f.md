The three-day 'Momiji Light-Up 2026' will take place from 27 to 29 November at the former Mitsui villa district of Kanagawa Prefectural Oiso Shiroyama Park in Kokufuhongo, Oiso Town. The event will feature autumn leaves reflected in Fudo Pond and Japanese music performances.
