Certified NPO JUDOs, based in Hiratsuka and chaired by Kosei Inoue, held the event, titled 'Let's Play Judo with Tokyo Deaflympics Representatives!' on 18 November at Hiratsuka School for the Deaf. Children from the kindergarten and elementary school departments took part.
