The karate division of the 2026 Chigasaki City General Sports Tournament, also the 42nd Chigasaki City Karate Championship, was held on 15 November at Chigasaki City General Gymnasium. It was a commemorative tournament marking the 50th anniversary of the Chigasaki City Karate Federation’s founding.
