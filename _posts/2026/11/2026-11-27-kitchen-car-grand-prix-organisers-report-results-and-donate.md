The Kitchen Car No. 1 Grand Prix was held at Daiichi Cutter Kiiro Park, also known as Chuo Park, on 11 October. On 9 November, committee members and others reported the results to Mayor Hikaru Sato and donated collected funds through Chigasaki City.
