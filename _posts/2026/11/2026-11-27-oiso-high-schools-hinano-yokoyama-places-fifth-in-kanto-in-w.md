Second-year Oiso High School student Hinano Yokoyama placed fifth in the women's javelin at the 30th Kanto High School Selected Newcomer Track and Field Championships, held at Nissan Stadium, also known as Yokohama International Stadium, on October 24. She achieved consecutive personal bests.
