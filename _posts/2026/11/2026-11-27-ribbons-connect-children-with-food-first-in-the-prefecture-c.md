Fujisawa City signed a partnership agreement on the 24th with the General Incorporated Association Long Spoon Association, headquartered in Ichikawa, Chiba Prefecture. The agreement aims to promote the 'Food Ribbon Project,' which supports children’s access to food with help from local restaurants.
