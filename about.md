<h1>About Shonan News</h1>

<p>Shonan News is an automated digest of local news and events for the Shonan area of Japan. A script fetches public RSS feeds from local news and event sources each day, and a large language model writes a short English summary of each item's title and excerpt.</p>

<p>"Shonan" has no official boundary, so this site doesn't try to define one. Coverage is whatever the feeds carry, and between them they cover twelve municipalities in Kanagawa Prefecture: Fujisawa, Kamakura, Chigasaki, Hiratsuka, Zushi, Hayama, Samukawa, Oiso, Ninomiya and Nakai, plus Yokosuka and Miura on the Miura Peninsula, which most people wouldn't call Shonan at all.</p>

<p>No article bodies are read or reproduced. Only the RSS feed's title and excerpt are sent to the model, and every summary links back to the original Japanese article.</p>

<p>Summaries are generated and published automatically, without human review before publishing. They may contain errors, especially in names, dates and numbers. Always check the original source for anything that matters.</p>
